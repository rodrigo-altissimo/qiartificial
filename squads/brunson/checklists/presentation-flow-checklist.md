# Presentation Flow and Timing Checklist

> Source: Expert Secrets, Chapters 10-14 - Perfect Webinar Delivery
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When rehearsing or reviewing presentation flow and pacing

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Perfect Webinar content written
- [ ] Slides designed and sequenced
- [ ] Stack Slide and close prepared
- [ ] Rehearsal time allocated (minimum 2 full run-throughs)

---

## STEP 1: Opening Segment (0-15 Minutes)

### 1.1 Hook and Promise (0-5 min)
- [ ] Pattern interrupt in first 30 seconds
- [ ] Big Promise stated clearly by minute 2
- [ ] Future pace: "Imagine if by the end of this..."
- [ ] Reason to stay until the end given
- [ ] Energy level HIGH from the very start

### 1.2 Credibility and Connection (5-10 min)
- [ ] Brief origin story (2-3 minutes, not life story)
- [ ] Key results shared with specifics
- [ ] Connected to audience: "I was just like you when..."
- [ ] Established authority without arrogance
- [ ] Audience thinking: "This person understands me"

### 1.3 Framework Setup (10-15 min)
- [ ] Three Secrets previewed: "I'm going to share 3 things..."
- [ ] Each secret teased with curiosity-building language
- [ ] Rules set: "Stay until the end, take notes, ask questions"
- [ ] Transitioned smoothly into Secret #1
- [ ] Audience engaged and committed to staying

---

## STEP 2: Content Delivery (15-60 Minutes)

### 2.1 Secret #1 (15-30 min)
- [ ] Introduced with hook: "The first thing I discovered was..."
- [ ] Epiphany Bridge story told with emotional arc
- [ ] Teaching framework presented clearly
- [ ] Key insight delivered (belief shift moment)
- [ ] Transition to Secret #2 smooth and natural
- [ ] Time check: staying within 15 minutes for this section

### 2.2 Secret #2 (30-45 min)
- [ ] Connected to Secret #1: "Now that you know [#1], you might think..."
- [ ] Different Epiphany Bridge story (different character)
- [ ] Internal belief addressed and reframed
- [ ] Teaching content empowers and simplifies
- [ ] Audience feels "I can actually do this"
- [ ] Time check: 15 minutes for this section

### 2.3 Secret #3 (45-60 min)
- [ ] Connected to previous secrets: "The last piece of the puzzle..."
- [ ] External belief addressed and reframed
- [ ] Creates urgency for action NOW
- [ ] Final teaching moment before transition
- [ ] Audience primed: vehicle works, I can do it, now is the time
- [ ] Time check: 15 minutes for this section

---

## STEP 3: Transition to Close (60-65 Minutes)

### 3.1 Bridge to Offer
- [ ] "Let me ask you a question..." moment
- [ ] Permission to share "something that can help"
- [ ] Recap: "We covered [Secret 1], [Secret 2], [Secret 3]..."
- [ ] Gap identified: "But implementation is where most people get stuck"
- [ ] Natural introduction: "That's why I created [Offer Name]..."
- [ ] Transition feels like service, not selling

---

## STEP 4: The Close (65-90 Minutes)

### 4.1 Offer Presentation (65-75 min)
- [ ] Each offer component introduced with enthusiasm
- [ ] Values stacked progressively (running total visible)
- [ ] Bonuses presented as "But wait, there's more..."
- [ ] Fast-action bonus creates urgency
- [ ] Total value contrasted against actual price

### 4.2 Risk Reversal (75-80 min)
- [ ] Guarantee presented boldly
- [ ] "If all this did was [ONE thing]" closes used (3 variations)
- [ ] "Who this is for" and "Who this is NOT for" segments
- [ ] Scarcity/urgency element reinforced
- [ ] Final emotional appeal

### 4.3 CTA and Q&A (80-90 min)
- [ ] Clear, repeated CTA: "Go to [URL] right now"
- [ ] Step-by-step instructions for purchasing
- [ ] Q&A answers common objections (pre-scripted)
- [ ] Each Q&A answer ends with CTA repetition
- [ ] Final closing statement and sign-off

---

## STEP 5: Delivery Quality

### 5.1 Energy and Pacing
- [ ] Voice energy varies (not monotone throughout)
- [ ] Strategic pauses after key points
- [ ] Speed increases during exciting moments
- [ ] Slows down for important teaching moments
- [ ] Standing (not sitting) during delivery for energy

### 5.2 Engagement Techniques
- [ ] Asks questions: "Type YES if you agree"
- [ ] Uses "imagine" and "picture this" language
- [ ] Calls out specific audience segments
- [ ] Uses humor appropriately (breaks tension)
- [ ] Maintains eye contact with camera (virtual)

### 5.3 Timing Discipline
- [ ] Total presentation: 60-90 minutes
- [ ] Content section: 40-50% of total time
- [ ] Close section: 25-35% of total time
- [ ] Practiced with timer to hit marks
- [ ] Buffer time for Q&A or organic moments

---

## Validation
- [ ] Full rehearsal completed at least twice
- [ ] Timing matches target for each section
- [ ] Emotional arc tested: curiosity -> belief -> desire -> action
- [ ] Transitions between sections feel natural
- [ ] Someone unfamiliar with the topic understood and was engaged

---

## Scoring
- 34-38/38 = Masterful presentation delivery
- 27-33/38 = Strong presentation, polish timing and transitions
- 20-26/38 = Needs more rehearsal and refinement
- Below 20 = Major restructuring or additional practice needed
