# Reverse Engineering Checklist

> Agent: brunson-swipe
> Source: DotCom Secrets - Funnel Hacking & Reverse Engineering
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When reverse-engineering a specific funnel element to replicate or improve

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Target funnel or element identified for reverse engineering
- [ ] Purchased through the funnel (or accessed all pages)
- [ ] All pages and emails screenshotted/saved
- [ ] Comparison template ready for your own version

---

## STEP 1: Deconstruct the Architecture

### 1.1 Funnel Map
- [ ] Complete funnel flow charted (entry --> exit)
- [ ] Every page URL documented
- [ ] Every decision point mapped (yes/no paths)
- [ ] Technology stack identified (ClickFunnels, WordPress, etc.)
- [ ] Page count and funnel depth noted

### 1.2 Traffic-to-Funnel Connection
- [ ] How traffic enters the funnel documented
- [ ] Pre-frame elements before the funnel identified
- [ ] Traffic source congruence with landing page rated
- [ ] Multiple entry points identified (if they exist)

---

## STEP 2: Deconstruct the Messaging

### 2.1 Headline Formulas
- [ ] Every headline in the funnel transcribed
- [ ] Headline patterns identified (question, bold claim, how-to, etc.)
- [ ] Most effective headline identified (based on placement/emphasis)
- [ ] Headline formulas extracted for your swipe file

### 2.2 Copy Structure
- [ ] Sales copy structure mapped (sections, order, length)
- [ ] Persuasion sequence identified (problem, agitation, solution, etc.)
- [ ] Key phrases and power words cataloged
- [ ] Word count and reading level noted per page

### 2.3 Story Identification
- [ ] Every story in the funnel documented
- [ ] Story types classified (Epiphany Bridge, proof, parable)
- [ ] Story placement in the funnel noted (where they appear)
- [ ] Story effectiveness assessed

---

## STEP 3: Deconstruct the Offer

### 3.1 Offer Breakdown
- [ ] Core offer components listed
- [ ] Every bonus documented with stated value
- [ ] Total value stack replicated
- [ ] Price point and payment options noted
- [ ] Guarantee type and terms documented

### 3.2 Pricing Psychology
- [ ] Price anchoring techniques identified
- [ ] Comparison points used (what is the price compared to?)
- [ ] Price justification language documented
- [ ] Upsell pricing strategy analyzed

---

## STEP 4: Deconstruct the Follow-Up

### 4.1 Email Sequence
- [ ] All emails in the sequence saved (minimum 14 days tracked)
- [ ] Subject line patterns documented
- [ ] Email type classified per email (Soap Opera, Seinfeld, pitch)
- [ ] CTA patterns and frequency noted
- [ ] Sequence timing documented (daily, every 2 days, etc.)

### 4.2 Retargeting & Re-engagement
- [ ] Retargeting ads identified after visiting the funnel
- [ ] Abandoned cart sequences documented
- [ ] Re-engagement strategies noted
- [ ] Multi-channel follow-up mapped (email + SMS + ads)

---

## STEP 5: Create Your Blueprint

### 5.1 Model, Don't Copy
- [ ] Identified what to MODEL (structure, flow, principles)
- [ ] Identified what to AVOID (specific copy, exact wording)
- [ ] Your unique angle and differentiation defined
- [ ] Blueprint adapted for your audience and offer

### 5.2 Implementation Plan
- [ ] Priority elements listed (build these first)
- [ ] Timeline for building your version set
- [ ] Resources needed identified
- [ ] Testing plan defined (what to test first)

---

## Validation
- [ ] Every page in the funnel fully deconstructed
- [ ] Email sequence tracked for minimum 14 days
- [ ] Offer structure fully documented and analyzed
- [ ] Your own blueprint created based on the reverse engineering
- [ ] Clear list of what to model vs what to make unique

---

## Scoring
- 28-32/32 = Complete reverse engineering, blueprint ready
- 22-27/32 = Thorough deconstruction, minor gaps
- 16-21/32 = Key elements deconstructed but incomplete
- Below 16 = Surface-level analysis, go deeper
