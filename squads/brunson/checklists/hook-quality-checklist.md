# Hook Quality and Variety Checklist

> Source: Traffic Secrets, Chapters 12-13 - Hook, Story, Offer
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When creating and evaluating hooks for content and ads

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Target audience pain points and desires documented
- [ ] Competitive hooks analyzed (what's working in your market)
- [ ] Content format defined (video, text, image, audio)
- [ ] Offer or CTA the hook should lead to

---

## STEP 1: Hook Types Coverage

### 1.1 Question Hooks
- [ ] Pain-based question: "Tired of [specific pain]?"
- [ ] Curiosity question: "Did you know that [surprising fact]?"
- [ ] Challenge question: "Can you [do this thing] in [timeframe]?"
- [ ] Qualifying question: "Are you a [identity] who wants [result]?"
- [ ] At least 3 question-style hooks created

### 1.2 Statement Hooks
- [ ] Bold claim: "[Specific result] in [specific timeframe]"
- [ ] Contrarian statement: "Everything you know about [topic] is wrong"
- [ ] Story teaser: "I lost everything... and it was the best thing that happened"
- [ ] Secret reveal: "The [industry] doesn't want you to know this..."
- [ ] At least 3 statement-style hooks created

### 1.3 Visual/Pattern Interrupt Hooks
- [ ] Unexpected visual that stops the scroll
- [ ] Before/after comparison image
- [ ] Text overlay on compelling image
- [ ] Motion or animation element (for video)
- [ ] At least 2 visual-first hooks created

---

## STEP 2: Hook Quality Standards

### 2.1 Attention Capture
- [ ] Hook grabs attention in under 3 seconds (text) or 1 second (video)
- [ ] Hook is specific to the target audience (not generic)
- [ ] Hook creates an emotional response (curiosity, shock, desire)
- [ ] Hook stands out from surrounding content (thumb-stopping)
- [ ] Hook works without context (standalone power)

### 2.2 Relevance
- [ ] Hook connects to a REAL pain point or desire
- [ ] Language matches what the audience actually says
- [ ] Hook is timely and current (not outdated references)
- [ ] Hook resonates with the specific segment targeted
- [ ] Hook does NOT attract wrong audience

### 2.3 Transition to Story
- [ ] Hook naturally flows into the story/content
- [ ] Promise made in hook is delivered in the content
- [ ] No bait-and-switch (hook and content are congruent)
- [ ] Curiosity opened in hook is resolved in story
- [ ] Hook sets the frame for the entire piece

---

## STEP 3: Hook Variety

### 3.1 Angle Diversity
- [ ] At least one hook per emotion: curiosity, fear, desire, anger, humor
- [ ] Different entry points: problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware
- [ ] Hooks for different segments within your audience
- [ ] Seasonal or timely hooks alongside evergreen ones
- [ ] Hooks from different perspectives (yours, student's, industry)

### 3.2 Format Diversity
- [ ] Text-only hooks for articles and posts
- [ ] Video hooks for social media and ads
- [ ] Audio hooks for podcasts and audio content
- [ ] Image hooks for social media and display ads
- [ ] Email subject line hooks for email marketing

---

## STEP 4: Hook Testing Framework

### 4.1 Testing Protocol
- [ ] 5+ hooks tested per campaign before scaling
- [ ] Each hook tested with minimum $5-$10 or 500 impressions
- [ ] Metrics: CTR, engagement rate, view duration (video)
- [ ] Winners defined: top 20% by performance
- [ ] Losers killed quickly (don't waste budget on underperformers)

### 4.2 Iteration
- [ ] Winning hooks analyzed: what makes them work?
- [ ] New variations created based on winning patterns
- [ ] Hooks refreshed every 2-4 weeks (fatigue prevention)
- [ ] Top-performing hooks archived in swipe file
- [ ] Patterns documented for future hook creation

---

## STEP 5: Hook Integration

### 5.1 Content Integration
- [ ] Every piece of content starts with a tested hook
- [ ] Hook-Story-Offer framework applied consistently
- [ ] Hooks aligned with the Epiphany Bridge story that follows
- [ ] Hooks matched to traffic temperature (cold needs stronger hooks)
- [ ] Hook strength proportional to the ask (bigger offer = stronger hook)

---

## Validation
- [ ] Minimum 10 hooks created across multiple types and formats
- [ ] At least 5 hooks tested with real audience
- [ ] Winning hooks identified and documented
- [ ] Hook creation is a repeatable system (not ad-hoc)
- [ ] Hook performance reviewed weekly

---

## Scoring
- 26-30/30 = Professional-grade hook system
- 20-25/30 = Good variety, improve testing discipline
- 15-19/30 = Hooks too narrow or untested
- Below 15 = Hook creation fundamentals needed
