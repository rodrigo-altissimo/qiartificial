# Multi-Funnel Stacking Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets - Funnel Stacking + Traffic Secrets Ch. 16
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building or auditing a multi-funnel ecosystem

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] At least one frontend funnel live and converting
- [ ] Value Ladder fully defined with multiple tiers
- [ ] Email list has minimum 1,000 subscribers
- [ ] Backend offer(s) ready for deployment

---

## STEP 1: Frontend Funnel Stack

### 1.1 Lead Generation Layer
- [ ] Primary squeeze funnel active and converting
- [ ] At least 2 lead magnets targeting different segments
- [ ] Each lead magnet feeds into segmented email sequences
- [ ] Quiz or survey funnel for smart segmentation
- [ ] Free + shipping offer funnel converting buyers

### 1.2 Self-Liquidating Layer
- [ ] SLO funnel covering ad spend
- [ ] Tripwire offer converting leads into buyers
- [ ] Book funnel or mini-course funnel as entry point
- [ ] Each frontend funnel has unique OTO sequence
- [ ] Frontend funnels not cannibalizing each other

---

## STEP 2: Middle Funnel Stack

### 2.1 Presentation Layer
- [ ] Perfect Webinar funnel for core offer
- [ ] Webinar available in both live and automated versions
- [ ] Product launch funnel for seasonal promotions
- [ ] Challenge funnel for engagement-based selling
- [ ] Each presentation funnel targets different awareness level

### 2.2 Ascension Paths
- [ ] Frontend buyers automatically invited to webinar
- [ ] Webinar non-buyers enter nurture sequence
- [ ] Challenge completers receive special backend offer
- [ ] Cross-funnel promotion calendar established

---

## STEP 3: Backend Funnel Stack

### 3.1 High-Ticket Layer
- [ ] Application funnel for premium offer
- [ ] Phone/call funnel for highest tier
- [ ] Qualification criteria defined and automated
- [ ] Sales team or closer trained on offer
- [ ] Backend funnel only receives warm/qualified traffic

### 3.2 Continuity Layer
- [ ] Membership or subscription funnel active
- [ ] Retention funnel in place (win-back sequences)
- [ ] Annual vs. monthly pricing options tested
- [ ] Continuity offer woven into all funnel exits

---

## STEP 4: Inter-Funnel Connections

### 4.1 Traffic Flow Between Funnels
- [ ] Thank-you pages promote next funnel in stack
- [ ] Email sequences bridge between funnels
- [ ] Retargeting audiences segmented by funnel stage
- [ ] Buyers from Funnel A see ads for Funnel B
- [ ] Non-buyers from Funnel A enter re-engagement sequence

### 4.2 Timing and Sequencing
- [ ] Minimum 3-day gap between major offers
- [ ] Follow-up funnel activates 24 hours after purchase
- [ ] Ascension offers presented at peak engagement moments
- [ ] Seasonal/launch funnels scheduled on content calendar
- [ ] No more than 2 active promotions simultaneously

---

## STEP 5: Stack Economics

### 5.1 Financial Validation
- [ ] Each funnel independently profitable or break-even
- [ ] Combined LTV tracked across all funnels
- [ ] Attribution model accounts for multi-funnel journeys
- [ ] Stack increases average customer value by 3x+ over single funnel
- [ ] Budget allocated proportionally to funnel performance

---

## Validation
- [ ] Funnel map drawn showing all connections visually
- [ ] Every subscriber has a next-step funnel to enter
- [ ] No dead-ends in the customer journey
- [ ] Stack tested end-to-end with test customer
- [ ] Revenue per customer tracked across full stack

---

## Scoring
- 28-32/32 = World-class funnel ecosystem
- 22-27/32 = Strong stack, optimize connections
- 16-21/32 = Stack has gaps, priority fixes needed
- Below 16 = Rebuild stack strategy from Value Ladder
