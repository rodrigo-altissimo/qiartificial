# Business Model Health Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets - Business Fundamentals + Expert Secrets
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When evaluating the overall health of a funnel-based business model

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Business has been operating for at least 90 days
- [ ] Revenue data available
- [ ] Customer data accessible
- [ ] Basic understanding of business economics

---

## STEP 1: Revenue Model

### 1.1 Revenue Streams
- [ ] Frontend revenue tracked (first purchase)
- [ ] Backend revenue tracked (upsells, cross-sells)
- [ ] Recurring revenue tracked (subscriptions, memberships)
- [ ] Affiliate/partnership revenue tracked (if applicable)
- [ ] At least 2 revenue streams active (not dependent on one)

### 1.2 Revenue Distribution
- [ ] Frontend covers ad spend (self-liquidating or close)
- [ ] Backend generates the majority of profit
- [ ] Recurring revenue growing month over month
- [ ] Revenue per customer increasing over time
- [ ] No single product accounts for more than 60% of total revenue

---

## STEP 2: Value Ladder Health

### 2.1 Ladder Completeness
- [ ] Entry-level offer active (free to $7)
- [ ] Frontend offer active ($7-$97)
- [ ] Core offer active ($97-$997)
- [ ] Premium offer active ($997+)
- [ ] Continuity offer active (recurring)
- [ ] Each rung converting at acceptable rates

### 2.2 Ascension Rate
- [ ] Percentage of leads who become buyers tracked
- [ ] Percentage of buyers who ascend to next tier tracked
- [ ] Ascension rate above 10% per tier
- [ ] Average time from lead to highest-tier purchase calculated
- [ ] Ascension bottlenecks identified and addressed

---

## STEP 3: Customer Economics

### 3.1 Unit Economics
- [ ] Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) known
- [ ] Average Order Value (AOV) calculated
- [ ] Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) calculated (or projected)
- [ ] LTV:CAC ratio above 3:1 (healthy: above 5:1)
- [ ] Payback period acceptable (under 90 days ideally)

### 3.2 Customer Health
- [ ] Customer satisfaction measured (NPS, surveys, reviews)
- [ ] Refund rate below 10%
- [ ] Churn rate for recurring products below 10% monthly
- [ ] Customer support volume manageable
- [ ] Repeat purchase rate tracked and growing

---

## STEP 4: Funnel Ecosystem

### 4.1 Funnel Portfolio
- [ ] Multiple funnels active (not just one)
- [ ] Funnels serve different points on the Value Ladder
- [ ] Funnels connected through follow-up sequences
- [ ] New funnels planned and in development pipeline
- [ ] Each funnel has dedicated traffic strategy

### 4.2 Traffic Resilience
- [ ] Traffic comes from 3+ sources
- [ ] No single traffic source accounts for more than 50%
- [ ] Owned traffic (email list) generates revenue independently
- [ ] Organic/earned traffic growing alongside paid
- [ ] Traffic strategy adaptable to platform changes

---

## STEP 5: Operational Health

### 5.1 Team and Systems
- [ ] Key roles filled (or outsourced): marketing, support, fulfillment
- [ ] SOPs documented for recurring tasks
- [ ] Automation handles routine operations
- [ ] Bottleneck: founder is not the single point of failure
- [ ] Business can operate for 2 weeks without founder

### 5.2 Financial Health
- [ ] Monthly profit margin above 30%
- [ ] Cash reserves cover 3+ months of operating expenses
- [ ] Revenue trend: growing or stable (not declining)
- [ ] Ad spend scaled responsibly (not outpacing profit)
- [ ] Financial reporting reviewed monthly

---

## STEP 6: Growth Readiness

### 6.1 Scaling Assessment
- [ ] Current funnels can handle 2-5x more traffic
- [ ] Fulfillment can scale with increased sales
- [ ] Customer support can handle increased volume
- [ ] Payment processing can handle increased transactions
- [ ] Team capacity exists for growth projects

---

## Validation
- [ ] All business model components assessed
- [ ] Key metrics documented and benchmarked
- [ ] Top 3 growth opportunities identified
- [ ] Top 3 risks or weaknesses identified
- [ ] 90-day improvement plan created

---

## Scoring
- 32-36/36 = Healthy, scalable business model
- 26-31/36 = Good model, address weak areas before scaling
- 20-25/36 = Business model has significant gaps
- Below 20 = Fundamental business model restructuring needed
