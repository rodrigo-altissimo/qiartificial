# Traffic Strategy Completeness Checklist

> Source: Traffic Secrets - Full Framework
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building or auditing a comprehensive traffic strategy

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Secret Formula complete (WHO/WHERE/BAIT/RESULT)
- [ ] Dream 100 list built and organized
- [ ] At least one funnel live and converting
- [ ] Budget allocated for traffic generation

---

## STEP 1: Traffic Foundations

### 1.1 Traffic You Control
- [ ] Paid ads active on at least 1 platform
- [ ] Facebook/Meta ads running (if relevant to audience)
- [ ] Google/YouTube ads running (if relevant to audience)
- [ ] Retargeting campaigns active for all funnel visitors
- [ ] Budget tracked with daily/weekly spending limits
- [ ] ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) monitored daily

### 1.2 Traffic You Earn
- [ ] Content publishing schedule active (minimum 3x/week)
- [ ] SEO strategy defined for long-term organic traffic
- [ ] Guest appearances on podcasts/shows (minimum 2/month)
- [ ] PR or media coverage efforts underway
- [ ] Affiliate/JV partnerships generating referral traffic

### 1.3 Traffic You Own
- [ ] Email list being built actively (daily new subscribers)
- [ ] Email list segmented by source and interest
- [ ] SMS list building (if appropriate for market)
- [ ] Messenger/chatbot list growing
- [ ] Push notification subscribers building

---

## STEP 2: Platform Strategy

### 2.1 Primary Platform (Master This First)
- [ ] ONE platform chosen as primary focus
- [ ] Content format mastered for that platform (video, text, audio)
- [ ] Publishing consistently at optimal frequency
- [ ] Engagement rate tracked and improving
- [ ] Audience growing week over week

### 2.2 Distribution Strategy
- [ ] Primary content repurposed for secondary platforms
- [ ] Each platform has a specific role in the funnel
- [ ] Cross-platform promotion strategy defined
- [ ] Each platform drives traffic to owned media (email list)
- [ ] Platform-specific best practices followed

---

## STEP 3: Traffic Temperature Management

### 3.1 Cold Traffic Strategy
- [ ] Hook-focused ads for attention capture
- [ ] Bridge page or content to warm up before funnel
- [ ] Longer nurture sequence for cold leads
- [ ] Lower price point offers for cold traffic conversion
- [ ] Cost per lead tracked and optimized

### 3.2 Warm Traffic Strategy
- [ ] Retargeting ads for website visitors and engagers
- [ ] Content-to-offer pipeline defined
- [ ] Email sequences for list that hasn't bought
- [ ] Webinar or challenge invitations for warm leads
- [ ] Conversion rate higher than cold traffic (verified)

### 3.3 Hot Traffic Strategy
- [ ] Customer email promotions for backend offers
- [ ] Exclusive offers for existing buyers
- [ ] Upsell/cross-sell campaigns active
- [ ] Referral program for existing customers
- [ ] Highest conversion rate tier (verified)

---

## STEP 4: Content Engine

### 4.1 Hook, Story, Offer Framework
- [ ] Every piece of content follows Hook-Story-Offer
- [ ] Hooks tested and rotated (minimum 5 variations)
- [ ] Stories connect to Epiphany Bridge format
- [ ] Offers are clear CTAs (even in free content)
- [ ] Content drives to one specific funnel entry point

### 4.2 Content Calendar
- [ ] Monthly content themes planned
- [ ] Weekly publishing schedule documented
- [ ] Content batched for efficiency
- [ ] Evergreen content repurposed cyclically
- [ ] Performance metrics reviewed weekly

---

## STEP 5: Scaling Framework

### 5.1 Scaling Readiness
- [ ] Profitable traffic channel identified (ROAS > 2:1)
- [ ] Scaling budget approved and available
- [ ] Creative refresh cycle established (new ads every 2-4 weeks)
- [ ] Landing pages A/B tested before scaling
- [ ] Support/fulfillment can handle increased volume

---

## Validation
- [ ] All 3 traffic types represented (controlled, earned, owned)
- [ ] Multiple traffic sources generating leads (not dependent on 1)
- [ ] Cost per acquisition within profitable range
- [ ] Traffic strategy documented and repeatable
- [ ] Monthly traffic report reviewed with key metrics

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Comprehensive, diversified traffic strategy
- 24-29/34 = Strong strategy, fill gaps in underserved areas
- 18-23/34 = Traffic strategy too narrow, diversify sources
- Below 18 = Fundamental traffic strategy needed before scaling
