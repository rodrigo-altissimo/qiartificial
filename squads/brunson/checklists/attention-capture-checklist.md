# Attention Capture Metrics Checklist

> Source: Traffic Secrets - Attention and Hook Optimization
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When measuring and optimizing attention capture across all content

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Content published on at least one platform
- [ ] Analytics tracking configured
- [ ] Baseline metrics established (minimum 7 days of data)
- [ ] Understanding of platform-specific attention metrics

---

## STEP 1: Content Attention Metrics

### 1.1 Video Content
- [ ] Average view duration tracked per video
- [ ] 3-second view rate above 65% (hook is working)
- [ ] ThruPlay rate (15+ seconds) above 30%
- [ ] 50% completion rate above 15%
- [ ] 100% completion rate tracked for overall content quality
- [ ] Drop-off points identified on audience retention graph

### 1.2 Written Content
- [ ] Time on page tracked (target: 2+ minutes for articles)
- [ ] Scroll depth measured (target: 50%+ reach bottom)
- [ ] Bounce rate below 70% for landing pages
- [ ] Click-through rate on in-content CTAs
- [ ] Page exit points identified

### 1.3 Social Media Content
- [ ] Engagement rate per post (likes, comments, shares)
- [ ] Save rate tracked (indicates high-value content)
- [ ] Share rate tracked (indicates viral potential)
- [ ] Profile visits from content measured
- [ ] Follower growth correlated with content performance

---

## STEP 2: Ad Attention Metrics

### 2.1 Paid Ad Performance
- [ ] Click-through rate (CTR) above 1% for cold traffic
- [ ] CTR above 2% for warm/retargeting traffic
- [ ] Cost per click (CPC) within profitable range
- [ ] Frequency below 3 (before ad fatigue sets in)
- [ ] Relevance/quality score above 7/10

### 2.2 Hook Effectiveness
- [ ] Video hook rate: 3-second views / impressions > 50%
- [ ] Thumb-stop ratio improving over time
- [ ] Best hooks identified across all campaigns
- [ ] Hook patterns documented (what themes/angles win)
- [ ] Winning hooks replicated in new variations

---

## STEP 3: Email Attention Metrics

### 3.1 Subject Line Performance
- [ ] Open rate above 20% for warm list
- [ ] Open rate above 15% for general list
- [ ] Subject line A/B tests running on every major send
- [ ] Best subject line patterns documented
- [ ] Preview text optimized (not just subject line)

### 3.2 Email Engagement
- [ ] Click rate above 2% per email
- [ ] Click-to-open rate above 10%
- [ ] Unsubscribe rate below 0.5% per email
- [ ] Reply rate tracked (engagement indicator)
- [ ] Link click patterns analyzed (what topics get clicks)

---

## STEP 4: Funnel Attention Metrics

### 4.1 Landing Page Attention
- [ ] Above-the-fold engagement measured (time, scrolls, clicks)
- [ ] Headline capturing attention (low immediate bounce rate)
- [ ] Video play rate above 30% on pages with video
- [ ] Form start rate above 50% of page visitors
- [ ] CTA button click rate tracked

### 4.2 Webinar Attention
- [ ] Show-up rate above 25% of registrants
- [ ] Attendees staying past 30 minutes: above 50%
- [ ] Attendees staying to the close: above 30%
- [ ] Chat engagement during webinar tracked
- [ ] Drop-off points identified in webinar analytics

---

## STEP 5: Optimization Actions

### 5.1 Attention Gap Analysis
- [ ] Weakest attention metric identified
- [ ] Root cause hypothesized (weak hook? wrong audience? slow load?)
- [ ] 3 test variations created to address the gap
- [ ] Test timeline: 5-7 days with sufficient traffic
- [ ] Winner implemented, next weakest point targeted

### 5.2 Continuous Improvement
- [ ] Weekly review of all attention metrics
- [ ] Monthly comparison of trends (improving or declining)
- [ ] Content audit: what captures most attention? Replicate it.
- [ ] Platform algorithm changes monitored and adapted to
- [ ] Team or system for consistent attention metric monitoring

---

## Validation
- [ ] All key attention metrics being tracked across channels
- [ ] Dashboard or report consolidating attention data
- [ ] Weekly optimization actions taken based on data
- [ ] Attention metrics trending upward month over month
- [ ] Attention capture strategy documented and repeatable

---

## Scoring
- 28-32/32 = Data-driven attention optimization system
- 22-27/32 = Good tracking, improve optimization frequency
- 16-21/32 = Gaps in tracking or optimization
- Below 16 = Attention metrics not being tracked, critical fix needed
