# Scarcity and Urgency Genuineness Checklist

> Source: Expert Secrets + DotCom Secrets - Ethical Urgency
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When implementing scarcity or urgency elements in offers and funnels

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Offer defined and ready for market
- [ ] Understanding of the difference between genuine and manufactured urgency
- [ ] Commitment to ethical marketing practices
- [ ] Funnel technology supports deadline/scarcity features

---

## STEP 1: Scarcity Types

### 1.1 Quantity Scarcity
- [ ] Limited spots/seats genuinely limited (reason exists)
- [ ] Number is specific (not vague "limited availability")
- [ ] Reason for limit is logical (coaching capacity, venue size, etc.)
- [ ] Counter or tracker shows remaining spots
- [ ] When sold out, offer genuinely closes

### 1.2 Time Scarcity
- [ ] Deadline is real and enforced
- [ ] Reason for deadline exists (launch period, cohort start, event date)
- [ ] Countdown timer reflects actual deadline
- [ ] Timer does NOT reset when page is revisited (evergreen exception below)
- [ ] After deadline, offer changes or disappears

### 1.3 Bonus Scarcity
- [ ] Fast-action bonuses expire at a real deadline
- [ ] Bonuses are genuinely removed after deadline
- [ ] Bonus availability matches stated limits
- [ ] Different bonuses for different phases (early bird, last chance)
- [ ] Post-deadline buyers do NOT receive expired bonuses

---

## STEP 2: Urgency Types

### 2.1 Natural Urgency
- [ ] Cohort or group start date (can't join after it starts)
- [ ] Live event date (happens once at a specific time)
- [ ] Seasonal relevance (back to school, New Year, etc.)
- [ ] Market timing (algorithm change, regulation, trend)
- [ ] Urgency connected to real external factor

### 2.2 Created Urgency
- [ ] Price increase after specific date (and it ACTUALLY increases)
- [ ] Bonus removal after deadline (and bonuses ARE removed)
- [ ] Cart close with genuine reopen schedule
- [ ] Early-bird pricing that transitions to regular pricing
- [ ] All created urgency is ENFORCED (not bluffed)

### 2.3 Evergreen Urgency (Special Rules)
- [ ] If using evergreen deadlines, each person gets their OWN deadline
- [ ] Deadline starts from THEIR opt-in/registration date
- [ ] Technology enforces individual deadlines (Deadline Funnel, etc.)
- [ ] Deadline is consistent (if 72 hours, always 72 hours)
- [ ] Offer genuinely changes after their personal deadline

---

## STEP 3: Genuineness Validation

### 3.1 Honesty Check
- [ ] Every scarcity claim is truthful
- [ ] Every deadline is real and enforced
- [ ] No fake countdown timers that reset
- [ ] No "only 3 spots left" that never changes
- [ ] No "closing tonight" that reopens tomorrow

### 3.2 Ethical Standards
- [ ] You would be comfortable if a journalist investigated your claims
- [ ] Customers who purchased under urgency don't feel deceived later
- [ ] Scarcity adds genuine value (not just pressure)
- [ ] Business model doesn't depend on deception to sell
- [ ] Repeat customers trust your urgency claims

### 3.3 Legal Compliance
- [ ] FTC guidelines on urgency claims followed
- [ ] No "bait and switch" with pricing or availability
- [ ] Documentation exists proving scarcity was real
- [ ] Ad platform policies on urgency/scarcity followed
- [ ] Disclaimer language included where appropriate

---

## STEP 4: Implementation

### 4.1 Technical Setup
- [ ] Countdown timer configured and tested
- [ ] Timer syncs correctly across devices and browsers
- [ ] Cart/checkout closed after deadline (automated)
- [ ] Inventory counter updates in real-time (if quantity limited)
- [ ] Redirect page ready for post-deadline visitors

### 4.2 Communication
- [ ] Scarcity/urgency communicated across all channels (email, ads, page)
- [ ] Reason WHY there is scarcity/urgency explained
- [ ] Reminders sent as deadline approaches (24hr, 6hr, 1hr)
- [ ] Final email at the actual deadline moment
- [ ] Post-deadline "closed" communication sent

---

## STEP 5: Performance Measurement

### 5.1 Impact Assessment
- [ ] Conversion rate compared: with urgency vs. without
- [ ] Majority of sales come in final 24-48 hours (normal pattern)
- [ ] Customer satisfaction not negatively impacted by urgency
- [ ] Refund rate not elevated due to pressure-buying
- [ ] Urgency strategy contributing to overall revenue goals

---

## Validation
- [ ] All scarcity/urgency claims are genuine and enforceable
- [ ] Technology enforces deadlines automatically
- [ ] Communication plan covers the full urgency timeline
- [ ] Ethical standards met (you'd proudly explain your approach)
- [ ] Performance data shows urgency increases conversions ethically

---

## Scoring
- 28-32/32 = Ethical, effective urgency strategy
- 22-27/32 = Good strategy, verify enforcement and genuineness
- 16-21/32 = Gaps in enforcement or honesty
- Below 16 = Urgency strategy needs ethical redesign
