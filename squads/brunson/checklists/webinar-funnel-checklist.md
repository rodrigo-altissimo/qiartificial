# Webinar Funnel Setup Checklist

> Source: Expert Secrets + DotCom Secrets - Webinar Funnel Architecture
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When setting up the complete webinar funnel (pages, emails, tech)

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Perfect Webinar presentation complete
- [ ] Offer and Stack Slide ready
- [ ] Webinar platform chosen (live or automated)
- [ ] Email service provider connected
- [ ] Payment processor configured

---

## STEP 1: Registration Funnel

### 1.1 Registration Page
- [ ] Compelling headline: "Free Training: [Big Promise]"
- [ ] Date/time clearly displayed (live) or "Watch Now" (automated)
- [ ] 3-5 bullet points of what they'll learn
- [ ] Social proof element (attendee count, testimonials)
- [ ] Simple form: name + email (no more fields)
- [ ] No exit navigation or distracting links
- [ ] Urgency element (limited spots, starting soon)

### 1.2 Thank You / Confirmation Page
- [ ] Confirms registration successfully
- [ ] Calendar invite download or add-to-calendar button
- [ ] Indoctrination video (builds anticipation and pre-frames)
- [ ] Share buttons to invite others
- [ ] "While you wait" resource or micro-offer

### 1.3 Reminder System
- [ ] Email confirmation immediately after registration
- [ ] Reminder email 24 hours before webinar
- [ ] Reminder email 1 hour before webinar
- [ ] Reminder email 15 minutes before (with link)
- [ ] SMS reminders if number collected

---

## STEP 2: Webinar Delivery

### 2.1 Live Webinar Setup
- [ ] Webinar platform tested with audio and video
- [ ] Screen sharing works correctly
- [ ] Chat/Q&A feature enabled
- [ ] Recording enabled for replay
- [ ] Backup plan if tech fails (phone call-in, backup platform)

### 2.2 Automated Webinar Setup
- [ ] Pre-recorded webinar uploaded to platform
- [ ] Simulated live experience configured (chat, timing)
- [ ] Multiple showing times available
- [ ] Registration to viewing flow seamless
- [ ] Replay access configured (limited time)

### 2.3 During Webinar
- [ ] CTA button appears at the right time (during the close)
- [ ] Link to order page visible in chat/description
- [ ] Engagement prompts throughout (type "yes" in chat)
- [ ] Attendee count visible (social proof)
- [ ] Q&A addressed live or via pre-written responses

---

## STEP 3: Post-Webinar Follow-Up

### 3.1 Replay Sequence
- [ ] Replay email sent within 1 hour of webinar ending
- [ ] Replay available for 24-72 hours (not indefinitely)
- [ ] Replay page includes offer and CTA below video
- [ ] Reminder email: "Replay expiring in 24 hours"
- [ ] Final email: "Last chance - replay removed tonight"

### 3.2 Non-Attendee Sequence
- [ ] Email to no-shows within 2 hours: "You missed it!"
- [ ] Replay access provided with urgency
- [ ] Different subject lines than attendee sequence
- [ ] Highlight what they missed and results others got
- [ ] Final deadline same as attendee replay

### 3.3 Close Sequence (All Registrants)
- [ ] Day 1: Replay + key takeaway email
- [ ] Day 2: Testimonial/case study email
- [ ] Day 3: Objection-handling email (FAQ format)
- [ ] Day 4: Urgency email (bonuses expiring)
- [ ] Day 5: Final deadline email (cart closing)

---

## STEP 4: Order Process

### 4.1 Order Page
- [ ] Sales page or direct order form linked from webinar
- [ ] Stack Slide elements replicated on order page
- [ ] Guarantee displayed prominently
- [ ] Payment options: full pay + payment plan
- [ ] Order bump present on checkout

### 4.2 Post-Purchase
- [ ] OTO 1 presented immediately after purchase
- [ ] OTO 2 follows (if applicable)
- [ ] Thank you page with access instructions
- [ ] Welcome email with login/download details
- [ ] Buyer added to customer list (removed from prospect)

---

## STEP 5: Metrics and Optimization

### 5.1 Key Metrics to Track
- [ ] Registration rate (target: 20-40% of page visitors)
- [ ] Show-up rate (target: 25-40% of registrants)
- [ ] Stick rate (target: 50%+ stay to the close)
- [ ] Conversion rate (target: 5-15% of attendees)
- [ ] Revenue per registrant calculated

---

## Validation
- [ ] Full funnel tested end-to-end (registration to purchase)
- [ ] All emails tested for delivery and link accuracy
- [ ] Webinar watched start to finish for quality
- [ ] Mobile experience tested on registration and replay
- [ ] Analytics tracking confirmed on all pages

---

## Scoring
- 32-36/36 = Complete, professional webinar funnel
- 26-31/36 = Solid funnel, fix gaps before scaling traffic
- 20-25/36 = Missing key components, address before launch
- Below 20 = Major rebuild needed
