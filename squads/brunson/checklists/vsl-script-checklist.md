# VSL Script Checklist

> Agent: brunson-copy
> Source: DotCom Secrets - Video Sales Letter Framework
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When writing or auditing a Video Sales Letter script

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Target avatar defined with specific pain points
- [ ] Offer and price point finalized
- [ ] Epiphany Bridge story prepared
- [ ] Three false beliefs identified (vehicle, internal, external)
- [ ] Value stack components listed with dollar values

---

## STEP 1: The Hook (First 15 Seconds)

### 1.1 Pattern Interrupt
- [ ] Opens with a bold statement, question, or shocking fact
- [ ] Breaks the viewer out of their scroll/browsing pattern
- [ ] Is relevant to the target audience (not clickbait)

### 1.2 Big Promise
- [ ] States the core outcome the viewer will get
- [ ] Includes specificity (timeframe, amount, method)
- [ ] Triggers enough curiosity to keep watching

### 1.3 Qualification
- [ ] Tells the viewer who this is for
- [ ] Tells the viewer who this is NOT for
- [ ] Creates exclusivity and self-selection

---

## STEP 2: The Story (Build Connection)

### 2.1 Backstory
- [ ] Establishes relatable starting point
- [ ] Shows you were once where the viewer is now
- [ ] Builds empathy and trust

### 2.2 The Struggle
- [ ] Describes failed attempts and frustrations
- [ ] Mirrors the viewer's own experience
- [ ] Creates emotional engagement

### 2.3 The Epiphany
- [ ] Clear moment of discovery or realization
- [ ] Feels authentic and specific (not generic)
- [ ] Naturally leads to the solution/offer

### 2.4 The Result
- [ ] Shows transformation after the epiphany
- [ ] Includes concrete proof (numbers, screenshots, testimonials)
- [ ] Makes the viewer believe "this could work for me too"

---

## STEP 3: The Content (Destroy False Beliefs)

### 3.1 Secret #1 (Vehicle)
- [ ] Reframes what the viewer thinks they need
- [ ] Introduces the new opportunity/vehicle
- [ ] Supported by a mini Epiphany Bridge story

### 3.2 Secret #2 (Internal)
- [ ] Addresses "I can't do this" belief
- [ ] Shows why the viewer IS capable
- [ ] Supported by proof or testimony

### 3.3 Secret #3 (External)
- [ ] Addresses "it won't work for me because..." belief
- [ ] Removes the external excuse
- [ ] Supported by case study or logic

---

## STEP 4: The Transition & Stack

### 4.1 Transition to Offer
- [ ] Bridges from teaching to selling naturally
- [ ] Uses "So here's what I did..." or "The question is..."
- [ ] Viewer feels they need MORE (not complete)

### 4.2 The Stack
- [ ] Each offer component presented individually
- [ ] Each component has an assigned dollar value
- [ ] Total value stacked before price reveal
- [ ] Price positioned as a fraction of total value

### 4.3 Price Reveal
- [ ] Anchored against the total value stack
- [ ] Compared to alternatives (coaching, DIY, competitors)
- [ ] Feels like an obvious bargain

---

## STEP 5: The Close

### 5.1 Guarantee
- [ ] Risk reversal clearly stated
- [ ] Guarantee is specific and bold
- [ ] Makes the decision feel risk-free

### 5.2 Urgency & Scarcity
- [ ] Real reason for urgency (not fake scarcity)
- [ ] Deadline or limited quantity stated
- [ ] Consequence of not acting made clear

### 5.3 Final CTA
- [ ] Tells viewer exactly what to click/do
- [ ] Restates the core benefit one final time
- [ ] Button/link instruction is crystal clear

---

## Validation
- [ ] Script length matches price point (higher price = longer VSL)
- [ ] Script reads naturally when spoken aloud
- [ ] Emotional arc flows: curiosity --> empathy --> belief --> desire --> action
- [ ] No section drags or feels repetitive
- [ ] Testimonials/proof inserted at key skepticism points

---

## Scoring
- 32-36/36 = Record-ready VSL script
- 25-31/36 = Strong script, refine weak sections
- 18-24/36 = Needs significant revision
- Below 18 = Rewrite from framework scratch
