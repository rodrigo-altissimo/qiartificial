# Big Domino Identification Checklist

> Source: Expert Secrets, Chapter 6 - The Big Domino
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When identifying the ONE belief that makes all other objections irrelevant

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] New Opportunity defined and validated
- [ ] Dream customer's false beliefs cataloged
- [ ] Offer and mechanism clearly articulated
- [ ] Understanding of customer's current beliefs about the topic

---

## STEP 1: Big Domino Discovery

### 1.1 Core Belief Identification
- [ ] Identified the ONE thing they must believe for everything else to become irrelevant
- [ ] This belief is about your NEW OPPORTUNITY (not just your product)
- [ ] If they believe THIS one thing, they MUST buy
- [ ] If they don't believe this, nothing else matters
- [ ] Big Domino stated as a single, clear sentence

### 1.2 Big Domino Statement Formula
- [ ] Follows format: "If I can make them believe that [NEW OPPORTUNITY] is the key to [DESIRED RESULT] and is only achievable through [MY SPECIFIC VEHICLE], then all other objections become irrelevant"
- [ ] Statement is specific, not vague
- [ ] Statement is believable (not hyperbolic)
- [ ] Statement addresses the core desire
- [ ] Statement positions your vehicle as the ONLY path

---

## STEP 2: Domino Chain Mapping

### 2.1 Supporting Beliefs
- [ ] Listed 5-10 smaller beliefs that support the Big Domino
- [ ] Each supporting belief, when knocked down, moves toward the Big Domino
- [ ] Supporting beliefs are sequenced logically
- [ ] No critical supporting belief is missing
- [ ] Each belief can be addressed with a story or proof point

### 2.2 False Belief Connections
- [ ] Connected each false belief to the Big Domino chain
- [ ] Identified which false beliefs PREVENT Big Domino acceptance
- [ ] Prioritized false beliefs by blocking power
- [ ] Each false belief has a counter-narrative (Epiphany Bridge story)
- [ ] False beliefs mapped to Vehicle, Internal, and External categories

---

## STEP 3: Big Domino Validation

### 3.1 Logical Test
- [ ] If Big Domino falls, does the sale logically follow? YES
- [ ] Can you knock down the Big Domino without addressing every objection? YES
- [ ] Is the Big Domino achievable in one webinar/presentation? YES
- [ ] Does the Big Domino create an "of course!" reaction? YES
- [ ] Is it simple enough to understand immediately?

### 3.2 Emotional Test
- [ ] Big Domino connects to a deep desire (not surface want)
- [ ] Believing the Big Domino creates excitement and hope
- [ ] Big Domino dissolves the primary FEAR of the prospect
- [ ] It shifts identity: "I am someone who [believes this]"
- [ ] It creates urgency: "I need to act on this NOW"

---

## STEP 4: Big Domino Proof

### 4.1 Evidence Stack
- [ ] Personal story proving the Big Domino is true
- [ ] Client/student stories proving it works for others
- [ ] Data or research supporting the claim (if available)
- [ ] Logical argument explaining WHY it must be true
- [ ] Demonstration or example showing it in action

### 4.2 Presentation Strategy
- [ ] Big Domino introduced early in presentation (the "one thing")
- [ ] Entire presentation structured to knock down this ONE domino
- [ ] Each section/secret chips away at resistance to Big Domino
- [ ] Close directly ties back to Big Domino belief
- [ ] Stack Slide reinforces Big Domino with value proof

---

## STEP 5: Common Mistakes Check

### 5.1 Anti-Patterns
- [ ] NOT trying to knock down multiple Big Dominos (only ONE)
- [ ] NOT making Big Domino about price (it's about belief)
- [ ] NOT making it too complex (must be one sentence)
- [ ] NOT confusing Big Domino with unique mechanism
- [ ] NOT leaving Big Domino implicit (must be explicitly stated)

---

## Validation
- [ ] Big Domino written as one clear sentence
- [ ] Tested with 3-5 prospects: does believing it make them want to buy?
- [ ] Entire pitch restructured around knocking down this ONE domino
- [ ] All Three Secrets in webinar connect back to Big Domino
- [ ] Close feels natural after Big Domino acceptance

---

## Scoring
- 26-30/30 = Crystal-clear Big Domino identified
- 20-25/30 = Strong Big Domino, refine proof stack
- 15-19/30 = Big Domino needs sharpening
- Below 15 = Big Domino not yet identified, return to research
