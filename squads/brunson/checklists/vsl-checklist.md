# VSL (Video Sales Letter) Script Quality Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets - VSL Framework + Expert Secrets Presentation Model
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When writing or reviewing a Video Sales Letter script

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Offer defined with complete value stack
- [ ] Target audience pain points and desires mapped
- [ ] Proof elements collected (testimonials, data, results)
- [ ] Big Domino and false beliefs identified

---

## STEP 1: Opening Hook (First 30 Seconds)

### 1.1 Attention Capture
- [ ] Opens with a bold claim, question, or pattern interrupt
- [ ] Speaks directly to the dream customer (not everyone)
- [ ] Creates curiosity that demands they keep watching
- [ ] Does NOT mention the product name yet
- [ ] Tone matches audience expectations (professional, casual, urgent)

### 1.2 Qualification
- [ ] Identifies who this is for: "If you [specific situation]..."
- [ ] Excludes wrong audience: "This is NOT for people who..."
- [ ] Viewer self-selects within first 60 seconds
- [ ] Promise of what they'll discover by watching
- [ ] Teases the unique mechanism or discovery

---

## STEP 2: Problem Agitation (Minutes 1-5)

### 2.1 Pain Amplification
- [ ] Describes their current situation accurately (they feel "seen")
- [ ] Uses their language and specific frustrations
- [ ] Agitates the pain: consequences of NOT solving this
- [ ] Emotional connection: "I know how it feels when..."
- [ ] Establishes shared enemy or broken system

### 2.2 Failed Solutions
- [ ] Acknowledges what they've already tried
- [ ] Validates: "It's not your fault, here's why those didn't work..."
- [ ] Positions old solutions as fundamentally flawed
- [ ] Creates openness for a NEW approach
- [ ] Eliminates competitor options without naming them

---

## STEP 3: Story and Solution (Minutes 5-15)

### 3.1 Origin Story
- [ ] Shares how you discovered the solution
- [ ] Epiphany Bridge format: struggle -> discovery -> transformation
- [ ] Story is relatable and emotionally engaging
- [ ] Positions the discovery as something special/unique
- [ ] Transitions from story to mechanism explanation

### 3.2 Unique Mechanism
- [ ] Introduces WHY this solution works (the mechanism)
- [ ] Mechanism has a proprietary name
- [ ] Explains it simply (5th grade reading level)
- [ ] Differentiates from everything else they've tried
- [ ] Creates "aha moment" for the viewer

### 3.3 Proof and Results
- [ ] Your personal results with specifics
- [ ] 3-5 customer/student testimonials
- [ ] Before/after data or demonstrations
- [ ] Third-party validation if available
- [ ] Proof addresses "will this work for ME?" concern

---

## STEP 4: Offer Presentation (Minutes 15-25)

### 4.1 Offer Introduction
- [ ] Natural transition from proof to offer
- [ ] Offer named and positioned as the vehicle for their transformation
- [ ] Component stacking: each piece described with value
- [ ] Bonuses added to increase perceived value
- [ ] Total value contrasted against price

### 4.2 Price Justification
- [ ] Comparison to alternatives (coaching, courses, DIY)
- [ ] ROI calculation or payback scenario
- [ ] "If all this did was [ONE thing], would it be worth it?"
- [ ] Price feels like a no-brainer given the value
- [ ] Payment plan option mentioned (if applicable)

### 4.3 Guarantee
- [ ] Risk reversal guarantee clearly stated
- [ ] Bold and specific (not generic "satisfaction guaranteed")
- [ ] Duration appropriate for the offer type
- [ ] Guarantee builds confidence, not suspicion
- [ ] "You literally can't lose" positioning

---

## STEP 5: Close and CTA (Final Minutes)

### 5.1 Urgency and Scarcity
- [ ] Genuine reason to act NOW (not fake timers)
- [ ] Limited availability, expiring bonus, or rising price
- [ ] Consequences of waiting clearly stated
- [ ] Fear of missing out activated appropriately
- [ ] Deadline is real and enforced

### 5.2 Final CTA
- [ ] Clear, specific next step: "Click the button below"
- [ ] CTA repeated 2-3 times in closing section
- [ ] Final emotional appeal connecting back to their dream outcome
- [ ] Ends with confidence and certainty
- [ ] Post-VSL page or form matches CTA promise

---

## STEP 6: Technical Quality

### 6.1 Production
- [ ] Audio clear and professional
- [ ] Video format matches audience preference (talking head, slides, hybrid)
- [ ] Duration appropriate: 15-45 minutes depending on price point
- [ ] Captions/subtitles available
- [ ] Mobile-friendly viewing experience

---

## Validation
- [ ] Script read aloud for natural flow
- [ ] Timing verified against target duration
- [ ] All proof claims are truthful and verifiable
- [ ] Legal compliance checked (FTC guidelines for testimonials)
- [ ] A/B test plan for hook and CTA variations

---

## Scoring
- 32-36/36 = High-converting VSL ready to deploy
- 26-31/36 = Strong VSL, refine weak sections
- 20-25/36 = Significant gaps in persuasion flow
- Below 20 = Major rewrite needed using framework
