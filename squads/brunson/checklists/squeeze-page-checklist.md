# Squeeze Page Checklist

> Agent: brunson-pages
> Source: DotCom Secrets - Squeeze Page Funnel & Lead Generation
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building or auditing a squeeze page (opt-in page)

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Lead magnet created and ready for delivery
- [ ] Email autoresponder sequence connected
- [ ] Thank-you/bridge page prepared
- [ ] Traffic source identified (determines messaging angle)

---

## STEP 1: Above the Fold

### 1.1 Headline
- [ ] Clear, benefit-driven headline
- [ ] Speaks to the #1 pain or desire of the dream customer
- [ ] Under 15 words
- [ ] Creates curiosity or urgency

### 1.2 Sub-Headline
- [ ] Expands on headline promise
- [ ] Adds specificity (what they get, how fast)
- [ ] Under 25 words

### 1.3 Visual Element
- [ ] Hero image or mockup of the lead magnet
- [ ] Image reinforces the perceived value
- [ ] No stock photos that feel generic

### 1.4 Opt-In Form
- [ ] Visible above the fold without scrolling
- [ ] Asks for minimal information (name + email preferred)
- [ ] CTA button has action-oriented text (not just "Submit")
- [ ] Button color contrasts with page background

---

## STEP 2: Page Structure

### 2.1 Simplicity
- [ ] No navigation menu (no escape routes)
- [ ] No sidebar or distracting elements
- [ ] Single focused action: opt in
- [ ] Page loads in under 3 seconds

### 2.2 Curiosity-Based Design
- [ ] Page creates desire without revealing the full answer
- [ ] Bullet points hint at value without giving it away
- [ ] Reader feels they MUST opt in to get the answer

### 2.3 Trust Elements
- [ ] Privacy statement near the form ("We respect your privacy")
- [ ] No spam promise or unsubscribe mention
- [ ] Optional: social proof count ("Join 10,000+ subscribers")

---

## STEP 3: Messaging & Copy

### 3.1 Lead Magnet Promise
- [ ] Clearly states what the visitor will receive
- [ ] Emphasizes the transformation, not just the format
- [ ] Uses "Get Instant Access" or similar immediacy language

### 3.2 Bullet Points (3-5 max)
- [ ] Each bullet is a curiosity-driven benefit
- [ ] Bullets use "How to..." or "The secret to..." format
- [ ] No bullet reveals the full answer (open loops)

### 3.3 Urgency/Scarcity (Optional)
- [ ] If used, urgency is real and justified
- [ ] Countdown timer or limited availability stated
- [ ] Does not feel manipulative or fake

---

## STEP 4: Technical & Mobile

### 4.1 Mobile Optimization
- [ ] Page renders correctly on mobile devices
- [ ] Form is easy to fill on a phone
- [ ] Button is thumb-friendly size
- [ ] Text is readable without zooming

### 4.2 Speed & Tracking
- [ ] Page loads in under 3 seconds on mobile
- [ ] Tracking pixel installed (Facebook, Google, etc.)
- [ ] Thank-you page redirect works correctly
- [ ] Split test variation prepared (A/B test ready)

---

## Validation
- [ ] Page has ONE clear action (opt in)
- [ ] Headline and lead magnet are congruent
- [ ] Complete opt-in flow tested (submit --> thank you --> email delivery)
- [ ] Page viewed on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- [ ] No broken images, links, or form errors

---

## Scoring
- 26-30/30 = Launch-ready squeeze page
- 20-25/30 = Solid page, optimize minor elements
- 14-19/30 = Needs work on core conversion elements
- Below 14 = Rebuild from template
