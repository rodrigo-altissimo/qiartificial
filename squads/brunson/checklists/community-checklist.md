# Community Engagement Checklist

> Source: Expert Secrets + Traffic Secrets - Community Building
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building or evaluating community engagement and health

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Community platform selected and set up
- [ ] At least 50 members in the community
- [ ] Community purpose and rules defined
- [ ] Dedicated time or resources for community management

---

## STEP 1: Community Foundation

### 1.1 Purpose and Positioning
- [ ] Community purpose stated clearly in description/pinned post
- [ ] Rules of engagement posted and enforced
- [ ] Community positioned as exclusive/valuable (not open to everyone)
- [ ] Connection to the larger movement/cause is clear
- [ ] New members understand the purpose within first 5 minutes

### 1.2 Onboarding Experience
- [ ] Welcome message or video for new members
- [ ] Introduction prompt: "Tell us about yourself and your goal"
- [ ] Quick-start guide or orientation post pinned
- [ ] Buddy system or mentor assignment (for premium communities)
- [ ] First-week engagement prompts to prevent silent members

---

## STEP 2: Content and Activity

### 2.1 Leader-Generated Content
- [ ] Leader posts minimum 2-3 times per week
- [ ] Content mix: teaching, inspiration, behind-the-scenes, challenges
- [ ] Live sessions scheduled regularly (weekly or bi-weekly)
- [ ] Q&A opportunities available
- [ ] Leader responds to comments (at least selectively)

### 2.2 Member-Generated Content
- [ ] Members sharing wins and results regularly
- [ ] Members asking questions and helping each other
- [ ] User-generated content encouraged with prompts
- [ ] Best member content highlighted or featured
- [ ] Contribution feels natural, not forced

### 2.3 Engagement Prompts
- [ ] Daily or weekly discussion prompts posted
- [ ] Themed days (Win Wednesday, Feature Friday, etc.)
- [ ] Challenges or mini-competitions run monthly
- [ ] Polls and surveys used for engagement and feedback
- [ ] Member milestones acknowledged and celebrated

---

## STEP 3: Community Health Metrics

### 3.1 Activity Metrics
- [ ] Daily active members tracked (target: 10%+ of total members)
- [ ] Posts per day tracked (should be growing or stable)
- [ ] Comment rate per post tracked
- [ ] Member-to-member interactions happening (not just member-to-leader)
- [ ] Lurker-to-contributor ratio improving over time

### 3.2 Growth Metrics
- [ ] New members joining weekly/monthly
- [ ] Member retention rate tracked (how many stay after 30, 90 days)
- [ ] Reasons for leaving identified (exit surveys if possible)
- [ ] Net growth positive (more joining than leaving)
- [ ] Growth rate sustainable and manageable

### 3.3 Sentiment Metrics
- [ ] Overall tone positive and supportive
- [ ] Complaints addressed quickly and empathetically
- [ ] Members feel heard and valued
- [ ] No toxic behavior tolerated
- [ ] Member satisfaction surveyed quarterly

---

## STEP 4: Moderation and Safety

### 4.1 Community Guidelines
- [ ] Clear rules posted and visible
- [ ] No self-promotion without permission
- [ ] No spam or irrelevant content
- [ ] Respectful communication required
- [ ] Consequences for violations defined and enforced

### 4.2 Moderation Team
- [ ] At least one moderator besides the leader
- [ ] Moderators trained on guidelines and tone
- [ ] Response time for reported issues under 24 hours
- [ ] Escalation process for serious issues defined
- [ ] Moderation actions documented

---

## STEP 5: Monetization Integration

### 5.1 Value-First Approach
- [ ] Community provides genuine value (not just a sales channel)
- [ ] 80% value content, 20% promotional (or less)
- [ ] Offers positioned as tools to help members achieve their goals
- [ ] Premium tiers create ascension within the community
- [ ] Free members see enough value to want more (not excluded)

### 5.2 Revenue Attribution
- [ ] Revenue from community members tracked
- [ ] Community members have higher LTV than non-members (verify)
- [ ] Referrals from community members tracked
- [ ] Community reduces support costs (members help each other)
- [ ] Community increases retention for paid products

---

## STEP 6: Scaling the Community

### 6.1 Sustainability
- [ ] Community can function for a week without leader intervention
- [ ] Member-leaders or ambassadors identified and empowered
- [ ] Content and engagement systems are repeatable
- [ ] Growth does not degrade quality (moderation scales too)
- [ ] Technology supports current and projected member count

---

## Validation
- [ ] Community active with daily engagement
- [ ] Members report feeling valued and connected
- [ ] Community supports business goals (retention, revenue, referrals)
- [ ] Moderation systems working effectively
- [ ] Growth and health metrics tracked and trending positive

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Thriving, engaged community
- 24-29/34 = Healthy community, optimize engagement and growth
- 18-23/34 = Community needs more attention and structure
- Below 18 = Community at risk, intervention needed
