# Three Secrets Quality Checklist

> Source: Expert Secrets, Chapters 10-12 - The Three Secrets
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When crafting or validating the Three Secrets for a Perfect Webinar

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Big Domino identified and stated clearly
- [ ] False beliefs mapped (Vehicle, Internal, External)
- [ ] Epiphany Bridge stories collected (minimum 3)
- [ ] New Opportunity clearly defined

---

## STEP 1: Secret #1 - The Vehicle (New Opportunity)

### 1.1 False Belief Targeted
- [ ] Specific Vehicle false belief identified from research
- [ ] Belief stated in customer's own language
- [ ] Belief is the #1 reason they reject the vehicle
- [ ] Example: "Funnels only work for internet marketers"

### 1.2 Epiphany Bridge Story
- [ ] Story has a relatable character (you or a student)
- [ ] Backstory: character had the SAME false belief
- [ ] Journey: what happened that challenged the belief
- [ ] Epiphany moment: the "aha" that changed everything
- [ ] New belief: what they believe NOW instead
- [ ] Results: concrete outcomes from the new belief

### 1.3 Teaching Content
- [ ] Teaches a framework or concept related to the vehicle
- [ ] Content provides genuine value (not just a tease)
- [ ] Framework has a memorable name or acronym
- [ ] Audience can start implementing immediately
- [ ] But realizes full implementation needs your offer

### 1.4 Transition
- [ ] Bridges naturally to Secret #2
- [ ] Creates curiosity: "But there was still a problem..."
- [ ] Audience understands the vehicle but has internal doubt
- [ ] Smooth handoff maintaining emotional momentum

---

## STEP 2: Secret #2 - Internal Belief (They Can Do It)

### 2.1 False Belief Targeted
- [ ] Specific Internal false belief identified
- [ ] Belief is about THEIR ability, not the vehicle
- [ ] Stated in their voice: "I'm not [smart/techy/experienced] enough"
- [ ] This belief is the #1 self-doubt blocking action

### 2.2 Epiphany Bridge Story
- [ ] Different character than Secret #1 (ideally a student)
- [ ] Character is relatable to the audience's situation
- [ ] Character had the SAME internal doubt
- [ ] Story shows how they overcame despite the doubt
- [ ] Results: someone "just like them" succeeded
- [ ] Emotional impact: "if they can, I can"

### 2.3 Teaching Content
- [ ] Framework addresses HOW ordinary people achieve this
- [ ] Simplifies the process (makes it feel achievable)
- [ ] Provides a step-by-step or shortcut element
- [ ] Removes perceived complexity
- [ ] Builds confidence in their ability to succeed

### 2.4 Transition
- [ ] Bridges to Secret #3 naturally
- [ ] Addresses: "OK I believe in the vehicle and myself, BUT..."
- [ ] Introduces external factor concerns
- [ ] Momentum building toward the offer

---

## STEP 3: Secret #3 - External Belief (Now Is the Time)

### 3.1 False Belief Targeted
- [ ] Specific External false belief identified
- [ ] Belief is about external factors (timing, economy, competition)
- [ ] Example: "The market is too saturated" / "I don't have time"
- [ ] This is the last major excuse before buying

### 3.2 Epiphany Bridge Story
- [ ] Character faced the SAME external challenge
- [ ] Story shows success DESPITE the external factor
- [ ] Reframes the external factor as an opportunity or non-issue
- [ ] Creates urgency: "waiting actually makes it worse"
- [ ] Results: external factors didn't prevent success

### 3.3 Teaching Content
- [ ] Framework for overcoming or leveraging external factors
- [ ] Shows why NOW is actually ideal timing
- [ ] Provides data or evidence countering the external excuse
- [ ] Creates FOMO for not acting immediately
- [ ] Positions the offer as the solution to external barriers

### 3.4 Transition to Close
- [ ] All three beliefs now broken
- [ ] Audience should feel: vehicle works, I can do it, now is the time
- [ ] Natural bridge: "The only question is HOW to implement all this..."
- [ ] Leads directly into offer introduction
- [ ] Emotional peak reached before presenting the stack

---

## STEP 4: Three Secrets Integration

### 4.1 Coherence Check
- [ ] All three secrets support the Big Domino
- [ ] Stories are diverse (different characters, situations)
- [ ] Teaching content builds cumulatively
- [ ] Emotional arc progresses: curiosity -> belief -> desire
- [ ] Each secret is 10-15 minutes (total 30-45 minutes)

### 4.2 Quality Check
- [ ] Stories are practiced and polished
- [ ] Teaching frameworks are clear and actionable
- [ ] No secret is filler or repetitive
- [ ] Audience engagement maintained throughout all three
- [ ] Transitions between secrets feel natural

---

## Validation
- [ ] Three Secrets address Vehicle, Internal, and External beliefs
- [ ] Each secret has a complete Epiphany Bridge story
- [ ] Each secret includes genuine teaching content
- [ ] Combined, the three secrets make the offer feel like the obvious next step
- [ ] Tested with sample audience: beliefs successfully shifted

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Masterful Three Secrets presentation
- 24-29/34 = Strong secrets, polish stories and transitions
- 18-23/34 = Secrets need more emotional depth or better stories
- Below 18 = Return to false belief research and story collection
