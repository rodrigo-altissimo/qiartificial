# Guarantee Strength Checklist

> Source: Expert Secrets + DotCom Secrets - Risk Reversal
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When designing or evaluating the guarantee for any offer

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Offer defined with clear deliverables
- [ ] Price point set
- [ ] Understanding of industry-standard guarantees
- [ ] Confidence in your product's ability to deliver results

---

## STEP 1: Guarantee Type Selection

### 1.1 Guarantee Options Evaluated
- [ ] Money-Back Guarantee (30/60/90 days)
- [ ] Conditional Guarantee (results-based: "If you don't get X...")
- [ ] Double-Your-Money-Back Guarantee (extreme confidence play)
- [ ] "Keep Everything" Guarantee (refund but keep the materials)
- [ ] Performance Guarantee (tied to specific outcome)
- [ ] Selected type matches offer, price, and audience expectations

### 1.2 Duration Selection
- [ ] Duration long enough for customer to experience value
- [ ] Not so long they forget about the purchase
- [ ] Digital products: 30-60 days standard
- [ ] Coaching/programs: aligned with program length
- [ ] Physical products: 30-90 days depending on shipping

---

## STEP 2: Guarantee Strength

### 2.1 Risk Reversal Power
- [ ] Guarantee eliminates the #1 financial fear of buying
- [ ] Guarantee language is bold and confident (not wishy-washy)
- [ ] Customer feels they "can't lose" by trying
- [ ] Guarantee ADDS to the offer value (not an afterthought)
- [ ] Guarantee demonstrates YOUR confidence in the product

### 2.2 Specificity
- [ ] Guarantee is specific about what's covered
- [ ] Terms are clear and understandable (no legal jargon)
- [ ] Process for claiming guarantee is simple (not 47 steps)
- [ ] What they keep vs. what they return is clear
- [ ] Contact method for guarantee claims is stated

### 2.3 Boldness Scale
- [ ] Basic (1/5): "30-day money-back guarantee"
- [ ] Good (2/5): "60-day no-questions-asked money-back guarantee"
- [ ] Strong (3/5): "90-day results guarantee: if you don't [result], full refund"
- [ ] Bold (4/5): "Double your money back if you don't [specific result]"
- [ ] Extreme (5/5): "Keep everything AND get your money back"
- [ ] Selected level matches price point and confidence level

---

## STEP 3: Guarantee Positioning

### 3.1 Placement in Sales Process
- [ ] Guarantee mentioned after the offer stack (not before)
- [ ] Guarantee presented as "you literally can't lose"
- [ ] Guarantee has its own visual element (badge, seal, stamp)
- [ ] Guarantee repeated near the final CTA/buy button
- [ ] Guarantee addressed again in FAQ section

### 3.2 Language Framing
- [ ] Framed positively ("Try it risk-free") not defensively
- [ ] Named with a compelling title ("The [Brand] Promise")
- [ ] Written from customer's perspective, not legal perspective
- [ ] Includes a "worst case scenario" frame: "At worst, you get [X]"
- [ ] Connected to the transformation promise

---

## STEP 4: Guarantee Economics

### 4.1 Refund Rate Management
- [ ] Expected refund rate calculated (industry: 5-15%)
- [ ] Refund rate factored into pricing and profit margin
- [ ] Product quality ensures low refund rate
- [ ] Onboarding process reduces refund requests
- [ ] Satisfaction check-in before guarantee expires

### 4.2 Guarantee ROI
- [ ] Conversion increase from guarantee exceeds refund costs
- [ ] A/B tested: sales with guarantee vs. without
- [ ] Stronger guarantee tested vs. weaker guarantee
- [ ] Optimal guarantee identified through data
- [ ] Net revenue positive after accounting for refunds

---

## STEP 5: Protection Measures

### 5.1 Legitimate Safeguards
- [ ] Terms of guarantee documented clearly on sales page
- [ ] No unreasonable hurdles to claim (but basic process required)
- [ ] Conditional guarantees have clear, measurable conditions
- [ ] Refund process automated or streamlined
- [ ] Customer service trained on guarantee handling

### 5.2 Abuse Prevention
- [ ] Clear terms prevent serial refunders
- [ ] Digital product access revoked upon refund
- [ ] Refund requesters tagged for future reference
- [ ] "Keep everything" guarantee reserved for lower-ticket only
- [ ] Unusual refund patterns flagged for review

---

## Validation
- [ ] Guarantee type selected and terms written
- [ ] Guarantee is the strongest you're comfortable offering
- [ ] Guarantee positioned and designed in sales materials
- [ ] Refund process tested and working
- [ ] Guarantee increases conversion when tested

---

## Scoring
- 26-30/30 = Powerful, conversion-boosting guarantee
- 20-25/30 = Good guarantee, consider strengthening
- 15-19/30 = Guarantee too weak or poorly positioned
- Below 15 = Guarantee needs redesign or stronger offer confidence
