# Attractive Character Profile Completeness Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets, Chapter 4 - The Attractive Character
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building or auditing your Attractive Character for email and content

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Business and niche defined
- [ ] Target audience identified
- [ ] Willingness to share personal stories publicly
- [ ] Understanding that the AC is a persona (real but curated)

---

## STEP 1: Identity Elements

### 1.1 Backstory
- [ ] Origin story written (how you got into this field)
- [ ] Struggle/failure included (makes you relatable)
- [ ] Turning point/epiphany moment defined
- [ ] Story shows transformation (before/after)
- [ ] Story is honest and verifiable

### 1.2 Character Type Selected
- [ ] The Leader: inspires followers to a better future
- [ ] The Adventurer/Crusader: shares journey of discovery
- [ ] The Reporter/Evangelist: investigates and shares findings
- [ ] The Reluctant Hero: didn't want attention but results demanded it
- [ ] One primary type chosen (can have secondary)

### 1.3 Parables
- [ ] Minimum 5 teaching stories collected
- [ ] Each parable teaches a key principle through narrative
- [ ] Stories reference specific moments and conversations
- [ ] Stories are reusable across content formats
- [ ] Stories organized by theme/lesson

---

## STEP 2: Character Flaws and Polarity

### 2.1 Character Flaws
- [ ] At least 2-3 flaws acknowledged publicly
- [ ] Flaws are genuine (not humble-brags)
- [ ] Flaws make you more human and relatable
- [ ] Audience sees themselves in your imperfections
- [ ] Flaws do not undermine your expertise in your topic

### 2.2 Polarity
- [ ] Strong opinions stated on 3-5 topics in your niche
- [ ] Willing to take a stand that some will disagree with
- [ ] Polarity attracts ideal customers MORE than it repels
- [ ] Lines drawn: "I believe X, and if you believe Y, that's OK but..."
- [ ] Not offensive, just clear and confident in position

---

## STEP 3: Communication Style

### 3.1 Voice and Tone
- [ ] Writing voice documented (formal, casual, intense, humorous)
- [ ] Voice consistent across platforms and content types
- [ ] Signature phrases or expressions identified (minimum 3)
- [ ] Communication style matches target audience expectations
- [ ] Voice is authentically YOU (not copied from someone else)

### 3.2 Storylines
- [ ] Loss and Redemption storyline available
- [ ] Us vs. Them storyline defined
- [ ] Before and After storyline with specifics
- [ ] Amazing Discovery storyline prepared
- [ ] Secret Telling storyline ready ("What they don't want you to know...")

### 3.3 Content Themes
- [ ] 3-5 content pillars defined for the AC
- [ ] Each pillar has 10+ sub-topics or angles
- [ ] Content reinforces expertise AND personality
- [ ] Mix of teaching, entertaining, and inspiring content
- [ ] Content calendar reflects AC personality consistently

---

## STEP 4: AC in Email Marketing

### 4.1 Soap Opera Sequence Readiness
- [ ] Origin story ready for email 1 (setting the stage)
- [ ] High drama / backstory for email 2
- [ ] Epiphany moment for email 3
- [ ] Hidden benefits revealed in email 4
- [ ] Urgency and CTA for email 5
- [ ] AC personality shines through all 5 emails

### 4.2 Seinfeld Email Readiness
- [ ] 20+ Seinfeld email topics brainstormed
- [ ] Each email has an entertaining angle + lesson + CTA
- [ ] Daily life observations connected to business principles
- [ ] AC voice consistent and recognizable
- [ ] Audience looks forward to reading emails

---

## STEP 5: AC Across Platforms

### 5.1 Multi-Platform Consistency
- [ ] AC persona consistent on email, social, video, podcast
- [ ] Visual branding matches AC personality
- [ ] Bio/about page tells AC story compellingly
- [ ] Community interactions reflect AC character
- [ ] Guest appearances maintain AC voice and positioning

### 5.2 Evolution Plan
- [ ] AC can evolve over time without losing core identity
- [ ] New stories and experiences continuously added
- [ ] Audience grows with you (not left behind)
- [ ] Core values remain stable even as tactics change

---

## Validation
- [ ] AC profile documented on a single reference page
- [ ] 3 people in your audience can describe your AC accurately
- [ ] AC feels authentic (not performed or forced)
- [ ] Content creation is easier because AC is well-defined
- [ ] AC attracts dream customers and repels wrong audience

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Compelling, fully-developed Attractive Character
- 24-29/34 = Strong AC, develop more stories and polish voice
- 18-23/34 = AC needs more depth and personality definition
- Below 18 = Fundamental AC development needed before scaling content
