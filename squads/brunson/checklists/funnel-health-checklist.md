# Funnel Performance Health Check

> Source: Traffic Secrets + DotCom Secrets - Funnel Optimization
> Version: 1.0
> Use: Weekly/monthly review of live funnel performance metrics

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Funnel has been live for minimum 7 days
- [ ] At least 100 visitors have entered the funnel
- [ ] Analytics tracking confirmed working
- [ ] Baseline metrics established from first week

---

## STEP 1: Traffic Health

### 1.1 Traffic Volume
- [ ] Daily visitor count meets target threshold
- [ ] Traffic sources diversified (not 100% from one source)
- [ ] Traffic quality consistent (bounce rate under 70%)
- [ ] Cost per click (CPC) within acceptable range
- [ ] Click-through rate (CTR) above 1% for ads

### 1.2 Traffic Temperature
- [ ] Cold traffic converting at expected rate (1-3%)
- [ ] Warm traffic converting at expected rate (3-8%)
- [ ] Hot traffic converting at expected rate (8-20%)
- [ ] Retargeting campaigns active for non-converters
- [ ] Traffic temperature segmentation working

---

## STEP 2: Conversion Health

### 2.1 Opt-In Page
- [ ] Opt-in rate above 20% (cold traffic)
- [ ] Opt-in rate above 35% (warm traffic)
- [ ] Opt-in rate above 50% (hot traffic)
- [ ] If below targets, test headline variations
- [ ] If below targets, test lead magnet appeal

### 2.2 Sales Page
- [ ] Frontend conversion rate above 1% (cold)
- [ ] Frontend conversion rate above 3% (warm)
- [ ] Page scroll depth tracked and satisfactory
- [ ] Video play rate above 30% (if VSL)
- [ ] Video watch time above 50% (if VSL)

### 2.3 OTO Performance
- [ ] OTO 1 take rate above 15%
- [ ] OTO 2 take rate above 8%
- [ ] Order bump take rate above 25%
- [ ] Downsell conversion compensating for declined OTOs
- [ ] Each OTO profitable independently

---

## STEP 3: Revenue Health

### 3.1 Unit Economics
- [ ] Average Order Value (AOV) calculated and tracked
- [ ] Earnings Per Click (EPC) above Cost Per Click (CPC)
- [ ] Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) within target
- [ ] Lifetime Value (LTV) growing over time
- [ ] Refund rate below 10%

### 3.2 Profitability
- [ ] Frontend funnel breaking even or profitable
- [ ] Backend offers generating profit
- [ ] Follow-up sequences generating additional revenue
- [ ] Overall ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) above 2:1
- [ ] Profit margin above 30% after all costs

---

## STEP 4: Email Health

### 4.1 Follow-Up Performance
- [ ] Email open rate above 20%
- [ ] Email click rate above 2%
- [ ] Unsubscribe rate below 1% per email
- [ ] Soap Opera Sequence converting within 5 days
- [ ] Seinfeld emails maintaining engagement weekly

### 4.2 Deliverability
- [ ] Emails landing in primary inbox (test monthly)
- [ ] Bounce rate below 2%
- [ ] Spam complaint rate below 0.1%
- [ ] List hygiene performed monthly (remove cold subscribers)

---

## STEP 5: Optimization Priorities

### 5.1 Identify Weakest Link
- [ ] Calculate conversion rate at each funnel step
- [ ] Identify the step with lowest relative performance
- [ ] Focus optimization on the weakest step first
- [ ] A/B test one element at a time
- [ ] Allow statistical significance before declaring winner

---

## Validation
- [ ] All metrics documented in tracking spreadsheet
- [ ] Week-over-week trends analyzed
- [ ] Top 3 optimization priorities identified
- [ ] Next A/B tests planned and scheduled
- [ ] Budget allocation adjusted based on performance

---

## Scoring
- 30-35/35 = Healthy, high-performing funnel
- 24-29/35 = Good performance, optimize weak points
- 18-23/35 = Underperforming, significant optimization needed
- Below 18 = Funnel needs major intervention or rebuild
