# Dream 100 List Quality Checklist

> Source: Traffic Secrets, Chapters 2-4 - The Dream 100
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building and validating your Dream 100 list for traffic generation

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Dream Customer avatar complete (WHO from Secret Formula)
- [ ] Congregation points identified (WHERE from Secret Formula)
- [ ] At least 1 platform selected as primary focus
- [ ] Understanding of Dream 100 concept (people/places with your audience)

---

## STEP 1: List Building

### 1.1 Categories Covered
- [ ] Top influencers/thought leaders in your niche (minimum 20)
- [ ] Top podcasts your audience listens to (minimum 10)
- [ ] Top YouTube channels your audience watches (minimum 10)
- [ ] Top blogs/websites your audience reads (minimum 10)
- [ ] Top Facebook groups your audience belongs to (minimum 10)
- [ ] Top email newsletters in your space (minimum 10)
- [ ] Top forums or communities (minimum 5)
- [ ] Top events/conferences in your niche (minimum 5)

### 1.2 List Diversity
- [ ] Mix of mega (1M+ followers) and micro (10K-100K) influencers
- [ ] At least 30% are accessible/reachable (not just aspirational)
- [ ] Multiple platforms represented (not all on one platform)
- [ ] Both direct competitors AND adjacent market leaders included
- [ ] List includes both individual creators and media properties

---

## STEP 2: List Quality

### 2.1 Audience Validation
- [ ] Each Dream 100 target actually has YOUR dream customer in their audience
- [ ] Audience overlap verified (not just same industry, same PERSON)
- [ ] Audience is engaged (comments, shares, not just followers)
- [ ] Audience demographics match your ideal buyer
- [ ] Removed targets with wrong audience despite surface similarity

### 2.2 Opportunity Assessment
- [ ] Each target rated: Easy / Medium / Hard to access
- [ ] Identified which targets accept guest appearances
- [ ] Identified which targets accept sponsored content
- [ ] Identified which targets have affiliate programs
- [ ] Identified which targets are open to collaboration

---

## STEP 3: Contact Strategy

### 3.1 Relationship Building Plan
- [ ] Follow all 100 on their primary platform
- [ ] Engage authentically with their content daily (comments, shares)
- [ ] Purchased their products/services (at least top 20)
- [ ] Left genuine reviews or testimonials
- [ ] Connected on personal level (not just business pitch)

### 3.2 Outreach Sequence
- [ ] "Dig your well before you're thirsty" phase: 30-60 days of engagement
- [ ] First contact: provide value, ask nothing
- [ ] Second contact: offer collaboration or help
- [ ] Third contact: propose specific partnership
- [ ] Follow-up system in place (CRM or spreadsheet tracking)

---

## STEP 4: List Organization

### 4.1 Tracking System
- [ ] Spreadsheet or CRM with all 100 targets
- [ ] Contact information collected for each
- [ ] Relationship status tracked (cold/warm/hot/active)
- [ ] Last interaction date logged
- [ ] Outcome of each interaction recorded

### 4.2 Prioritization
- [ ] Top 10 "work your way in" targets identified
- [ ] Top 10 "buy your way in" targets identified
- [ ] Quick-win targets identified (easiest to access first)
- [ ] Long-term relationship targets identified
- [ ] Quarterly review of list planned

---

## STEP 5: Integration with Traffic Strategy

### 5.1 Work Your Way In
- [ ] Guest content plan for top 10 work-in targets
- [ ] Value proposition clear for each collaboration
- [ ] Content ready to deliver when opportunity arises
- [ ] Pitch templates prepared for different scenarios

### 5.2 Buy Your Way In
- [ ] Ad platforms identified where Dream 100 audiences exist
- [ ] Budget allocated for sponsored placement
- [ ] Affiliate/JV terms prepared
- [ ] Sponsored content formats defined (ad, email, shoutout)

---

## Validation
- [ ] Full list of 100 contacts documented and organized
- [ ] At least 20 contacted in first 30 days
- [ ] At least 5 relationships progressing toward collaboration
- [ ] Traffic from Dream 100 efforts is measurable
- [ ] List reviewed and updated monthly

---

## Scoring
- 28-32/32 = World-class Dream 100 strategy
- 22-27/32 = Strong list, intensify relationship building
- 16-21/32 = List needs more depth and follow-through
- Below 16 = Rebuild list with better audience validation
