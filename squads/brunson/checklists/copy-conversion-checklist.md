# Copy Conversion Elements Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets + Expert Secrets - Copywriting Principles
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When reviewing any sales copy for conversion optimization

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] First draft of copy completed
- [ ] Offer clearly defined with pricing
- [ ] Target audience research available
- [ ] Competitor copy analyzed for benchmarking

---

## STEP 1: Attention and Hook

### 1.1 Opening Power
- [ ] First sentence stops the reader/viewer in their tracks
- [ ] Hook relates to a CURRENT pain or desire (not abstract)
- [ ] Pattern interrupt used (unexpected angle or statement)
- [ ] Reader compelled to continue within 5 seconds
- [ ] Hook tested: would it work as a standalone social post?

### 1.2 Curiosity Loops
- [ ] Open loops created early (questions posed, answers promised later)
- [ ] "But first..." or "Before I share that..." transitions used
- [ ] Curiosity maintained throughout (not just at the beginning)
- [ ] Major reveal or payoff timed before the close
- [ ] Reader always has a reason to keep reading

---

## STEP 2: Emotional Engagement

### 2.1 Pain Points
- [ ] Specific pains mentioned (not generic "struggling")
- [ ] Language matches exactly what customer says
- [ ] Pain agitated: consequences of inaction described
- [ ] Reader feels understood and validated
- [ ] Pain connected to deeper emotional needs (status, security, freedom)

### 2.2 Desire Amplification
- [ ] Future state painted vividly (what life looks like after)
- [ ] Sensory language used (see, feel, hear the transformation)
- [ ] Emotional benefits highlighted (not just features)
- [ ] Status elevation implied (how others will perceive them)
- [ ] Desire feels attainable (not fantasy)

---

## STEP 3: Persuasion Elements

### 3.1 Social Proof
- [ ] Testimonials placed throughout (not just in one section)
- [ ] Variety: video, text, screenshots, case studies
- [ ] Testimonials address different objections
- [ ] Numbers cited: "10,000+ customers" or "97% satisfaction"
- [ ] Proof escalates as the ask increases

### 3.2 Authority
- [ ] Expert credentials mentioned naturally (not bragging)
- [ ] Results demonstrated with specifics
- [ ] Published works, media features, or endorsements cited
- [ ] Teaching/speaking experience referenced
- [ ] Authority built through competence, not just claims

### 3.3 Scarcity and Urgency
- [ ] Genuine scarcity element present (limited spots, expiring price)
- [ ] Urgency connected to real deadline or consequence
- [ ] "What happens if you wait" clearly articulated
- [ ] Fast-action incentive for immediate decision
- [ ] NOT fake countdown timers that reset

---

## STEP 4: Offer Clarity

### 4.1 What They Get
- [ ] Every component of the offer explicitly stated
- [ ] Benefits of each component explained (not just listed)
- [ ] Format and delivery method clear
- [ ] Time to implement or see results stated
- [ ] No ambiguity about what is included

### 4.2 Price Justification
- [ ] Value anchoring used (compare to alternatives)
- [ ] ROI scenario painted clearly
- [ ] "If all this did was [ONE thing]" close included
- [ ] Payment plan lowers perceived barrier
- [ ] Price positioned as an investment, not a cost

### 4.3 Risk Reversal
- [ ] Guarantee stated clearly and prominently
- [ ] Guarantee is bold enough to build confidence
- [ ] "Worse case scenario" addressed: "You get X even if Y"
- [ ] Guarantee removes the last objection
- [ ] Reader feels they literally cannot lose

---

## STEP 5: Call to Action

### 5.1 CTA Quality
- [ ] CTA is clear: reader knows EXACTLY what to do
- [ ] CTA uses action language: "Get", "Start", "Claim", "Join"
- [ ] CTA appears multiple times (not just at the end)
- [ ] CTA stands out visually (button, color, size)
- [ ] CTA creates micro-commitment: "Yes, I want [benefit]"

### 5.2 Objection Handling
- [ ] Top 5 objections addressed within the copy
- [ ] FAQ section handles remaining concerns
- [ ] Each objection answer reinforces the value
- [ ] "But what if..." scenarios addressed proactively
- [ ] Close feels inevitable, not pushy

---

## STEP 6: Technical Copy Quality

### 6.1 Readability
- [ ] Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- [ ] Subheadings every 3-5 paragraphs for scanning
- [ ] Bullet points for lists and benefits
- [ ] Bold text highlights key phrases
- [ ] 6th-8th grade reading level (Hemingway App check)

### 6.2 Flow and Rhythm
- [ ] Sentence length varies (short. Medium ones. And then longer, more descriptive sentences that build momentum.)
- [ ] Transitions between sections are smooth
- [ ] No section feels like a dead end
- [ ] Copy builds momentum toward the close
- [ ] Read aloud: sounds natural and conversational

---

## Validation
- [ ] Copy reviewed by someone in the target audience
- [ ] All claims truthful and verifiable
- [ ] Legal compliance checked (FTC, disclaimers)
- [ ] Copy proofread for grammar and spelling
- [ ] A/B test plan for key elements (headline, CTA, proof)

---

## Scoring
- 36-40/40 = Professional, conversion-optimized copy
- 28-35/40 = Strong copy, optimize identified weak areas
- 20-27/40 = Copy needs significant revision for conversion
- Below 20 = Major rewrite recommended using this framework
