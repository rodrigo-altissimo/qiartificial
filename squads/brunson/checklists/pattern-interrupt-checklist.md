# Pattern Interrupt Checklist

> Agent: brunson-hooks
> Source: Traffic Secrets - Pattern Interrupts & Scroll-Stopping Techniques
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When creating or auditing pattern interrupts for ads, videos, or content

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Target platform identified (Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, email, etc.)
- [ ] Audience browsing behavior understood for that platform
- [ ] Competitor content analyzed (what does the "normal" look like?)
- [ ] Core message or offer to deliver after the interrupt defined

---

## STEP 1: Visual Pattern Interrupts

### 1.1 Image/Video Disruption
- [ ] Visual stands out from typical feed content
- [ ] Uses unexpected colors, compositions, or imagery
- [ ] Does NOT look like a polished ad (native feel can win)
- [ ] Movement or contrast draws the eye (for video: first 1-3 seconds)

### 1.2 Format Breaking
- [ ] Uses a format the audience does not expect
- [ ] Example: text-heavy image in a visual feed
- [ ] Example: raw/unpolished video among polished content
- [ ] Example: unusual aspect ratio or layout

### 1.3 Face & Emotion
- [ ] Human face visible (if applicable)
- [ ] Expression conveys strong emotion (surprise, intensity, joy)
- [ ] Eye contact with the viewer/camera
- [ ] Face is close-up (fills the frame)

---

## STEP 2: Verbal/Text Pattern Interrupts

### 2.1 Opening Line
- [ ] First sentence breaks the expected pattern
- [ ] Does NOT start with "Hey guys" or generic openers
- [ ] Uses a bold claim, strange statement, or shocking fact
- [ ] Reader/viewer stops mid-scroll to process

### 2.2 Contradiction Interrupt
- [ ] Statement contradicts common belief in the niche
- [ ] Audience thinks "Wait, what?" and must engage
- [ ] Contradiction is defensible (not just shock for shock's sake)

### 2.3 Direct Address
- [ ] Calls out the specific audience directly
- [ ] "If you're a [specific person] who [specific situation]..."
- [ ] Reader feels personally identified and spoken to
- [ ] Creates instant relevance and self-selection

---

## STEP 3: Structural Interrupts

### 3.1 Timing
- [ ] Interrupt happens in the first 1-3 seconds (video)
- [ ] Interrupt is in the first line (text/email)
- [ ] Interrupt is the first visual element (image/page)
- [ ] No slow build-up before the interrupt

### 3.2 Contrast with Environment
- [ ] Analyzed what "normal" content looks like on the platform
- [ ] Interrupt is deliberately different from the norm
- [ ] Stands out in a feed of 100 similar posts
- [ ] Different enough to notice, relevant enough to care

### 3.3 Pattern-Break Sequences
- [ ] For longer content: interrupts repeated every 60-90 seconds
- [ ] Each interrupt resets attention (new angle, visual, or statement)
- [ ] Prevents the audience from zoning out
- [ ] Each interrupt deepens engagement (not just random breaks)

---

## STEP 4: Platform-Specific Tuning

### 4.1 Social Media Feeds
- [ ] Optimized for silent autoplay (captions, visuals carry the message)
- [ ] Thumbnail/preview is the interrupt itself
- [ ] Works in both mobile and desktop formats
- [ ] Aspect ratio matches platform best practices

### 4.2 Email
- [ ] Subject line IS the pattern interrupt
- [ ] Preview text reinforces the interrupt
- [ ] First line of body continues the disruption
- [ ] Email does not look like a newsletter template

### 4.3 Landing Pages
- [ ] Headline is the pattern interrupt
- [ ] Page design breaks expectations for the niche
- [ ] Above-the-fold content stops the visitor from bouncing
- [ ] No generic stock imagery or template feel

---

## Validation
- [ ] Interrupt tested against 5 pieces of competitor content
- [ ] Clearly stands out when placed in a simulated feed/inbox
- [ ] 3 people outside the project confirmed "I would stop and look"
- [ ] Interrupt leads naturally into the core message (not just shock)
- [ ] Interrupt is appropriate for the brand and audience

---

## Scoring
- 24-28/28 = Scroll-stopping interrupt, ready to deploy
- 18-23/28 = Good interrupt, sharpen one element
- 12-17/28 = Not disruptive enough, needs bolder approach
- Below 12 = Will blend in, redesign from scratch
