# Funnel Unit Economics Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets - Funnel Math + Traffic Secrets
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When calculating and validating the financial viability of a funnel

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Funnel live with at least 500 visitors
- [ ] Payment and analytics data accessible
- [ ] Ad spend data available (if running paid traffic)
- [ ] Understanding of basic funnel math concepts

---

## STEP 1: Cost Metrics

### 1.1 Traffic Costs
- [ ] Cost Per Click (CPC) calculated per traffic source
- [ ] Cost Per 1,000 Impressions (CPM) tracked
- [ ] Total ad spend tracked daily and monthly
- [ ] Organic traffic cost estimated (content creation time/cost)
- [ ] CPC trending down or stable over time

### 1.2 Customer Acquisition Costs
- [ ] Cost Per Lead (CPL) calculated: ad spend / total leads
- [ ] Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) calculated: ad spend / total customers
- [ ] CPA compared against industry benchmarks
- [ ] CPA sustainable at current scale
- [ ] CPA for each traffic source compared

---

## STEP 2: Revenue Metrics

### 2.1 Frontend Revenue
- [ ] Earnings Per Click (EPC) calculated: total revenue / total clicks
- [ ] EPC compared against CPC (must be higher for profitability)
- [ ] Frontend conversion rate: buyers / total visitors
- [ ] Average frontend order value calculated
- [ ] Order bump revenue tracked separately

### 2.2 OTO Revenue
- [ ] OTO 1 revenue tracked separately
- [ ] OTO 2 revenue tracked separately
- [ ] Downsell revenue tracked
- [ ] Total OTO revenue as percentage of frontend revenue
- [ ] OTOs increasing AOV by 30%+ (target)

### 2.3 Average Order Value (AOV)
- [ ] AOV calculated: total revenue / total orders
- [ ] AOV with OTOs vs. without OTOs compared
- [ ] AOV trending upward over time
- [ ] AOV exceeds CPA (breakeven or profitable frontend)
- [ ] AOV benchmarked against similar funnels in market

---

## STEP 3: Profitability Analysis

### 3.1 Frontend Profitability
- [ ] Frontend profit per customer: AOV - CPA - COGS
- [ ] Frontend ROAS (Return on Ad Spend): revenue / ad spend
- [ ] Frontend ROAS above 1:1 (breakeven) target
- [ ] If frontend is negative, loss per customer quantified
- [ ] Frontend loss acceptable if backend LTV compensates

### 3.2 Backend Profitability
- [ ] Backend revenue per customer calculated (30-day, 90-day, 365-day)
- [ ] Backend conversion rate from frontend buyers
- [ ] Backend profit margin calculated
- [ ] Total customer profitability: frontend + backend
- [ ] Backend makes up majority of total profit

### 3.3 Lifetime Value (LTV)
- [ ] 30-day LTV calculated
- [ ] 90-day LTV calculated
- [ ] 12-month LTV calculated or projected
- [ ] LTV increasing over time (more backend offers, better retention)
- [ ] LTV:CAC ratio above 3:1 (minimum viable), ideally 5:1+

---

## STEP 4: Key Ratios

### 4.1 Essential Ratios
- [ ] EPC > CPC (clicks are profitable)
- [ ] AOV > CPA (orders are profitable)
- [ ] LTV > 3x CAC (business is sustainable)
- [ ] Refund rate < 10% (product-market fit)
- [ ] Churn rate < 10% monthly (retention health)

### 4.2 Breakeven Analysis
- [ ] Breakeven point identified: how many sales to cover fixed costs
- [ ] Time to breakeven per customer calculated
- [ ] Breakeven on ad spend: days to recover CPA
- [ ] Cash flow positive on 30-day rolling basis
- [ ] Margin of safety: how much can CPA increase before unprofitable

---

## STEP 5: Optimization Levers

### 5.1 Revenue Optimization
- [ ] Highest-impact revenue lever identified (conversion rate, AOV, LTV)
- [ ] 10% improvement in key metric modeled financially
- [ ] Price testing planned or completed
- [ ] New OTO or order bump opportunities identified
- [ ] Backend offer expansion planned

### 5.2 Cost Optimization
- [ ] Ad creative refresh reducing CPC over time
- [ ] Audience targeting refinement improving conversion
- [ ] Funnel page optimization reducing cost per conversion
- [ ] Automation reducing fulfillment and support costs
- [ ] Overhead costs reviewed and trimmed where possible

---

## STEP 6: Financial Projections

### 6.1 Scaling Model
- [ ] Revenue projected at 2x, 5x, 10x current traffic
- [ ] Costs projected at each scale level
- [ ] Margin maintained or improved at scale
- [ ] Cash requirements for scaling calculated
- [ ] Risk factors at each scale level identified

---

## Validation
- [ ] All key metrics calculated and documented
- [ ] Financial model built in spreadsheet
- [ ] Model validated against 30 days of actual data
- [ ] Top 3 financial improvement opportunities identified
- [ ] Monthly financial review cadence established

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Clear, profitable unit economics
- 24-29/34 = Healthy economics, optimize for maximum profit
- 18-23/34 = Economic gaps need addressing before scaling
- Below 18 = Unit economics unsustainable, restructure before spending
