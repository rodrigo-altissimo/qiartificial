# Identity Framework Checklist

> Agent: brunson-advisor
> Source: Expert Secrets - Identity & Attractive Character Framework
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building or auditing the identity and attractive character for a brand or funnel

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Business niche and market clearly defined
- [ ] Personal story and background documented
- [ ] Target audience's aspirational identity understood
- [ ] Competitors' positioning analyzed

---

## STEP 1: Attractive Character Foundation

### 1.1 Backstory
- [ ] Origin story documented (where you started)
- [ ] Key struggle or "dark moment" identified
- [ ] Transformation arc clear (from where to where)
- [ ] Story is relatable to the dream customer
- [ ] Vulnerability is genuine, not manufactured

### 1.2 Character Traits
- [ ] Parables/teaching stories collected (minimum 5)
- [ ] Character flaws acknowledged (makes you human)
- [ ] Polarizing opinions identified (what you stand for AND against)
- [ ] Unique voice and communication style defined

### 1.3 Identity Type (Select Primary)
- [ ] The Leader: "Follow me, I've been where you are"
- [ ] The Adventurer/Crusader: "Join me on this mission"
- [ ] The Reporter/Evangelist: "I discovered this and must share"
- [ ] The Reluctant Hero: "I didn't want this role, but here I am"

---

## STEP 2: Belief System

### 2.1 Core Beliefs
- [ ] 3-5 core beliefs clearly articulated
- [ ] Each belief challenges the status quo in your market
- [ ] Beliefs naturally attract your dream customer
- [ ] Beliefs naturally repel non-ideal customers
- [ ] Beliefs are defensible with stories and evidence

### 2.2 Cause/Mission
- [ ] Bigger mission beyond making money defined
- [ ] Mission resonates with the target audience's values
- [ ] "We are fighting against [enemy/injustice]" is clear
- [ ] "We are fighting for [future/vision]" is clear

### 2.3 Us vs Them
- [ ] Clear "in-group" identity defined (who WE are)
- [ ] Clear "out-group" contrast (what we are NOT)
- [ ] Shared vocabulary or terminology established
- [ ] Group identity creates belonging and pride

---

## STEP 3: Communication & Content

### 3.1 Voice DNA
- [ ] Tone defined (authoritative, casual, inspirational, etc.)
- [ ] Vocabulary patterns identified (signature phrases, power words)
- [ ] Content style consistent across all channels
- [ ] Voice is recognizable even without seeing the name

### 3.2 Story Inventory
- [ ] Origin story (how you got started)
- [ ] Epiphany stories (key breakthrough moments)
- [ ] "I was wrong" stories (humility and growth)
- [ ] Student/client success stories (proof of method)
- [ ] Behind-the-scenes stories (authenticity and access)
- [ ] Minimum 10 stories in inventory

### 3.3 Content Pillars
- [ ] 3-5 content themes identified
- [ ] Each pillar connects to a core belief
- [ ] Each pillar naturally leads to an offer
- [ ] Content calendar built around these pillars

---

## STEP 4: Identity Deployment

### 4.1 Across Funnels
- [ ] Identity elements present in all squeeze pages
- [ ] Attractive Character voice in all sales copy
- [ ] Email sequences written in the AC voice
- [ ] Webinars and presentations embody the identity

### 4.2 Social Presence
- [ ] Social media profiles reflect the identity
- [ ] Content consistently reinforces core beliefs
- [ ] Engagement style matches the character type
- [ ] Community interactions embody the brand identity

---

## Validation
- [ ] 3 people in target market can describe your brand in 1 sentence
- [ ] Brand identity is distinct from top 3 competitors
- [ ] All content from the last 30 days is consistent with identity
- [ ] Attractive Character elements present in every funnel
- [ ] Story inventory has minimum 10 usable stories

---

## Scoring
- 28-32/32 = Strong, cohesive identity, fully deployed
- 22-27/32 = Identity clear, needs deployment in some areas
- 16-21/32 = Identity partially defined, significant gaps
- Below 16 = Identity framework needs foundational work
