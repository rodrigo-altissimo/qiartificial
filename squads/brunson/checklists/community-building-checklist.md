# Community Building Checklist

> Agent: brunson-movement
> Source: Expert Secrets - Mass Movement & Community Building
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building or auditing a community around a brand, offer, or movement

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Charismatic leader / Attractive Character established
- [ ] Cause or mission defined (what the movement stands for)
- [ ] New opportunity identified (the vehicle the community rallies around)
- [ ] Platform for community interaction selected
- [ ] Initial seed members identified (first 10-50 true believers)

---

## STEP 1: Community Foundation

### 1.1 Shared Identity
- [ ] Group name or label defined (what members call themselves)
- [ ] Shared vocabulary established (insider terms and phrases)
- [ ] Visual identity elements (logos, colors, symbols)
- [ ] "We are the people who..." statement articulated
- [ ] Clear distinction from outsiders (us vs them)

### 1.2 Shared Mission
- [ ] Mission statement is inspiring and specific
- [ ] Every member can articulate why they are here
- [ ] Mission is bigger than any individual product or offer
- [ ] Progress toward the mission is visible and celebrated

### 1.3 Entry Ritual
- [ ] Clear onboarding process for new members
- [ ] Welcome message or video from the leader
- [ ] Introduction prompt ("Share your story and goal")
- [ ] Quick win delivered in the first 48 hours

---

## STEP 2: Engagement Architecture

### 2.1 Content Rhythm
- [ ] Regular content schedule (daily, weekly, or event-based)
- [ ] Mix of content types (teaching, inspiration, stories, wins)
- [ ] Leader is visibly active and engaged
- [ ] Content sparks discussion, not just consumption

### 2.2 Member Interaction
- [ ] Members encouraged to help and support each other
- [ ] Peer recognition systems in place (shoutouts, highlights)
- [ ] Collaboration opportunities exist (challenges, projects)
- [ ] Toxic behavior has clear rules and enforcement

### 2.3 Rituals & Traditions
- [ ] Weekly or monthly recurring events (live calls, Q&As)
- [ ] Celebration rituals for member wins
- [ ] Challenges or campaigns that create shared experience
- [ ] Traditions that reinforce the group identity

---

## STEP 3: Value & Retention

### 3.1 Value Delivery
- [ ] Members get value they cannot get elsewhere
- [ ] Exclusive content, access, or resources for members
- [ ] Community provides accountability and support
- [ ] Members see tangible results from participation

### 3.2 Retention Mechanics
- [ ] Progress tracking visible (levels, milestones, badges)
- [ ] Long-term goals that keep members engaged
- [ ] "Cost of leaving" is high (relationships, status, access)
- [ ] Regular re-engagement for inactive members

### 3.3 Member Success Spotlight
- [ ] Success stories shared regularly
- [ ] Process for collecting and publishing wins
- [ ] Members become proof of the method/movement
- [ ] Success creates aspiration for newer members

---

## STEP 4: Growth & Scaling

### 4.1 Organic Growth
- [ ] Members naturally invite others (word of mouth)
- [ ] Shareable content and moments created regularly
- [ ] Referral incentives or programs in place
- [ ] Community reputation attracts new members

### 4.2 Moderation & Quality
- [ ] Moderators or community managers assigned
- [ ] Clear community guidelines posted and enforced
- [ ] Quality of discussion maintained as group grows
- [ ] Spam and self-promotion policies defined

---

## Validation
- [ ] Community has active daily engagement (not a ghost town)
- [ ] Member retention rate above 60% after 90 days
- [ ] At least 5 member success stories documented
- [ ] Community generates organic referrals
- [ ] Leader is visibly present and engaged weekly

---

## Scoring
- 26-30/30 = Thriving community with strong engagement
- 20-25/30 = Active community, strengthen weak areas
- 14-19/30 = Community exists but engagement is low
- Below 14 = Community needs foundational rebuilding
