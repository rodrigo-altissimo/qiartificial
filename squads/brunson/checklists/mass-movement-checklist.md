# Mass Movement Building Checklist

> Source: Expert Secrets, Chapters 2-3 - Creating a Mass Movement
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building a movement around your expert brand and new opportunity

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Expert positioning established
- [ ] New Opportunity defined
- [ ] Audience of at least 100 engaged followers
- [ ] Passion for the cause beyond just profit

---

## STEP 1: The Charismatic Leader

### 1.1 Leader Identity
- [ ] You have accepted the role of leader (not hiding behind brand)
- [ ] Your origin story is crafted and compelling
- [ ] You share your journey publicly (struggles AND victories)
- [ ] You are polarizing (take strong stances, not vanilla)
- [ ] You communicate with certainty and conviction

### 1.2 Leader Behaviors
- [ ] You lead by example (practice what you preach)
- [ ] You create content consistently (daily/weekly)
- [ ] You are accessible to your community (engage, reply)
- [ ] You have a signature look, phrase, or style
- [ ] You stand for something AND against something

### 1.3 Attractive Character Traits
- [ ] Backstory: relatable origin that builds connection
- [ ] Parables: teaching stories you tell repeatedly
- [ ] Character flaws: vulnerabilities that build trust
- [ ] Polarity: willingness to draw a line in the sand
- [ ] Productivity: consistently producing and shipping

---

## STEP 2: The Future-Based Cause

### 2.1 Vision Definition
- [ ] Clear vision of the future you are creating
- [ ] Vision stated in ONE compelling sentence
- [ ] Vision is about your followers' transformation, not your profit
- [ ] Vision is bigger than any single product or service
- [ ] Vision creates identity: followers see themselves IN it

### 2.2 Cause Elements
- [ ] Us vs. Them dynamic established (who is the common enemy?)
- [ ] Common enemy is NOT a person but a system, belief, or approach
- [ ] Enemy is something followers already dislike
- [ ] Your movement provides the alternative to the enemy
- [ ] Followers can rally around the cause with pride

### 2.3 Identity Shift
- [ ] New identity label created for followers (Funnel Hackers, etc.)
- [ ] Identity is aspirational and positive
- [ ] Wearing/displaying the identity is desirable
- [ ] Community rituals reinforce the identity
- [ ] Identity distinguishes followers from non-members

---

## STEP 3: The New Opportunity

### 3.1 Vehicle Positioning
- [ ] New Opportunity is the vehicle to achieve the vision
- [ ] It replaces the old broken approach (not just improves it)
- [ ] Vehicle has a unique and memorable name
- [ ] Vehicle is accessible: "anyone" can use it with guidance
- [ ] Vehicle creates hope that this time will be different

### 3.2 Opportunity-Cause Alignment
- [ ] Using the vehicle ALSO advances the cause
- [ ] Followers who succeed become proof for the movement
- [ ] Success stories fuel recruitment of new followers
- [ ] Movement grows as more people achieve results
- [ ] The cause and the vehicle are inseparable

---

## STEP 4: Movement Amplification

### 4.1 Community Building
- [ ] Dedicated space for followers to gather (group, forum, event)
- [ ] Regular live interaction (weekly live, monthly call)
- [ ] Follower achievements celebrated publicly
- [ ] User-generated content encouraged and shared
- [ ] Mentorship or peer support structure exists

### 4.2 Movement Marketing
- [ ] Content consistently reinforces the cause
- [ ] Hashtags or slogans used across platforms
- [ ] Events (virtual or live) deepen commitment
- [ ] Merchandise or physical artifacts available
- [ ] Referral mechanism: followers recruit followers

---

## Validation
- [ ] All 3 components present: Charismatic Leader + Cause + New Opportunity
- [ ] Followers can articulate what the movement stands for
- [ ] Community is active without constant leader intervention
- [ ] New members joining organically through word-of-mouth
- [ ] Movement has survived at least one controversy or challenge

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = True mass movement in motion
- 24-29/34 = Strong foundation, amplify community elements
- 18-23/34 = Movement components need strengthening
- Below 18 = Foundation building needed before movement can ignite
