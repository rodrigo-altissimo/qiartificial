# Launch Sequence Completeness Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets + Expert Secrets - Product Launch Framework
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When planning and executing a product or offer launch

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Product/offer finalized and ready for delivery
- [ ] Email list of at least 500 subscribers
- [ ] Sales page and funnel built and tested
- [ ] Launch timeline established (typically 7-14 days)

---

## STEP 1: Pre-Launch Phase (2-4 Weeks Before)

### 1.1 Audience Priming
- [ ] Content about the PROBLEM published (not the product)
- [ ] Stories shared about discovering the solution
- [ ] Curiosity built through "something big is coming" teasers
- [ ] Audience surveyed on their biggest challenge (engagement + research)
- [ ] Dream 100 notified about upcoming launch

### 1.2 Affiliate/JV Recruitment
- [ ] Top 20 potential affiliates contacted
- [ ] Affiliate resources prepared (swipe copy, images, links)
- [ ] Commission structure defined and competitive
- [ ] Affiliate contest or incentives planned (if applicable)
- [ ] Affiliate onboarding and tracking system configured

### 1.3 Technical Preparation
- [ ] All funnel pages built, tested, and QA'd
- [ ] Email sequences written and loaded
- [ ] Payment processor tested with real transactions
- [ ] Analytics and tracking verified on all pages
- [ ] Support team briefed on the offer and FAQ

---

## STEP 2: Launch Content Phase (Days 1-7)

### 2.1 Content Piece 1: The Opportunity
- [ ] Introduces the new opportunity or big idea
- [ ] Shares your story and why this matters
- [ ] Provides genuine value (teaches something useful)
- [ ] Creates desire to learn more
- [ ] CTA: "Stay tuned for the next piece"

### 2.2 Content Piece 2: The Transformation
- [ ] Shows proof of concept with case studies/results
- [ ] Addresses the #1 objection (Vehicle false belief)
- [ ] Deeper teaching builds authority
- [ ] Social proof from others who've used the approach
- [ ] CTA: "Part 3 coming soon - this is where it all clicks"

### 2.3 Content Piece 3: The Ownership Experience
- [ ] Reveals how to implement (the how-to teaser)
- [ ] Addresses Internal and External false beliefs
- [ ] Paints the picture of life after implementation
- [ ] Sets up the offer: "I've made it easy for you..."
- [ ] CTA: "Doors open tomorrow - be ready"

---

## STEP 3: Cart Open Phase (Days 7-10)

### 3.1 Cart Open Day
- [ ] Launch email: "It's here! [Offer Name] is now available"
- [ ] Sales page live and accessible
- [ ] Social media announcement across all platforms
- [ ] Affiliate notifications sent: "Promote now!"
- [ ] Early-bird or fast-action bonus active

### 3.2 Mid-Launch Emails
- [ ] Day 1: Launch announcement + key benefits
- [ ] Day 2: Case study or testimonial spotlight
- [ ] Day 3: FAQ and objection handling email
- [ ] Day 4: Behind-the-scenes or personal story email
- [ ] Day 5: Social proof compilation email

### 3.3 Live Events During Launch
- [ ] Live Q&A session planned (1-2 during cart open)
- [ ] Webinar or training event for launch registrants
- [ ] Live social media engagement during launch
- [ ] Community posts and engagement
- [ ] Affiliate leaderboard updates (if applicable)

---

## STEP 4: Cart Close Phase (Final 48 Hours)

### 4.1 Urgency Sequence
- [ ] 48-hour warning email sent
- [ ] 24-hour warning email with final bonuses reminder
- [ ] 12-hour email: "Last chance" with scarcity emphasis
- [ ] 6-hour email: personal appeal from founder
- [ ] 1-hour email: "Closing in 60 minutes"
- [ ] Final email: "Doors closing NOW" at deadline

### 4.2 Deadline Enforcement
- [ ] Cart closes at exact stated time
- [ ] Offer genuinely unavailable after deadline
- [ ] Redirect page for post-deadline visitors (waitlist)
- [ ] Bonuses removed as promised
- [ ] Price changes enforced (if rising price was the scarcity)

---

## STEP 5: Post-Launch Phase

### 5.1 Buyer Experience
- [ ] Thank you and welcome email sent immediately
- [ ] Onboarding sequence activated for buyers
- [ ] Access to product/course delivered instantly
- [ ] Quick-start guide or orientation provided
- [ ] Support resources communicated

### 5.2 Non-Buyer Follow-Up
- [ ] "Cart closed" email acknowledging they didn't buy
- [ ] Waitlist offer for next opening
- [ ] Continued value emails (don't abandon non-buyers)
- [ ] Downsell or alternative offer after 7 days
- [ ] Re-engagement for future launches

### 5.3 Launch Debrief
- [ ] Total revenue from launch calculated
- [ ] Key metrics documented: conversion rate, email stats, ad ROAS
- [ ] Affiliate performance reviewed
- [ ] What worked well documented
- [ ] What to improve for next launch noted

---

## Validation
- [ ] Complete launch timeline documented and followed
- [ ] All emails sent on schedule
- [ ] Technical systems worked without major issues
- [ ] Revenue met or exceeded projections
- [ ] Post-launch improvement plan created

---

## Scoring
- 34-38/38 = Professional, high-performing product launch
- 27-33/38 = Strong launch, optimize weak phases
- 20-26/38 = Launch had significant gaps
- Below 20 = Launch fundamentals need to be established
