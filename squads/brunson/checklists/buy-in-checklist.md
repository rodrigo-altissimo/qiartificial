# Buy Your Way In Campaign Checklist

> Source: Traffic Secrets, Chapters 8-10 - Buying Your Way In
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When launching or optimizing paid traffic campaigns

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Funnel live and tested (at least test purchase completed)
- [ ] Dream 100 list identifies where audience congregates
- [ ] Ad budget allocated (minimum $10-$50/day for testing)
- [ ] Tracking pixels installed on all funnel pages
- [ ] Creative assets prepared (images, videos, copy)

---

## STEP 1: Ad Platform Selection

### 1.1 Platform-Market Match
- [ ] Facebook/Instagram Ads if audience is B2C or broad B2B
- [ ] Google Ads if audience searches for your solution actively
- [ ] YouTube Ads if your offer benefits from video demonstration
- [ ] LinkedIn Ads if targeting specific B2B professionals
- [ ] TikTok Ads if targeting younger demographics
- [ ] Selected platform matches where Dream 100 audience lives

### 1.2 Platform Setup
- [ ] Business ad account created and verified
- [ ] Payment method connected and approved
- [ ] Tracking pixel/tag installed and firing on all pages
- [ ] Custom conversions or events configured
- [ ] Retargeting audiences created from website visitors

---

## STEP 2: Audience Targeting

### 2.1 Dream 100 Audience Targeting
- [ ] Interest targeting based on Dream 100 influencers
- [ ] Lookalike audiences from email list (if 1000+ emails)
- [ ] Lookalike audiences from customer list
- [ ] Custom audiences from funnel visitors
- [ ] Targeting refined to match Dream Customer demographics

### 2.2 Audience Segmentation
- [ ] Cold audience campaigns (never interacted)
- [ ] Warm audience campaigns (visited, engaged, not bought)
- [ ] Hot audience campaigns (buyers for upsells)
- [ ] Each segment has appropriate messaging and offer
- [ ] Budget split: 60% cold, 30% warm, 10% hot

---

## STEP 3: Ad Creative

### 3.1 Hook Testing
- [ ] Minimum 5 different hooks created per ad set
- [ ] Hooks follow Brunson's pattern: curiosity, controversy, story
- [ ] Visual hook grabs attention in first 0.5 seconds
- [ ] Copy hook captures interest in first line
- [ ] Each hook tested with $5-$10 before scaling

### 3.2 Ad Formats
- [ ] Image ads created (minimum 3 variations)
- [ ] Video ads created (15-sec, 30-sec, 60-sec versions)
- [ ] Carousel ads for multi-point storytelling
- [ ] Text-heavy vs. image-heavy versions tested
- [ ] UGC-style creative tested alongside polished versions

### 3.3 Copy Framework
- [ ] Ad copy follows Hook-Story-Offer framework
- [ ] Long-form and short-form copy versions tested
- [ ] CTA is clear and matches the landing page
- [ ] Social proof included (numbers, testimonials)
- [ ] Urgency or scarcity element present (if genuine)

---

## STEP 4: Campaign Structure

### 4.1 Testing Phase
- [ ] Campaign Objective matches goal (conversions, leads, traffic)
- [ ] Budget set for testing ($5-$20 per ad set per day)
- [ ] Multiple ad sets with different audiences
- [ ] Multiple ads per ad set (3-5 variations)
- [ ] Testing period: minimum 3-5 days before conclusions

### 4.2 Optimization
- [ ] Winning ads identified (CTR above 1%, CPC below target)
- [ ] Losing ads paused (after sufficient data, 1000+ impressions)
- [ ] Winning audiences expanded with lookalikes
- [ ] Ad frequency monitored (pause if above 3-4)
- [ ] Creative refreshed every 2-4 weeks

---

## STEP 5: Metrics and Scaling

### 5.1 Key Metrics Tracked
- [ ] Cost Per Click (CPC) below industry average
- [ ] Click-Through Rate (CTR) above 1%
- [ ] Cost Per Lead (CPL) within profitable range
- [ ] Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) below customer LTV
- [ ] Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) above 2:1

### 5.2 Scaling Protocol
- [ ] Increase budget by 20% every 3 days when profitable
- [ ] Duplicate winning ad sets to new audiences
- [ ] Launch on additional platforms once one platform mastered
- [ ] Never scale a losing campaign (fix first, then scale)
- [ ] Emergency pause criteria defined (CPA > 2x target)

---

## Validation
- [ ] At least one profitable campaign running
- [ ] Attribution is accurate (last-click and assisted conversions)
- [ ] Creative pipeline ensures fresh ads every 2 weeks
- [ ] Paid traffic feeding email list (traffic you own growing)
- [ ] Monthly ad spend report reviewed with ROI calculation

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Professional paid traffic operation
- 24-29/34 = Strong foundation, optimize and scale
- 18-23/34 = Campaign needs significant optimization
- Below 18 = Fundamentals needed before spending more
