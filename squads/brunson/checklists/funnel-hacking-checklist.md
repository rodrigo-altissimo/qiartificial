# Funnel Hacking Checklist

> Agent: brunson-advisor
> Source: DotCom Secrets - Funnel Hacking & Reverse Engineering Strategy
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When reverse-engineering a competitor's or industry leader's funnel

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Target funnel identified (competitor or aspirational funnel)
- [ ] Budget allocated for purchasing through the funnel ($50-$500)
- [ ] Screenshot and recording tools ready
- [ ] Spreadsheet or document prepared for notes
- [ ] Separate email address set up for tracking follow-up sequences

---

## STEP 1: Funnel Discovery

### 1.1 Find the Funnel
- [ ] Identified through Dream 100 list or competitor research
- [ ] Confirmed the funnel is actively running (has traffic/ads)
- [ ] Entry point URL documented
- [ ] Traffic sources identified (where are they advertising?)

### 1.2 Pre-Funnel Context
- [ ] Screenshotted the ad or content that drives traffic
- [ ] Noted the hook used in the ad/content
- [ ] Documented the platform (Facebook, YouTube, Google, etc.)
- [ ] Identified the traffic temperature being targeted

---

## STEP 2: Page-by-Page Analysis

### 2.1 Landing/Squeeze Page
- [ ] Screenshotted the full page (top to bottom)
- [ ] Documented the headline and sub-headline
- [ ] Noted the lead magnet or entry offer
- [ ] Recorded the CTA and form fields
- [ ] Checked for trust elements (testimonials, logos, counts)

### 2.2 Sales Page / Offer Page
- [ ] Screenshotted the full sales page
- [ ] Documented the value stack (every component and price)
- [ ] Noted the guarantee type and terms
- [ ] Recorded urgency/scarcity elements
- [ ] Identified the pricing and payment options

### 2.3 Upsell/OTO Pages
- [ ] Documented each upsell in sequence
- [ ] Noted pricing for each OTO
- [ ] Recorded the relationship between OTOs and core offer
- [ ] Checked for downsell pages on "No thanks"

### 2.4 Thank You / Confirmation Page
- [ ] Screenshotted the final page
- [ ] Noted any additional offers or CTAs
- [ ] Checked for community links or next steps
- [ ] Documented the delivery mechanism

---

## STEP 3: Email Sequence Analysis

### 3.1 Follow-Up Capture
- [ ] All emails received are saved/forwarded to analysis folder
- [ ] Subject lines documented with open rates (if trackable)
- [ ] Email frequency noted (daily, every other day, weekly)
- [ ] Sequence length tracked (how many emails total?)

### 3.2 Email Content Analysis
- [ ] Identified Soap Opera vs Seinfeld email patterns
- [ ] Noted CTAs in each email (what are they promoting?)
- [ ] Tracked when the first pitch appears
- [ ] Documented story arcs and persuasion techniques

---

## STEP 4: Funnel Metrics Estimation

### 4.1 Traffic Analysis
- [ ] Estimated traffic volume (SimilarWeb, SpyFu, or ad library)
- [ ] Identified primary traffic sources
- [ ] Noted ad spend indicators (how many ad variations running?)
- [ ] Estimated funnel age (how long has it been running?)

### 4.2 Revenue Modeling
- [ ] Estimated frontend conversion rate (industry average 1-3%)
- [ ] Calculated average cart value (frontend + OTOs)
- [ ] Modeled potential monthly revenue
- [ ] Identified the "profit center" (which offer drives most revenue?)

---

## STEP 5: Insights & Application

### 5.1 What to Model
- [ ] Listed top 3 elements to model in your own funnel
- [ ] Identified the funnel's strongest persuasion element
- [ ] Noted unique mechanisms or positioning to adapt
- [ ] Documented the overall funnel type and flow

### 5.2 What to Improve
- [ ] Identified weaknesses or gaps in the hacked funnel
- [ ] Listed opportunities to differentiate your version
- [ ] Noted what you would do differently
- [ ] Planned your own funnel improvements based on insights

---

## Validation
- [ ] Complete funnel documented with screenshots from entry to exit
- [ ] Email sequence tracked for minimum 14 days
- [ ] Revenue model estimated with assumptions documented
- [ ] Top 3 actionable insights extracted for your own funnel
- [ ] Funnel hack report compiled and stored in swipe file

---

## Scoring
- 28-32/32 = Comprehensive funnel hack, full intelligence gathered
- 22-27/32 = Strong analysis, fill in remaining gaps
- 16-21/32 = Partial hack, need deeper analysis on key areas
- Below 16 = Incomplete, re-run the full funnel hack process
