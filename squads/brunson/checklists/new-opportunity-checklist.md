# New Opportunity Creation Checklist

> Source: Expert Secrets, Chapters 4-5 - Creating a New Opportunity
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When crafting a "new opportunity" offer instead of an "improvement" offer

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Target market defined with clear pain points
- [ ] Existing solutions in the market researched
- [ ] Understanding of why "improvement offers" fail
- [ ] Expert positioning established or in progress

---

## STEP 1: Opportunity vs. Improvement

### 1.1 Offer Type Validation
- [ ] Offer is positioned as a NEW vehicle (not "better version of old")
- [ ] Does NOT say "do more of what you're doing"
- [ ] Does NOT require admitting past mistakes to adopt
- [ ] Feels like switching to something NEW and exciting
- [ ] Creates hope and excitement, not guilt or shame

### 1.2 Status Test
- [ ] Offer increases perceived status of the buyer
- [ ] Buyer does NOT feel stupid for past choices
- [ ] Adoption signals intelligence and forward-thinking
- [ ] Offer creates aspirational identity association
- [ ] Switching to this opportunity feels like an upgrade

---

## STEP 2: Opportunity Framework

### 2.1 The Vehicle
- [ ] New vehicle (opportunity) clearly named and defined
- [ ] It replaces the old vehicle entirely (not a modification)
- [ ] Clear reason why the old vehicle can't work anymore
- [ ] New vehicle has a believable mechanism for results
- [ ] Vehicle is unique to you or your system

### 2.2 Future-Based Cause
- [ ] Opportunity is tied to something bigger than the buyer
- [ ] There is a "movement" element to joining
- [ ] Buyer becomes part of a tribe or community
- [ ] The cause creates identity shift: "I am a [new identity]"
- [ ] Future vision is exciting and worth fighting for

---

## STEP 3: Opportunity Stack

### 3.1 Core Promise
- [ ] One clear, specific result promised
- [ ] Result is measurable or clearly visible
- [ ] Timeline for result is stated or implied
- [ ] Promise is bold but believable
- [ ] Promise addresses the #1 desire of dream customer

### 3.2 Unique Mechanism
- [ ] HOW the result is achieved is explained with a unique framework
- [ ] Framework has a proprietary name
- [ ] Mechanism is simple to understand in 2-3 sentences
- [ ] Mechanism explains why THIS works when others didn't
- [ ] Mechanism creates "aha moment" for the prospect

### 3.3 Proof of Concept
- [ ] Your own results with this new opportunity
- [ ] At least 3 others have achieved results
- [ ] Results documented with specifics
- [ ] Proof demonstrates the mechanism works
- [ ] Proof addresses "but will it work for ME?" concern

---

## STEP 4: Opportunity Positioning

### 4.1 Against the Market
- [ ] Old approach/industry clearly defined as the "thing to escape"
- [ ] New opportunity positioned as the obvious better path
- [ ] Transition from old to new feels natural and logical
- [ ] Competition is the OLD WAY, not other new opportunities
- [ ] You are the ONLY one offering this specific vehicle

### 4.2 Story Support
- [ ] Origin story: how YOU discovered this new opportunity
- [ ] Bridge story: how it transformed YOUR life/business
- [ ] Social proof stories: how OTHERS have transformed
- [ ] Before/after contrast is vivid and emotional
- [ ] Stories address all 3 types of false beliefs

---

## Validation
- [ ] Can explain the new opportunity in one clear sentence
- [ ] 5 prospects tested: they see it as NEW, not improvement
- [ ] Prospects feel excited, not defensive, when hearing the pitch
- [ ] Opportunity is differentiated from all known competitors
- [ ] Offer creates a "switching" behavior, not "adding on"

---

## Scoring
- 28-32/32 = Compelling new opportunity ready to market
- 22-27/32 = Strong opportunity, refine positioning
- 16-21/32 = More work needed on differentiation
- Below 16 = Rethink: you may have an improvement offer disguised
