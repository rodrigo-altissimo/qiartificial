# Product Launch Checklist

> Agent: brunson-launch
> Source: DotCom Secrets & Expert Secrets - Product Launch Funnels
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When planning or executing a product launch using funnel methodology

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Product or offer fully developed and ready to deliver
- [ ] Value Ladder position for this product defined
- [ ] Email list or audience built (minimum viable size for launch)
- [ ] Launch timeline set (recommended: 2-4 weeks)
- [ ] Sales page and checkout ready or in development

---

## STEP 1: Pre-Launch (Weeks 1-2)

### 1.1 Audience Warming
- [ ] Pre-launch content series planned (3-4 pieces minimum)
- [ ] Each piece teaches value while building anticipation
- [ ] Content addresses false beliefs one by one
- [ ] Open loops created between each content piece
- [ ] Social proof and excitement signals shared

### 1.2 Dream 100 Activation
- [ ] Dream 100 list contacted for potential partnerships
- [ ] Affiliate/JV partners briefed with launch details
- [ ] Promotional calendar shared with partners
- [ ] Swipe copy and resources provided to affiliates
- [ ] Commission structure finalized and communicated

### 1.3 List Building Sprint
- [ ] Lead magnet related to the launch offer deployed
- [ ] Pre-launch squeeze page live
- [ ] Waitlist or early-bird registration open
- [ ] Social media buildup content running
- [ ] Retargeting audiences being built from engagement

---

## STEP 2: Launch Week

### 2.1 Cart Open Event
- [ ] Launch event defined (webinar, video series, challenge, or live)
- [ ] Registration page live with clear date/time
- [ ] Reminder sequences set up (email + SMS if applicable)
- [ ] Scarcity/urgency elements real and in place (deadline, limited spots)
- [ ] Cart open timing synchronized across all channels

### 2.2 Sales Funnel Activation
- [ ] Sales page live and fully tested
- [ ] Checkout/order form working and tested
- [ ] OTO/upsell pages in place
- [ ] Email purchase confirmation sequence ready
- [ ] Access delivery automated (course, download, etc.)

### 2.3 Launch Emails
- [ ] Day 1: Cart open announcement email
- [ ] Day 2: Case study or proof email
- [ ] Day 3: FAQ and objection handling email
- [ ] Day 4-5: Urgency and final chance emails
- [ ] Cart close email with hard deadline

---

## STEP 3: During Launch

### 3.1 Momentum Management
- [ ] Daily engagement monitored (emails, social, comments)
- [ ] Live Q&A or support available during launch window
- [ ] Testimonials and wins shared in real-time
- [ ] Urgency genuinely increases as deadline approaches
- [ ] Sales metrics tracked daily (visits, conversions, revenue)

### 3.2 Objection Handling
- [ ] Common objections addressed in follow-up content
- [ ] FAQ page updated with new questions as they arise
- [ ] Personal outreach to high-value prospects on the fence
- [ ] "Last chance" messaging for cart close day

### 3.3 Partner Communication
- [ ] Affiliates updated on performance daily
- [ ] Top performers recognized and motivated
- [ ] Additional swipe copy provided if needed
- [ ] Leaderboard shared to drive friendly competition

---

## STEP 4: Cart Close

### 4.1 Final Push
- [ ] Final email sequence sent (2-3 emails on closing day)
- [ ] Social media posts emphasizing deadline
- [ ] Countdown timer accurate and enforced
- [ ] Last-minute bonus or incentive deployed (optional)

### 4.2 Deadline Enforcement
- [ ] Cart actually closes at the stated time
- [ ] Price increases or bonuses removed as promised
- [ ] "Cart closed" page ready for after deadline
- [ ] No exceptions (protects integrity for future launches)

---

## STEP 5: Post-Launch

### 5.1 Fulfillment
- [ ] All buyers have received access within 24 hours
- [ ] Welcome email and onboarding sequence triggered
- [ ] Quick-start guide or first-step content delivered
- [ ] Support channels open for new customer questions

### 5.2 Launch Debrief
- [ ] Total revenue calculated
- [ ] Conversion rates analyzed at each funnel step
- [ ] Best-performing emails and content identified
- [ ] Lessons learned documented for next launch
- [ ] Affiliate/partner results tracked and commissions calculated

### 5.3 Post-Launch Ascension
- [ ] Follow-up sequence for non-buyers planned (future reopening)
- [ ] Ascension offers prepared for new buyers
- [ ] Evergreen version of the funnel planned (if applicable)
- [ ] Next launch date tentatively set

---

## Validation
- [ ] Complete launch timeline executed as planned
- [ ] All funnel pages and emails functioning throughout
- [ ] Revenue goal met or exceeded (or gap analyzed)
- [ ] All buyers fulfilled and onboarded
- [ ] Debrief document completed with actionable insights

---

## Scoring
- 34-38/38 = Masterful launch execution
- 27-33/38 = Strong launch, optimize for next time
- 20-26/38 = Decent launch with significant improvement areas
- Below 20 = Launch fundamentals need rebuilding
