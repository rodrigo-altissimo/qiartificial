# Sales Page Copy Completeness Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets + Expert Secrets - Sales Page Architecture
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When writing or auditing a long-form sales page

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Offer stack finalized with pricing
- [ ] Target audience research complete
- [ ] Proof elements gathered (testimonials, screenshots, data)
- [ ] Headline variations brainstormed (minimum 5)

---

## STEP 1: Above the Fold

### 1.1 Headline
- [ ] Grabs attention and creates curiosity
- [ ] Speaks to specific desire or pain point
- [ ] Contains a benefit or promise
- [ ] Under 15 words (punchy and scannable)
- [ ] Tested at least 3 variations

### 1.2 Sub-Headline
- [ ] Expands on the headline's promise
- [ ] Adds specificity (numbers, timeframes, outcomes)
- [ ] Creates "I need to keep reading" reaction
- [ ] Addresses the dream customer directly

### 1.3 Hero Section
- [ ] Product image or video thumbnail visible
- [ ] Primary CTA button above the fold
- [ ] Brief credibility indicator (as seen in, # of customers)
- [ ] Clean design, not cluttered
- [ ] Mobile-responsive layout verified

---

## STEP 2: Problem and Agitation

### 2.1 Problem Statement
- [ ] Opens with relatable problem description
- [ ] Uses customer's own language and phrases
- [ ] Agitates: "What happens if you DON'T solve this..."
- [ ] Lists specific symptoms they experience
- [ ] Reader thinks: "This is exactly my situation"

### 2.2 Failed Solutions
- [ ] Acknowledges what they've tried before
- [ ] Explains why those approaches fail
- [ ] Validates their frustration: "It's not your fault"
- [ ] Creates openness for a different approach
- [ ] Positions your solution as fundamentally different

---

## STEP 3: Story and Solution

### 3.1 Origin/Discovery Story
- [ ] Your personal journey or discovery story
- [ ] Shows struggle -> breakthrough -> results
- [ ] Makes you relatable and trustworthy
- [ ] Introduces your unique method/mechanism
- [ ] Transitions from story to solution explanation

### 3.2 Solution Presentation
- [ ] Unique mechanism explained simply
- [ ] "Here's why this is different from everything else"
- [ ] 3-5 key benefits listed with explanations
- [ ] Each benefit addresses a specific desire or pain
- [ ] Solution feels achievable and accessible

---

## STEP 4: Proof Section

### 4.1 Social Proof
- [ ] Minimum 5 testimonials throughout the page
- [ ] Mix of video and text testimonials
- [ ] Testimonials address different objections
- [ ] Real names and photos (with permission)
- [ ] Results are specific (not vague "great product!")

### 4.2 Authority Proof
- [ ] Credentials or experience mentioned
- [ ] Media features or logos displayed
- [ ] Number of customers/students served
- [ ] Awards, certifications, or recognitions
- [ ] Case studies with detailed before/after

---

## STEP 5: Offer Section

### 5.1 Offer Presentation
- [ ] Transition from proof to offer feels natural
- [ ] Each offer component listed with description
- [ ] Individual value assigned to each component
- [ ] Bonuses listed separately with their own values
- [ ] Total value prominently displayed

### 5.2 Price Presentation
- [ ] Price anchored against higher alternatives
- [ ] Price anchored against total value of stack
- [ ] "Not $X, not $Y, just $Z" format used
- [ ] Payment plan option available and displayed
- [ ] Price feels like a steal relative to transformation

### 5.3 CTA
- [ ] Buy button large and prominent
- [ ] Button text is action-oriented ("Get Instant Access")
- [ ] Button appears multiple times on long pages
- [ ] Minimal form fields at checkout
- [ ] Security badges near payment area

---

## STEP 6: Risk Reversal and Close

### 6.1 Guarantee
- [ ] Bold guarantee prominently displayed
- [ ] Duration clear (30, 60, 90 days)
- [ ] Terms simple and easy to understand
- [ ] Positioned as "you can't lose"
- [ ] Graphic/badge for visual impact

### 6.2 Urgency and Scarcity
- [ ] Genuine reason to buy now
- [ ] Expiring bonuses, limited quantity, or rising price
- [ ] Countdown timer (if deadline-based)
- [ ] "What happens if you wait" section
- [ ] FOMO triggered appropriately

### 6.3 FAQ Section
- [ ] Top 5-10 objections addressed as FAQs
- [ ] Each FAQ ends with a reason to buy
- [ ] Technical questions answered (delivery, access)
- [ ] Refund policy clearly stated
- [ ] Final CTA after FAQ section

---

## Validation
- [ ] Page read aloud for flow and natural tone
- [ ] All links tested and working
- [ ] Page loads fast (under 3 seconds)
- [ ] Mobile experience tested end-to-end
- [ ] Legal compliance verified (disclaimers, privacy, terms)

---

## Scoring
- 36-40/40 = Professional, high-converting sales page
- 28-35/40 = Strong page, optimize weak sections
- 20-27/40 = Significant gaps, major revisions needed
- Below 20 = Complete rewrite recommended
