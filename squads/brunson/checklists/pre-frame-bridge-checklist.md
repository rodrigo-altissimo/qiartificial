# Pre-Frame Bridge Setup Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets, Chapter 7 - Funnels and Pre-Frames
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When setting up the bridge between traffic source and funnel

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Traffic source identified (paid, organic, email, affiliate)
- [ ] Landing page / funnel entry point built
- [ ] Dream customer avatar defined
- [ ] Understanding of traffic temperature (cold/warm/hot)

---

## STEP 1: Understand the Pre-Frame

### 1.1 Current Frame Assessment
- [ ] Where is the visitor coming from? (specific platform/page)
- [ ] What were they doing before they clicked? (context)
- [ ] What state of mind are they in? (curious, skeptical, eager)
- [ ] What do they expect to see next? (promise match)
- [ ] What is their relationship with you? (none, aware, fan)

### 1.2 Desired Frame
- [ ] What state of mind do you NEED them in to buy?
- [ ] What beliefs must they hold before seeing your offer?
- [ ] What objections must be dissolved before the pitch?
- [ ] What emotional state drives action for this offer?
- [ ] What level of trust is required for this price point?

---

## STEP 2: Bridge Type Selection

### 2.1 For Cold Traffic
- [ ] Article/blog post bridge (educate first)
- [ ] Video bridge (build rapport and authority)
- [ ] Quiz/survey bridge (engage and segment)
- [ ] Story-based bridge (Epiphany Bridge mini-version)
- [ ] Selected bridge warms traffic before offer exposure

### 2.2 For Warm Traffic
- [ ] Shorter bridge needed (they already know you)
- [ ] Reminder of transformation/results
- [ ] Social proof bridge (testimonials, case studies)
- [ ] Event-based bridge (limited time, special occasion)

### 2.3 For Hot Traffic
- [ ] Minimal bridge needed (direct to offer acceptable)
- [ ] Exclusive access framing
- [ ] Loyalty recognition element
- [ ] VIP or early-bird positioning

---

## STEP 3: Bridge Content Creation

### 3.1 Bridge Page Elements
- [ ] Headline matches traffic source promise
- [ ] Opening hooks attention in first 3 seconds
- [ ] Personal story or case study included
- [ ] One key belief shift accomplished
- [ ] Clear transition to the offer/funnel
- [ ] Single CTA pointing to next funnel page

### 3.2 Bridge Video (If Applicable)
- [ ] Hook in first 5 seconds
- [ ] Duration appropriate (2-5 min for cold, 1-2 min for warm)
- [ ] Establishes credibility quickly
- [ ] Shares relatable problem/solution
- [ ] Ends with clear CTA to continue

### 3.3 Message Congruence
- [ ] Ad headline echoed on bridge page
- [ ] Visual style consistent from ad to bridge
- [ ] Tone of voice matches traffic source context
- [ ] Promise from ad delivered or expanded on bridge
- [ ] No jarring shifts in topic or presentation

---

## STEP 4: Bridge-to-Funnel Transition

### 4.1 Seamless Handoff
- [ ] Bridge CTA leads directly to funnel page
- [ ] No confusing intermediate pages
- [ ] Visual design continuity maintained
- [ ] Emotional momentum preserved
- [ ] Curiosity increases through the transition

### 4.2 Frame Verification
- [ ] Visitor arrives at funnel in correct state of mind
- [ ] Main objections pre-handled by bridge
- [ ] Trust level appropriate for ask
- [ ] Desire for solution activated
- [ ] Ready to engage with the offer

---

## Validation
- [ ] Bridge tested with actual cold traffic sample
- [ ] Conversion rate at bridge CTA above 40%
- [ ] Time on bridge page appropriate (not bouncing instantly)
- [ ] Funnel conversion improves with bridge vs. without
- [ ] A/B test of bridge variations planned

---

## Scoring
- 26-30/30 = Expert-level pre-frame bridge
- 20-25/30 = Solid bridge, minor refinements
- 15-19/30 = Bridge needs strengthening
- Below 15 = Missing critical pre-frame elements
