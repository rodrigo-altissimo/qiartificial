# Secret Formula Checklist (WHO / WHERE / BAIT / RESULT)

> Source: DotCom Secrets, Chapter 1 - The Secret Formula
> Version: 1.0
> Use: Foundation check before building any funnel or campaign

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Business idea or existing business defined
- [ ] Market research completed (at minimum basic)
- [ ] Willingness to define a specific dream customer (not "everyone")

---

## STEP 1: WHO - Dream Customer

### 1.1 Demographics
- [ ] Age range defined (specific, not "18-65")
- [ ] Gender specified (if relevant to offer)
- [ ] Income level identified
- [ ] Geographic location narrowed
- [ ] Occupation or industry defined

### 1.2 Psychographics
- [ ] Top 3 desires/goals identified
- [ ] Top 3 fears/frustrations identified
- [ ] Core belief system understood
- [ ] Self-identity language mapped ("I am a...")
- [ ] Aspiration identity defined ("I want to become...")

### 1.3 Dream Customer Avatar
- [ ] Single person avatar created with a name
- [ ] Day-in-the-life narrative written
- [ ] Pain points ranked by intensity
- [ ] Buying behavior pattern identified
- [ ] Media consumption habits documented

---

## STEP 2: WHERE - Congregation Points

### 2.1 Online Congregations
- [ ] Top 5 websites they visit daily identified
- [ ] Top 3 social media platforms they use
- [ ] Top 5 influencers/thought leaders they follow
- [ ] Top 3 podcasts they listen to
- [ ] Top 3 YouTube channels they watch

### 2.2 Communities and Groups
- [ ] Facebook groups they belong to (list 5-10)
- [ ] Forums or Reddit communities they participate in
- [ ] Online communities or membership sites
- [ ] Professional associations or organizations
- [ ] Local meetups or events they attend

### 2.3 Dream 100 Seeds
- [ ] Initial list of 10 congregation owners/leaders
- [ ] These leaders have direct access to your dream customers
- [ ] Mix of large (aspirational) and small (accessible) targets

---

## STEP 3: BAIT - Irresistible Offer

### 3.1 Lead Magnet (Free Bait)
- [ ] Solves a specific, immediate problem
- [ ] Can be consumed in under 15 minutes
- [ ] Has a compelling, benefit-driven title
- [ ] Delivers one quick win
- [ ] Naturally creates desire for more (paid offer)

### 3.2 Offer Positioning
- [ ] Positioned as a "new opportunity" not "improvement"
- [ ] Uses language your dream customer already uses
- [ ] Clearly different from what competitors offer
- [ ] Value exceeds price by at least 10x (perceived)
- [ ] Solves the ONE thing they care about most

---

## STEP 4: RESULT - Transformation

### 4.1 Before/After State
- [ ] "Before" state clearly documented (pain, frustration)
- [ ] "After" state vividly described (dream outcome)
- [ ] Gap between before/after is emotionally compelling
- [ ] Transformation is believable and achievable
- [ ] Social proof exists for this transformation

### 4.2 Result Delivery
- [ ] Clear mechanism for HOW you deliver the result
- [ ] Timeline for results is specific and honest
- [ ] Success stories or case studies available
- [ ] Guarantee reduces risk of trying
- [ ] Result aligns with their aspiration identity

---

## Validation
- [ ] All 4 elements (WHO/WHERE/BAIT/RESULT) documented on one page
- [ ] Each element connects logically to the next
- [ ] Formula tested by explaining to someone outside your market
- [ ] They can understand and find it compelling
- [ ] Secret Formula serves as foundation for all funnels

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Rock-solid Secret Formula, ready to build
- 24-29/34 = Strong foundation, refine weak areas
- 18-23/34 = Gaps in formula, more research needed
- Below 18 = Return to market research phase
