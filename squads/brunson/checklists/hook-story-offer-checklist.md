# Hook, Story, Offer Checklist

> Agent: cross-agent (brunson-hooks + brunson-stories + brunson-offers)
> Source: DotCom Secrets & Expert Secrets - The HSO Framework
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When validating any piece of marketing against the Hook, Story, Offer framework

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Marketing asset to evaluate identified (ad, email, page, video, etc.)
- [ ] Target audience and dream customer defined
- [ ] Core offer the asset should drive toward known
- [ ] Platform where asset will appear identified

---

## STEP 1: The Hook

### 1.1 Hook Presence
- [ ] Asset has a clear, identifiable hook in the first 3 seconds / first line
- [ ] Hook stops the scroll, interrupts the pattern, or grabs attention
- [ ] Hook is specific to the target audience (not generic)
- [ ] Hook creates an open loop or curiosity gap

### 1.2 Hook Quality
- [ ] Hook makes a promise, asks a question, or makes a bold claim
- [ ] Hook is relevant to the offer that follows
- [ ] Hook would work even if the audience knows nothing about you
- [ ] Hook is truthful and the content delivers on it

### 1.3 Hook-Audience Match
- [ ] Hook speaks to the #1 desire or pain of the dream customer
- [ ] Hook calls out or identifies the target audience
- [ ] Non-ideal prospects would self-select out
- [ ] Hook matches the traffic temperature (cold needs stronger hook)

---

## STEP 2: The Story

### 2.1 Story Presence
- [ ] Asset contains a story element (not just facts and features)
- [ ] Story bridges the gap between the hook and the offer
- [ ] Story builds emotional connection with the audience
- [ ] Story is relevant to the transformation the offer provides

### 2.2 Story Structure (Epiphany Bridge)
- [ ] Backstory establishes relatability
- [ ] Struggle/journey creates emotional investment
- [ ] Epiphany moment shifts beliefs
- [ ] Transformation shows what is possible
- [ ] Bridge connects the story to the audience's situation

### 2.3 Belief Shifting
- [ ] Story addresses at least one false belief
- [ ] Vehicle belief: "This is the right approach" (addressed?)
- [ ] Internal belief: "I can do this" (addressed?)
- [ ] External belief: "It will work for me" (addressed?)
- [ ] Audience ends the story with a new empowering belief

### 2.4 Story Quality
- [ ] Story is authentic and specific (not generic or vague)
- [ ] Story has emotional peaks and valleys (not flat)
- [ ] Story length matches the context (short for ads, longer for pages)
- [ ] Story leaves the audience wanting the offer as the logical next step

---

## STEP 3: The Offer

### 3.1 Offer Presence
- [ ] Asset has a clear, specific offer or call to action
- [ ] Offer logically follows from the story
- [ ] Audience understands exactly what they are getting
- [ ] Offer feels like the obvious next step (not a hard sell)

### 3.2 Offer Quality
- [ ] Offer provides real value (not just a tease or gimmick)
- [ ] Value clearly outweighs the cost (money, time, or effort)
- [ ] Risk is addressed or reversed (guarantee, free trial, etc.)
- [ ] Urgency or scarcity element makes action feel timely

### 3.3 Offer Clarity
- [ ] CTA is unmissable and action-oriented
- [ ] Audience knows exactly what to do next (click, call, buy, opt in)
- [ ] No confusion about what happens after they take action
- [ ] Offer matches the promise made in the hook

---

## STEP 4: HSO Integration

### 4.1 Flow & Congruence
- [ ] Hook naturally leads into the Story
- [ ] Story naturally leads into the Offer
- [ ] All three elements feel like one cohesive piece (not three parts)
- [ ] Tone and voice are consistent throughout

### 4.2 The "One Thing" Test
- [ ] Hook, Story, and Offer all point to ONE core idea
- [ ] No competing messages or multiple pitches
- [ ] Audience walks away with one clear takeaway
- [ ] The "one thing" is aligned with the business goal

### 4.3 Platform Optimization
- [ ] HSO length and format match the platform (ad, email, page, video)
- [ ] Hook is optimized for the platform (visual for social, text for email)
- [ ] Story format fits the medium (video story vs written story)
- [ ] Offer CTA matches platform conventions (button, link, reply)

---

## STEP 5: Testing & Iteration

### 5.1 Variable Testing
- [ ] Same Story + Offer with 3 different Hooks tested
- [ ] Winning Hook with 2 different Story angles tested
- [ ] Best Hook + Story combo with Offer variations tested
- [ ] Performance data tracked per variation

### 5.2 Feedback Integration
- [ ] Audience response analyzed (comments, clicks, conversions)
- [ ] Weak element identified (Hook, Story, or Offer?)
- [ ] Iteration plan created for the weakest element
- [ ] Best-performing HSO combination documented in swipe file

---

## Validation
- [ ] All three elements (Hook, Story, Offer) clearly present
- [ ] Asset reviewed by someone outside the project for clarity
- [ ] HSO framework applied consistently across the campaign
- [ ] Performance benchmarks set for testing
- [ ] Asset ready for deployment on target platform

---

## Scoring
- 34-38/38 = Complete, powerful HSO execution
- 27-33/38 = Strong HSO, one element needs strengthening
- 20-26/38 = Framework present but lacks depth in key area
- Below 20 = HSO fundamentals need rework
