# Funnel Type Selection Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets, Chapter 8-10 - Funnel Types
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When choosing the right funnel type for an offer

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Value Ladder defined and validated
- [ ] Offer price point determined
- [ ] Traffic source identified
- [ ] Audience temperature understood (cold/warm/hot)

---

## STEP 1: Offer Classification

### 1.1 Price Point Assessment
- [ ] Free offer (lead gen) --> Use Lead Squeeze Funnel
- [ ] $1-$47 offer --> Use Tripwire/Two-Step Funnel
- [ ] $47-$297 offer --> Use Self-Liquidating Offer Funnel
- [ ] $297-$997 offer --> Use Perfect Webinar Funnel
- [ ] $997-$2,000 offer --> Use Webinar or Application Funnel
- [ ] $2,000+ offer --> Use Application/Phone Funnel or High-Ticket Closer

### 1.2 Offer Type Assessment
- [ ] Physical product --> Product Launch or Tripwire Funnel
- [ ] Digital product --> SLO or Webinar Funnel
- [ ] Service/coaching --> Application or Webinar Funnel
- [ ] Software/SaaS --> Free Trial or Tripwire Funnel
- [ ] Event/live --> Webinar or Challenge Funnel

---

## STEP 2: Frontend Funnel Selection

### 2.1 Lead Generation Funnels
- [ ] Squeeze Page Funnel: email opt-in for free resource
- [ ] Survey Funnel: segmentation before opt-in
- [ ] Summit Funnel: multi-speaker virtual event
- [ ] Quiz Funnel: engagement-based lead capture
- [ ] Selected funnel matches lead magnet type

### 2.2 Unboxing Funnels (Low Ticket)
- [ ] Two-Step Tripwire: free + shipping or $7-$27
- [ ] SLO Funnel: self-liquidating offer $27-$97
- [ ] Book Funnel: free + shipping physical book
- [ ] Cart Funnel: ecommerce-style product page
- [ ] OTO sequence designed (minimum 2 upsells)

---

## STEP 3: Middle Funnel Selection

### 3.1 Presentation Funnels
- [ ] Perfect Webinar Funnel: live or automated webinar
- [ ] Product Launch Funnel: Jeff Walker-style sequence
- [ ] Challenge Funnel: 5-30 day guided challenge
- [ ] Video Series Funnel: 3-4 part video sequence
- [ ] Presentation format matches audience preference

### 3.2 Selection Criteria
- [ ] Audience needs education before buying? --> Webinar/Video Series
- [ ] Audience needs social proof? --> Challenge/Launch
- [ ] Audience is warm/hot? --> Direct sales page OK
- [ ] Audience is cold? --> Longer nurture funnel required

---

## STEP 4: Backend Funnel Selection

### 4.1 High-Ticket Funnels
- [ ] Application Funnel: qualify before sales call
- [ ] Phone Funnel: direct to call booking
- [ ] High-Ticket Webinar: present then application
- [ ] VSL to Application: video sales letter then apply
- [ ] Sales team or closer available for calls

### 4.2 Continuity Funnels
- [ ] Membership Funnel: recurring subscription
- [ ] Newsletter Funnel: paid content subscription
- [ ] Retention mechanism defined
- [ ] Cancellation prevention strategy in place

---

## STEP 5: Funnel Stack Validation

### 5.1 Full Stack Check
- [ ] Frontend funnel feeds middle funnel
- [ ] Middle funnel feeds backend funnel
- [ ] Each funnel has follow-up sequence
- [ ] Funnels align with Value Ladder rungs
- [ ] No missing transition between funnels

---

## Validation
- [ ] Selected funnel type matches offer price point
- [ ] Funnel type proven in your market/niche
- [ ] Technical ability to build selected funnel exists
- [ ] Traffic strategy compatible with funnel type
- [ ] Funnel economics projected (CPL, CPA, AOV, LTV)

---

## Scoring
- 22-25/25 = Perfect funnel selection
- 17-21/25 = Good selection, verify edge cases
- 12-16/25 = Reconsider funnel type
- Below 12 = Wrong funnel for this offer
