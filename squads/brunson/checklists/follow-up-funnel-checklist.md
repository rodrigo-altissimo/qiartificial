# Follow-Up Funnel Architecture Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets, Chapter 6 + Traffic Secrets - Follow-Up Funnels
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When designing the complete follow-up system for all funnel exits

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Primary funnel live and generating leads/sales
- [ ] Email service provider configured with tagging/segmentation
- [ ] Soap Opera Sequence written and loaded
- [ ] Seinfeld emails planned or in production
- [ ] Understanding of customer lifecycle stages

---

## STEP 1: Follow-Up Funnel Map

### 1.1 Entry Point Segmentation
- [ ] Opt-in leads tagged by source (which funnel/lead magnet)
- [ ] Buyers tagged by product purchased
- [ ] Non-buyers tagged separately from buyers
- [ ] Webinar registrants tracked (attended vs. no-show)
- [ ] Cart abandoners identified and tagged

### 1.2 Sequence Mapping
- [ ] Every funnel exit point has a follow-up sequence
- [ ] Opt-in but didn't buy -> Soap Opera -> Seinfeld
- [ ] Bought frontend -> Backend offer sequence
- [ ] Declined OTO -> Alternative offer sequence
- [ ] Webinar no-show -> Replay + urgency sequence
- [ ] Cart abandoner -> Recovery sequence

---

## STEP 2: Core Follow-Up Sequences

### 2.1 New Lead Sequence
- [ ] Soap Opera Sequence (5 emails over 5 days)
- [ ] Transitions to Seinfeld daily emails after Day 5
- [ ] First offer presented by email 5
- [ ] If no purchase by Day 7, second offer angle sent
- [ ] If no purchase by Day 14, different offer presented

### 2.2 New Buyer Sequence
- [ ] Immediate: delivery and welcome email
- [ ] Day 1: Onboarding and quick-start guide
- [ ] Day 3: Check-in and engagement prompt
- [ ] Day 7: Upsell/cross-sell introduction
- [ ] Day 14: Backend offer or higher-tier invitation
- [ ] Day 30: Testimonial request

### 2.3 Re-Engagement Sequence
- [ ] Triggered after 30-60 days of no opens
- [ ] Subject lines dramatically different ("Is this goodbye?")
- [ ] 3-email sequence: re-engage, last chance, removal warning
- [ ] Non-responders removed or moved to cold segment
- [ ] Responders returned to active sequence

---

## STEP 3: Multi-Channel Follow-Up

### 3.1 Email Layer
- [ ] Primary follow-up channel configured and automated
- [ ] Segmentation working correctly (tested with test contacts)
- [ ] Deliverability optimized (authenticated domain, clean list)
- [ ] Personalization tokens working (first name, purchase history)
- [ ] Frequency: daily for new leads, 3-5x/week for ongoing

### 3.2 Retargeting Ads Layer
- [ ] Facebook/Instagram retargeting for non-buyers
- [ ] Google Display retargeting for website visitors
- [ ] YouTube retargeting for video viewers
- [ ] Ad messaging matches follow-up sequence stage
- [ ] Buyers excluded from non-buyer retargeting

### 3.3 Additional Channels (If Applicable)
- [ ] SMS follow-up for high-ticket prospects
- [ ] Direct mail for premium segments
- [ ] Messenger/chatbot for engagement
- [ ] Push notifications for app users
- [ ] Phone call follow-up for application funnel leads

---

## STEP 4: Ascension Follow-Up

### 4.1 Value Ladder Progression
- [ ] Each product purchase triggers next-tier offer sequence
- [ ] Timing: backend offers presented 7-14 days after purchase
- [ ] Ascension offers match customer engagement level
- [ ] Success with current product increases receptivity to upgrade
- [ ] Follow-up educates on value of next tier

### 4.2 Continuity Follow-Up
- [ ] Subscription/membership retention emails automated
- [ ] Weekly engagement emails highlighting new content
- [ ] Anniversary/milestone celebration emails
- [ ] Cancellation prevention sequence (triggered by cancellation intent)
- [ ] Win-back sequence for cancelled members

---

## STEP 5: Follow-Up Economics

### 5.1 Revenue Attribution
- [ ] Revenue from follow-up tracked separately from frontend
- [ ] Email revenue per subscriber calculated monthly
- [ ] Follow-up funnel ROAS measured
- [ ] Most profitable sequences identified
- [ ] Underperforming sequences flagged for improvement

### 5.2 Optimization
- [ ] Best-converting emails identified and analyzed
- [ ] Subject line testing ongoing for all sequences
- [ ] Timing optimization (day of week, time of day)
- [ ] Sequence length tested (shorter vs. longer)
- [ ] New offers A/B tested within sequences

---

## Validation
- [ ] Complete follow-up map drawn showing all sequences
- [ ] Every lead and buyer has a "next email" at all times
- [ ] No dead-ends: every sequence feeds into another
- [ ] All automations tested with real test contacts
- [ ] Revenue from follow-up exceeds revenue from frontend

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Comprehensive follow-up funnel ecosystem
- 24-29/34 = Strong system, fill gaps in sequences or channels
- 18-23/34 = Follow-up system incomplete, major sequences missing
- Below 18 = Critical: revenue left on table, build follow-up ASAP
