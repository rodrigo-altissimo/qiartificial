# Irresistible Offer Checklist

> Agent: brunson-offers
> Source: DotCom Secrets & Expert Secrets - Offer Creation & Stack Methodology
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When creating or auditing an offer to ensure it is truly irresistible

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Dream customer avatar defined with specific desires and pains
- [ ] Core transformation the offer delivers is clear
- [ ] Competitive landscape researched (what others charge and offer)
- [ ] Price point range determined based on Value Ladder position

---

## STEP 1: The Core Offer

### 1.1 Transformation Focus
- [ ] Offer is framed around the RESULT, not the process
- [ ] "You will get [outcome]" not "You will learn [topic]"
- [ ] Transformation is specific and measurable
- [ ] Timeline for transformation is stated or implied

### 1.2 New Opportunity Positioning
- [ ] Offer is positioned as a NEW opportunity (not an improvement)
- [ ] Buyer feels they are getting a new vehicle, not fixing an old one
- [ ] "This is different because..." is clearly articulated
- [ ] Excitement of something new vs fatigue of another attempt

### 1.3 Uniqueness
- [ ] Offer has a unique mechanism or method name
- [ ] Cannot be directly compared to commodity competitors
- [ ] Proprietary framework, system, or approach is named
- [ ] Buyer cannot get this exact thing anywhere else

---

## STEP 2: Value Stack Construction

### 2.1 Core Component
- [ ] Main deliverable clearly defined
- [ ] Assigned a standalone dollar value
- [ ] Value justified (what would this cost to get elsewhere?)
- [ ] Core component alone worth more than the asking price

### 2.2 Bonuses (3-5 recommended)
- [ ] Each bonus has a clear name and description
- [ ] Each bonus has an assigned dollar value
- [ ] Bonuses solve related but different problems
- [ ] At least one "fast action" or speed bonus
- [ ] At least one bonus that addresses a common objection

### 2.3 Total Value Stack
- [ ] All components listed with individual values
- [ ] Total value calculated (should be 5-10x the asking price)
- [ ] Stack presented visually (ascending value)
- [ ] Price anchoring makes the actual price feel small

---

## STEP 3: Risk Reversal

### 3.1 Guarantee
- [ ] Guarantee is bold and specific
- [ ] Duration stated (30-day, 60-day, or "try it and see")
- [ ] Guarantee removes the #1 buying objection
- [ ] Buyer feels they have MORE risk in NOT buying

### 3.2 Guarantee Types (pick one or combine)
- [ ] Money-back guarantee (standard risk reversal)
- [ ] Results guarantee (if you do X and don't get Y...)
- [ ] "Better than money back" guarantee (keep bonuses even if you refund)
- [ ] Conditional guarantee (complete the program, if no results, refund)

---

## STEP 4: Urgency & Scarcity

### 4.1 Scarcity Element
- [ ] Limited quantity OR limited time stated
- [ ] Scarcity is REAL and justified (not fake countdown)
- [ ] Reason for scarcity explained (limited spots, personal bandwidth)
- [ ] Consequence of missing out is clear

### 4.2 Urgency Element
- [ ] Deadline is specific and enforced
- [ ] Price increase after deadline or bonuses removed
- [ ] "Act now because..." reason is compelling
- [ ] Urgency creates action without feeling manipulative

---

## STEP 5: Offer Clarity Test

### 5.1 The "No Brainer" Test
- [ ] If you described this offer to a stranger, would they say "that's a no brainer"?
- [ ] Value-to-price ratio is obviously in the buyer's favor
- [ ] Offer feels generous, not gimmicky
- [ ] Buyer would feel foolish saying no

### 5.2 The Objection Test
- [ ] Top 5 objections listed and addressed within the offer
- [ ] "I can't afford it" --> payment plan or ROI framing
- [ ] "I don't have time" --> speed/shortcut bonus
- [ ] "Will it work for me?" --> guarantee and case studies
- [ ] "I've tried before" --> new opportunity positioning

---

## Validation
- [ ] Offer presented to 5 people in the target market for feedback
- [ ] At least 3 out of 5 said they would seriously consider buying
- [ ] Value stack math checks out (total value > 5x price)
- [ ] Guarantee is clearly stated with no confusing terms
- [ ] Offer can be explained in under 60 seconds

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Truly irresistible offer, launch immediately
- 24-29/34 = Strong offer, strengthen weak elements
- 18-23/34 = Good foundation but missing key components
- Below 18 = Offer needs significant redesign
