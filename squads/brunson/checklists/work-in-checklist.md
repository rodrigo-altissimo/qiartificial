# Work Your Way In Campaign Checklist

> Source: Traffic Secrets, Chapters 5-7 - Working Your Way In
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When executing organic/relationship-based traffic campaigns with Dream 100

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Dream 100 list built with contact details
- [ ] Relationship building phase started (minimum 2 weeks of engagement)
- [ ] Content portfolio ready (samples, guest content, value pieces)
- [ ] Clear offer/funnel ready to receive traffic

---

## STEP 1: Relationship Foundation

### 1.1 Pre-Outreach Engagement
- [ ] Following all targets on primary platform for 30+ days
- [ ] Commented meaningfully on 10+ of their posts
- [ ] Shared their content with genuine endorsement
- [ ] Purchased at least 1 product from top 20 targets
- [ ] Left a genuine review or testimonial for their product

### 1.2 Value-First Contact
- [ ] First outreach provides value (not a pitch)
- [ ] Personalized message referencing specific content they made
- [ ] Offered something useful: feedback, introduction, resource
- [ ] Message is concise (under 100 words)
- [ ] No "pick your brain" or "I'd love to connect" generic asks

---

## STEP 2: Guest Content Strategy

### 2.1 Podcast Guesting
- [ ] Identified top 20 podcasts from Dream 100 list
- [ ] One-sheet or media kit prepared
- [ ] 3-5 unique topic angles prepared for pitching
- [ ] Sample audio or previous interview available
- [ ] Follow-up system for podcast pitch responses

### 2.2 Guest Blog/Article Writing
- [ ] Identified 10 blogs/publications that accept guest content
- [ ] Sample articles written in their style and format
- [ ] Pitches customized to each publication's audience
- [ ] Bio includes CTA directing to your funnel
- [ ] Content provides genuine value (not thinly veiled promotion)

### 2.3 Collaboration Content
- [ ] Joint webinar or live-stream proposed to 5+ targets
- [ ] Interview series created featuring Dream 100 members
- [ ] Summit or bundle collaboration organized
- [ ] Co-created content (ebook, guide, tool) proposed
- [ ] Cross-promotion agreement drafted for collaborations

---

## STEP 3: Platform-Specific Tactics

### 3.1 Instagram/Social Work-In
- [ ] Engaging in Stories and Reels of Dream 100 targets
- [ ] DM conversations started (value-based, not pitch-based)
- [ ] Tagged in relevant content (authentically)
- [ ] Collaborated on joint posts or Lives
- [ ] IG-specific CTA driving to funnel link in bio

### 3.2 YouTube Work-In
- [ ] Meaningful comments on target's videos (first 1 hour)
- [ ] Response videos created referencing their content
- [ ] Collaboration videos proposed (complementary topics)
- [ ] YouTube description includes link to your funnel
- [ ] Channel subscribers growing from collaboration traffic

### 3.3 Community Work-In
- [ ] Active helpful member in Dream 100 Facebook groups
- [ ] Answering questions with genuine expertise (no pitching)
- [ ] Building reputation as go-to expert in the community
- [ ] Group owners recognize and value your contributions
- [ ] Permission-based mentions of your resources when relevant

---

## STEP 4: Follow-Up and Conversion

### 4.1 Relationship Progression
- [ ] Track all outreach in CRM/spreadsheet
- [ ] Follow up within 7 days if no response
- [ ] Maximum 3 follow-ups before moving on
- [ ] Celebrate and promote collaborations publicly
- [ ] Maintain relationship after initial collaboration

### 4.2 Traffic Conversion
- [ ] Every guest appearance includes CTA to specific funnel
- [ ] Custom landing page for each major collaboration
- [ ] Attribution tracking in place (UTM parameters)
- [ ] Conversion rate from earned traffic measured
- [ ] Best-performing collaborations repeated or expanded

---

## Validation
- [ ] At least 5 Work-In opportunities executed this month
- [ ] Traffic from earned sources measurable in analytics
- [ ] Relationships progressing (cold -> warm -> active)
- [ ] Email list growing from organic/earned efforts
- [ ] Work-In producing lower CAC than paid ads

---

## Scoring
- 28-32/32 = Expert-level organic traffic generation
- 22-27/32 = Strong effort, increase outreach volume
- 16-21/32 = Good start, need more consistency
- Below 16 = Relationship foundation needs more investment
