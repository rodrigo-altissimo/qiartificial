# Challenge Funnel Setup Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets + Expert Secrets - Challenge Funnel Framework
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building or evaluating a challenge funnel (5-30 day guided challenge)

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Core offer ready to present at the end of the challenge
- [ ] Topic expertise sufficient to deliver daily content
- [ ] Community platform selected for challenge interaction
- [ ] Challenge duration decided (5, 7, 14, 21, or 30 days)

---

## STEP 1: Challenge Design

### 1.1 Challenge Promise
- [ ] One clear, specific outcome promised by challenge end
- [ ] Promise is achievable within the challenge duration
- [ ] Promise addresses a burning desire of the dream customer
- [ ] Promise is compelling enough to register for
- [ ] Challenge name reflects the promise (action + result)

### 1.2 Challenge Structure
- [ ] Daily assignments are clear and completable (30-60 min each)
- [ ] Each day builds on the previous day (progressive)
- [ ] Difficulty escalates gradually (not overwhelming on Day 1)
- [ ] Content delivered in consistent format (video, text, audio)
- [ ] Each day has one clear action item to complete

### 1.3 Challenge Arc
- [ ] Day 1-2: Foundation and quick win (build momentum)
- [ ] Day 3-5: Core skill building (the substance)
- [ ] Day 6-7: Implementation and results (seeing the transformation)
- [ ] Arc creates emotional journey: excitement -> effort -> achievement
- [ ] Final day sets up the offer naturally

---

## STEP 2: Registration Funnel

### 2.1 Challenge Registration Page
- [ ] Headline: compelling promise of the challenge
- [ ] Clear dates and daily time commitment
- [ ] What they'll achieve by the end
- [ ] Social proof (past participants, testimonials)
- [ ] Price: free or low ticket ($1-$47)
- [ ] Registration form: name + email (+ payment if paid)

### 2.2 Challenge Indoctrination
- [ ] Welcome email with challenge details and expectations
- [ ] Pre-challenge preparation task (quick win before Day 1)
- [ ] Community group invitation and onboarding
- [ ] Calendar reminders for each challenge day
- [ ] Excitement and anticipation building before start

---

## STEP 3: Daily Challenge Delivery

### 3.1 Daily Content
- [ ] Each day has: teaching (10-20 min) + assignment (action)
- [ ] Content delivered at consistent time each day
- [ ] Videos or live training as primary format
- [ ] Supplementary resources: worksheets, templates, checklists
- [ ] Recap email summarizing the day's lesson and task

### 3.2 Daily Engagement
- [ ] Community post for each day's assignment
- [ ] Participants share their work/progress in community
- [ ] Facilitator provides feedback or acknowledgment
- [ ] Peer accountability encouraged (accountability partners)
- [ ] Daily engagement prompt to keep momentum

### 3.3 Live Elements
- [ ] At least 2 live Q&A sessions during the challenge
- [ ] Live sessions address common stuck points
- [ ] Live sessions build rapport and trust
- [ ] Recordings available for those who miss live
- [ ] Live finale or celebration event on final day

---

## STEP 4: Challenge-to-Offer Transition

### 4.1 Bridge to Offer
- [ ] Challenge demonstrates the WHAT and WHY (not full HOW)
- [ ] Participants realize they need more support/tools to continue
- [ ] Offer presented as "the next step" on final day
- [ ] Offer extends or accelerates what the challenge started
- [ ] Transition feels like a natural progression, not a sales pitch

### 4.2 Offer Presentation
- [ ] Stack Slide or offer presentation prepared for finale
- [ ] Challenge-specific testimonials featured (past challenge buyers)
- [ ] Fast-action bonus for challenge participants who buy immediately
- [ ] Deadline: offer available for limited time after challenge ends
- [ ] Follow-up emails for challenge participants who don't buy

### 4.3 Post-Challenge Sequence
- [ ] Day 1 after: Recap email + offer reminder
- [ ] Day 2: Testimonial from challenge buyer who got results
- [ ] Day 3: FAQ and objection handling
- [ ] Day 4: Final urgency (bonuses expire, price increases)
- [ ] Day 5: Cart close email

---

## STEP 5: Challenge Metrics

### 5.1 Engagement Metrics
- [ ] Registration rate (target: 20-40% of page visitors)
- [ ] Day 1 show-up rate (target: 60-80% of registrants)
- [ ] Daily completion rate tracked per day
- [ ] Community engagement rate (posts, comments)
- [ ] Final day attendance rate (target: 30-50% of registrants)

### 5.2 Conversion Metrics
- [ ] Offer conversion rate from challenge (target: 5-15%)
- [ ] Revenue per challenge participant calculated
- [ ] Comparison: challenge-originated buyers vs. other sources
- [ ] Challenge cost per acquisition calculated
- [ ] ROI of challenge validated

---

## STEP 6: Scalability

### 6.1 Evergreen Potential
- [ ] Challenge content recorded for automated replay
- [ ] Evergreen challenge option evaluated (ongoing enrollment)
- [ ] Community management system for concurrent challenges
- [ ] Onboarding and delivery automated where possible
- [ ] Quality maintained at scale (human elements preserved)

---

## Validation
- [ ] Challenge tested with beta group (minimum 20 participants)
- [ ] Daily content delivered on time for full duration
- [ ] Participants achieved the promised outcome (or close to it)
- [ ] Offer conversion from challenge meets target
- [ ] Participant feedback collected and positive

---

## Scoring
- 32-36/36 = Professional, high-converting challenge funnel
- 26-31/36 = Strong challenge, optimize engagement or conversion
- 20-25/36 = Challenge needs more structure or better offer bridge
- Below 20 = Challenge fundamentals need to be redesigned
