# Traffic Temperature Matching Checklist

> Source: Traffic Secrets, Chapter 11 + DotCom Secrets, Chapter 6
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When ensuring your messaging matches the traffic temperature

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Traffic sources identified and categorized
- [ ] Funnel pages built with copy
- [ ] Understanding of cold/warm/hot traffic definitions
- [ ] Ad campaigns or content strategy drafted

---

## STEP 1: Temperature Classification

### 1.1 Cold Traffic Identification
- [ ] Never heard of you or your brand before
- [ ] Came from paid ads to broad/interest audiences
- [ ] Found you through search (problem-aware, not brand-aware)
- [ ] Saw a shared post from someone else
- [ ] No prior engagement with your content

### 1.2 Warm Traffic Identification
- [ ] Follows you on social media but hasn't bought
- [ ] On your email list from a lead magnet
- [ ] Has visited your website before (retargeting audience)
- [ ] Has consumed your free content (blog, podcast, video)
- [ ] Knows who you are but hasn't committed financially

### 1.3 Hot Traffic Identification
- [ ] Has purchased from you before (existing customer)
- [ ] Active in your community or membership
- [ ] Regularly engages with your content
- [ ] Refers others to your business
- [ ] High trust and established relationship

---

## STEP 2: Cold Traffic Strategy

### 2.1 Messaging Requirements
- [ ] Lead with the PROBLEM, not your solution
- [ ] Hook addresses a pain point they feel right now
- [ ] No assumptions about prior knowledge of you
- [ ] Introduce yourself with credibility quickly (3-5 seconds)
- [ ] Offer something FREE or very low cost to start relationship

### 2.2 Funnel Matching
- [ ] Cold traffic directed to squeeze page or lead magnet
- [ ] NOT directed to high-ticket sales page (too much, too soon)
- [ ] Bridge page or content warms them before asking for money
- [ ] Follow-up sequence educates and builds trust over time
- [ ] First paid offer is low-ticket ($7-$47)

### 2.3 Content Style
- [ ] Educational and value-giving (not salesy)
- [ ] Problem-agitation-solution framework
- [ ] Stories of transformation (Epiphany Bridge)
- [ ] Clear but soft CTA (learn more, get the free guide)
- [ ] No jargon or insider language they won't understand

---

## STEP 3: Warm Traffic Strategy

### 3.1 Messaging Requirements
- [ ] Reference their existing awareness of you
- [ ] Build on content they've already consumed
- [ ] Deeper teaching with more specific solutions
- [ ] Social proof from people like them
- [ ] Direct invitation to next step (webinar, challenge, offer)

### 3.2 Funnel Matching
- [ ] Warm traffic directed to webinar or mid-ticket funnel
- [ ] Retargeting ads remind them of what they've seen
- [ ] Email sequences ascend from free to paid offers
- [ ] Challenge or event invitation for engagement boost
- [ ] Offer in $47-$497 range appropriate for warm traffic

### 3.3 Content Style
- [ ] More personal and direct than cold traffic content
- [ ] Can reference previous content or shared experiences
- [ ] Urgency and scarcity more acceptable at this stage
- [ ] Testimonials featured prominently
- [ ] Stronger CTA: register, buy, join, apply

---

## STEP 4: Hot Traffic Strategy

### 4.1 Messaging Requirements
- [ ] Treat them as insiders and VIPs
- [ ] Reference their past purchases and results
- [ ] Exclusive offers not available to cold/warm traffic
- [ ] Ascension language: "the next level for you"
- [ ] Higher-ticket asks are appropriate

### 4.2 Funnel Matching
- [ ] Hot traffic directed to backend/high-ticket offers
- [ ] Application funnel or direct call booking
- [ ] Membership upgrade or annual commitment offers
- [ ] Done-for-you or premium service offers
- [ ] $497-$25,000+ range appropriate for hot traffic

### 4.3 Content Style
- [ ] Insider/exclusive tone
- [ ] Behind-the-scenes and advanced content
- [ ] First access to new offers
- [ ] Community leadership opportunities
- [ ] Referral incentives and ambassador programs

---

## STEP 5: Temperature Transition System

### 5.1 Cold to Warm Conversion
- [ ] Lead magnet converts cold to warm (email capture)
- [ ] Follow-up sequence warms over 5-14 days
- [ ] Content consumption tracked for temperature scoring
- [ ] Retargeting shifts from cold to warm messaging
- [ ] Engagement triggers move contacts to warm segment

### 5.2 Warm to Hot Conversion
- [ ] First purchase converts warm to hot
- [ ] Onboarding experience exceeds expectations
- [ ] Community integration deepens commitment
- [ ] Backend offers presented at peak satisfaction
- [ ] Customer success celebrated and amplified

---

## Validation
- [ ] Every traffic source mapped to a temperature
- [ ] Each temperature has a matching funnel entry point
- [ ] Messaging verified as appropriate for each temperature
- [ ] No cold traffic hitting high-ticket pages directly
- [ ] Temperature-based segmentation working in email/CRM

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Expert temperature-matching across all traffic
- 24-29/34 = Good matching, verify edge cases
- 18-23/34 = Temperature mismatches need fixing
- Below 18 = Fundamental temperature strategy missing
