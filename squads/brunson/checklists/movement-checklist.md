# Movement Building Completeness Checklist

> Source: Expert Secrets, Chapters 2-3 + Traffic Secrets Community Building
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building or assessing the completeness of a movement around your brand

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Expert positioning established
- [ ] Audience of at least 500 engaged people
- [ ] New Opportunity defined
- [ ] Willingness to lead publicly and consistently

---

## STEP 1: The Charismatic Leader

### 1.1 Leader Presence
- [ ] You show up consistently (content, lives, events)
- [ ] Your face and voice are associated with the movement
- [ ] You share both wins AND struggles transparently
- [ ] You have strong opinions and are not afraid to voice them
- [ ] People follow YOU, not just your content

### 1.2 Leader Communication
- [ ] You speak with certainty about your mission
- [ ] You use inclusive language: "we," "our movement," "together"
- [ ] You tell stories that inspire and motivate
- [ ] You challenge your followers to take action
- [ ] You celebrate others' wins more than your own

---

## STEP 2: The Future-Based Cause

### 2.1 Vision Clarity
- [ ] Vision stated in one compelling sentence
- [ ] Vision is about THEIR future, not yours
- [ ] Vision is aspirational but achievable
- [ ] Vision creates emotional resonance when shared
- [ ] Vision is repeated consistently across all communications

### 2.2 Us vs. Them
- [ ] Common enemy identified (a system, belief, or approach -- NOT a person)
- [ ] "They" represent the old way, the broken system
- [ ] "We" represent the new way, the better future
- [ ] Enemy is something followers already resist
- [ ] Fighting the enemy creates bonding among followers

### 2.3 Title of Liberty
- [ ] Clear manifesto or declaration for the movement
- [ ] Followers can articulate what the movement stands for
- [ ] Movement has a rallying cry or signature phrase
- [ ] Movement can be summarized in a hashtag or tagline
- [ ] New members immediately understand what they're joining

---

## STEP 3: Identity Creation

### 3.1 New Identity
- [ ] New identity label exists for followers (Funnel Hackers, etc.)
- [ ] Identity is something people WANT to claim
- [ ] Identity creates belonging and exclusivity
- [ ] Identity is visible (t-shirts, badges, stickers, profile frames)
- [ ] Identity is used consistently in communications

### 3.2 Rituals and Customs
- [ ] Regular gathering or event (weekly live, monthly challenge)
- [ ] Shared vocabulary or inside language
- [ ] Achievement milestones celebrated publicly
- [ ] Initiation experience for new members
- [ ] Annual anchor event (conference, summit, anniversary)

---

## STEP 4: Community Infrastructure

### 4.1 Gathering Place
- [ ] Central community platform exists (group, forum, membership site)
- [ ] Community is active (posts daily, not ghost town)
- [ ] Moderation keeps the space positive and on-mission
- [ ] Members help each other (not just waiting for leader)
- [ ] New members welcomed and onboarded

### 4.2 Content Engine
- [ ] Regular content reinforces the cause and vision
- [ ] Content creates conversation and engagement
- [ ] User-generated content encouraged and amplified
- [ ] Success stories shared as movement proof points
- [ ] Content calendar aligned with movement milestones

---

## STEP 5: Movement Growth

### 5.1 Organic Growth
- [ ] Members actively recruit new members
- [ ] Referral mechanism in place (share for bonus, invite system)
- [ ] Movement visible on social media (hashtags, shares)
- [ ] Word-of-mouth generating new followers
- [ ] Movement growing without leader pushing every piece

### 5.2 Movement Monetization
- [ ] Movement supports the business (not just a cost center)
- [ ] Products and offers feel like movement advancement tools
- [ ] Buying is aligned with being a good movement member
- [ ] Premium tiers for deeper movement involvement
- [ ] Movement creates customer loyalty and retention

---

## Validation
- [ ] All 3 components present: Leader + Cause + New Opportunity
- [ ] Community active and self-sustaining
- [ ] Members can explain the movement to outsiders
- [ ] Movement survived adversity (didn't collapse at first challenge)
- [ ] Movement metrics tracked: members, engagement, growth rate

---

## Scoring
- 28-32/32 = Thriving movement in motion
- 22-27/32 = Strong foundation, amplify growth mechanisms
- 16-21/32 = Movement needs stronger cause or community
- Below 16 = Movement foundation needs to be built
