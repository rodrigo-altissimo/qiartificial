# Sales Letter Checklist

> Agent: brunson-copy
> Source: DotCom Secrets & Expert Secrets - Long-Form Copy Frameworks
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When writing or auditing a long-form sales letter

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Target avatar and dream customer profile complete
- [ ] Offer details finalized (deliverables, price, guarantee)
- [ ] Epiphany Bridge story drafted
- [ ] False belief patterns identified for audience
- [ ] Competitive positioning clear

---

## STEP 1: Pre-Head & Headline

### 1.1 Pre-Head (Pattern Interrupt)
- [ ] Pre-head calls out the specific audience
- [ ] Creates curiosity or urgency
- [ ] Under 15 words

### 1.2 Headline
- [ ] Makes a big bold promise or claim
- [ ] Speaks directly to the dream outcome
- [ ] Triggers curiosity to keep reading
- [ ] Tested against at least 3 alternative headlines

### 1.3 Sub-Headline
- [ ] Expands on the headline promise
- [ ] Adds specificity (timeframe, method, result)
- [ ] Reduces skepticism with a qualifier

---

## STEP 2: Opening & Story

### 2.1 Lead/Opening
- [ ] First paragraph hooks the reader immediately
- [ ] Establishes empathy ("I know how you feel...")
- [ ] Positions the reader's current pain or frustration
- [ ] Creates an open loop that demands reading further

### 2.2 Epiphany Bridge Story
- [ ] Shares backstory (where you were before)
- [ ] Describes the wall/struggle
- [ ] Reveals the epiphany moment
- [ ] Shows the transformation after the epiphany
- [ ] Connects your epiphany to the reader's situation

---

## STEP 3: Content & Belief Breaking

### 3.1 False Belief Destruction
- [ ] Vehicle false belief addressed and reframed
- [ ] Internal false belief addressed and reframed
- [ ] External false belief addressed and reframed
- [ ] Each reframe supported by story or proof

### 3.2 Value and Education
- [ ] Teaches a key insight or framework (giving value)
- [ ] Positions the offer as the logical next step
- [ ] Uses "if/then" logic to build inevitability
- [ ] Includes social proof or case studies

---

## STEP 4: The Offer & Close

### 4.1 Transition to Offer
- [ ] Smooth bridge from content to pitch
- [ ] "The only question is..." or similar transition
- [ ] Reader feels educated, not sold

### 4.2 Offer Presentation
- [ ] Each component of the offer listed with value
- [ ] Total value stacked visually
- [ ] Price revealed after full value stack
- [ ] Price anchored against total value

### 4.3 Guarantee
- [ ] Risk reversal clearly stated
- [ ] Guarantee is bold and specific
- [ ] Makes the decision feel safe

### 4.4 Call to Action
- [ ] CTA is clear, specific, and urgent
- [ ] Tells reader exactly what to do next
- [ ] Urgency or scarcity element present
- [ ] CTA repeated at least twice in the close

---

## STEP 5: Post-Script & Reinforcement

### 5.1 P.S. Sections
- [ ] P.S. restates the main benefit
- [ ] P.P.S. adds urgency or bonus
- [ ] Final P.S. addresses the "fence sitter" objection

---

## Validation
- [ ] Letter reads naturally from top to bottom (no jarring transitions)
- [ ] Every section earns the right to the next section
- [ ] Emotional arc: pain --> hope --> proof --> offer --> urgency
- [ ] Letter has been read aloud for flow and tone
- [ ] No paragraphs longer than 4 lines

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Ready to publish, strong sales letter
- 24-29/34 = Solid draft, polish specific sections
- 18-23/34 = Needs structural work on key areas
- Below 18 = Major rewrite required
