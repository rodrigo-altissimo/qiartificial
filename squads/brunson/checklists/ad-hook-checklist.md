# Ad Hook Set Completeness Checklist

> Source: Traffic Secrets, Chapters 12-14 - Ad Hooks and Creative
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When building ad creative sets for paid traffic campaigns

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Target audience clearly defined
- [ ] Offer/funnel the ad will drive to is ready
- [ ] Competitor ads analyzed (Facebook Ad Library, SpyFu, etc.)
- [ ] Budget for creative testing allocated

---

## STEP 1: Hook Angles

### 1.1 Pain-Based Hooks
- [ ] Hook 1: Addresses their #1 frustration directly
- [ ] Hook 2: "Stop doing [wrong thing] if you want [result]"
- [ ] Hook 3: Consequence hook: "If you [current behavior], you'll [bad outcome]"
- [ ] Each pain hook validated against actual customer language
- [ ] Pain hooks create urgency to solve the problem NOW

### 1.2 Desire-Based Hooks
- [ ] Hook 1: "How to [achieve dream result] in [timeframe]"
- [ ] Hook 2: Transformation hook: "[Person] went from [before] to [after]"
- [ ] Hook 3: Aspirational hook: "What if you could [dream scenario]?"
- [ ] Desire hooks paint a vivid picture of the desired outcome
- [ ] Desire hooks feel achievable (not fantasy)

### 1.3 Curiosity-Based Hooks
- [ ] Hook 1: "The #1 reason [most people fail at X] (it's not what you think)"
- [ ] Hook 2: Secret hook: "[Experts] don't want you to know this about [topic]"
- [ ] Hook 3: Counter-intuitive hook: "Why [doing less/opposite] gets [better results]"
- [ ] Curiosity hooks create an information gap
- [ ] Curiosity is resolved by clicking through (not in the ad itself)

---

## STEP 2: Creative Formats

### 2.1 Image Ads
- [ ] Minimum 3 image variations created
- [ ] Images are eye-catching and thumb-stopping
- [ ] Text on image is minimal (under 20% for Facebook)
- [ ] Brand colors or consistent visual identity
- [ ] Images tested: lifestyle, product, text-based, before/after

### 2.2 Video Ads
- [ ] Short-form: 15-second hook video
- [ ] Medium-form: 30-60 second story video
- [ ] Long-form: 2-5 minute educational/VSL-style
- [ ] First 3 seconds have a visual/verbal hook
- [ ] Captions/subtitles added (85% watch without sound)

### 2.3 Copy Variations
- [ ] Short copy: 1-2 sentences + CTA (for aware audiences)
- [ ] Medium copy: 3-5 sentences with story element
- [ ] Long copy: Full story with agitation and CTA
- [ ] Each copy length paired with different creative formats
- [ ] Copy tested with and without emojis/formatting

---

## STEP 3: Audience-Creative Matching

### 3.1 Cold Traffic Creatives
- [ ] Lead with the problem or curiosity (they don't know you)
- [ ] No assumptions about prior knowledge
- [ ] Credibility established within the ad (brief)
- [ ] CTA: learn more, get the free guide, watch the video
- [ ] Lower commitment ask matches their trust level

### 3.2 Warm Traffic Creatives
- [ ] Reference their previous interaction (visited site, watched video)
- [ ] Testimonials and social proof featured
- [ ] Stronger CTA: register, sign up, join
- [ ] Urgency elements acceptable at this temperature
- [ ] Ad reminds them of the value they've already seen

### 3.3 Hot Traffic Creatives
- [ ] Direct offer presentation (they know and trust you)
- [ ] Exclusive/VIP positioning in messaging
- [ ] Backend or upgrade offers featured
- [ ] Strong CTA: buy now, apply, upgrade
- [ ] Highest conversion expectation from this segment

---

## STEP 4: Ad Set Assembly

### 4.1 Testing Matrix
- [ ] Minimum 3 hooks x 3 creatives = 9 ad variations
- [ ] Each variation has unique tracking (UTM parameters)
- [ ] Budget spread evenly across variations for fair test
- [ ] Testing period: 3-5 days minimum per round
- [ ] Kill criteria defined (CTR below 0.5%, CPC above threshold)

### 4.2 Scaling Preparation
- [ ] Winning ads identified after test period
- [ ] Winning elements isolated: which hook? which format? which copy?
- [ ] New variations created combining winning elements
- [ ] Scaling budget ready for winners
- [ ] Creative refresh schedule planned (every 2-4 weeks)

---

## STEP 5: Compliance and Quality

### 5.1 Platform Compliance
- [ ] Ad copy complies with platform advertising policies
- [ ] No prohibited claims (guaranteed results, before/after restrictions)
- [ ] Landing page matches ad content (no bait-and-switch)
- [ ] Disclaimer text included where required
- [ ] Ad reviewed for potential rejection reasons

### 5.2 Brand Consistency
- [ ] Visual style consistent with brand guidelines
- [ ] Tone matches Attractive Character voice
- [ ] All ads drive to the correct funnel/landing page
- [ ] Contact information or brand identification present
- [ ] Ads represent the brand positively

---

## Validation
- [ ] Minimum 9 ad variations ready for testing
- [ ] Multiple hook angles represented (pain, desire, curiosity)
- [ ] Multiple formats represented (image, video, copy lengths)
- [ ] Audience-creative matching verified
- [ ] All ads reviewed for compliance before launch

---

## Scoring
- 30-34/34 = Professional ad creative set ready for testing
- 24-29/34 = Good variety, fill gaps in angles or formats
- 18-23/34 = Ad set too narrow, diversify before spending
- Below 18 = Ad creative fundamentals needed before launching
