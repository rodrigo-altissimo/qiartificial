# Expert Positioning Checklist

> Source: Expert Secrets, Chapters 1-3 - Finding Your Voice
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When establishing or validating your expert positioning in a market

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Market/niche selected
- [ ] Personal experience or results in this area
- [ ] Willingness to lead and teach
- [ ] Commitment to continuous learning in your topic

---

## STEP 1: Expert Foundation

### 1.1 Expertise Validation
- [ ] You have personal results in this topic area
- [ ] You have helped at least ONE other person get results
- [ ] You are at least 2-3 steps ahead of your target audience
- [ ] You have a unique perspective or framework
- [ ] You are actively practicing/learning (not just teaching theory)

### 1.2 Expert Type Identification
- [ ] Charismatic Leader/Expert: You ARE the brand
- [ ] Reporter/Curator: You research and compile best practices
- [ ] Reluctant Expert: Results speak, others asked you to teach
- [ ] Selected type matches your personality and strengths
- [ ] Positioning is authentic (not fabricated)

---

## STEP 2: Market Positioning

### 2.1 Category Creation
- [ ] You are NOT competing in existing "red ocean" category
- [ ] You have created or identified a sub-niche or new category
- [ ] Category name is memorable and descriptive
- [ ] You can be #1 in this category (or close to it)
- [ ] Category is small enough to dominate, large enough to profit

### 2.2 Unique Mechanism
- [ ] Your method has a unique name or framework
- [ ] It is different from the mainstream approach
- [ ] You can explain WHY your approach works differently
- [ ] The mechanism is proprietary (even if principles are universal)
- [ ] Mechanism creates curiosity and intrigue

---

## STEP 3: Authority Building

### 3.1 Credibility Elements
- [ ] Origin story crafted (how you discovered this)
- [ ] Results documented with specifics (numbers, timelines)
- [ ] Student/client results collected (minimum 3-5)
- [ ] Published content in your niche (book, course, podcast, blog)
- [ ] Speaking or teaching experience documented

### 3.2 Social Proof Stack
- [ ] Testimonials from students/clients (video preferred)
- [ ] Case studies with before/after data
- [ ] Media mentions or features (if available)
- [ ] Certifications or credentials (if relevant)
- [ ] Association with recognized figures in niche

---

## STEP 4: Communication Positioning

### 4.1 Attractive Character Elements
- [ ] Backstory defined and polished
- [ ] Character flaws acknowledged (relatability)
- [ ] Parables/teaching stories collected (minimum 5)
- [ ] Polarity established (willing to take strong positions)
- [ ] Identity defined: leader, adventurer, reporter, or reluctant hero

### 4.2 Content Voice
- [ ] Consistent tone across all platforms
- [ ] Signature phrases or terminology developed
- [ ] Teaching style identified (stories, data, demonstrations)
- [ ] Content pillars defined (3-5 core topics)
- [ ] Publishing cadence sustainable and consistent

---

## STEP 5: Platform Presence

### 5.1 Digital Footprint
- [ ] Primary platform chosen and active (1 platform mastered first)
- [ ] Profile optimized with expert positioning
- [ ] Content demonstrating expertise published regularly
- [ ] Lead magnet aligned with expert positioning
- [ ] Funnel in place to monetize expertise

### 5.2 Competitive Differentiation
- [ ] Top 5 competitors identified
- [ ] Your unique angle documented vs. each competitor
- [ ] Messaging clearly different from generic industry advice
- [ ] Positioned as THE expert for a specific sub-problem
- [ ] Can articulate why you, why now, why this approach

---

## Validation
- [ ] Elevator pitch communicates expert positioning in 30 seconds
- [ ] 5 people in your target market tested and understood your positioning
- [ ] Positioning attracts ideal customers and repels wrong ones
- [ ] You can defend your positioning under scrutiny
- [ ] Positioning is sustainable for 3-5+ years

---

## Scoring
- 32-36/36 = Rock-solid expert positioning
- 25-31/36 = Strong positioning, polish weak areas
- 18-24/36 = Positioning gaps, strengthen before scaling
- Below 18 = Foundational work needed before building audience
