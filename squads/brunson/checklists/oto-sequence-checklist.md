# OTO Sequence Optimization Checklist

> Source: DotCom Secrets - OTO Framework + Funnel Economics
> Version: 1.0
> Use: When designing or optimizing One-Time Offer sequences post-purchase

---

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Frontend offer live and converting
- [ ] OTO products/services defined
- [ ] One-click upsell technology configured
- [ ] Understanding of Average Order Value (AOV) target

---

## STEP 1: OTO Strategy

### 1.1 OTO Philosophy
- [ ] Each OTO enhances or accelerates the original purchase
- [ ] OTOs feel like a service (helping them succeed faster)
- [ ] Buyer should think "Of course I need this too"
- [ ] OTOs are NOT random unrelated products
- [ ] OTO sequence was designed before the funnel launched

### 1.2 Sequence Length
- [ ] Maximum 3 OTOs in sequence (diminishing returns after 3)
- [ ] Each OTO has a corresponding downsell
- [ ] Total sequence: OTO1 -> DS1 -> OTO2 -> DS2 -> OTO3 -> DS3
- [ ] Buyer reaches thank-you page within 6 clicks maximum
- [ ] Sequence does not feel overwhelming or "salesy"

---

## STEP 2: OTO 1 - The Complement

### 2.1 Offer Design
- [ ] Complements the frontend purchase (not duplicates it)
- [ ] Priced at 50-100% of the frontend price (or slightly higher)
- [ ] Enhances results or speeds up implementation
- [ ] Example: templates, tools, advanced training, done-for-you element
- [ ] Can be delivered instantly (digital preferred)

### 2.2 Page Design
- [ ] Video or text explaining the offer (60-90 seconds max)
- [ ] "Wait! Your order is not complete" or similar header
- [ ] One-click purchase button (no re-entering payment info)
- [ ] Value clearly stated with comparison to standalone price
- [ ] "No thanks, I'll pass" link clearly visible

### 2.3 Downsell 1
- [ ] Triggered when OTO 1 is declined
- [ ] Lower price point (30-50% of OTO 1 price)
- [ ] Stripped-down version or payment plan of OTO 1
- [ ] Different angle: "I understand, how about just [portion]?"
- [ ] Still valuable as a standalone purchase

---

## STEP 3: OTO 2 - The Expansion

### 3.1 Offer Design
- [ ] Expands on what they purchased (broader application)
- [ ] Different from OTO 1 (not just "more of the same")
- [ ] Example: membership, coaching, additional modules, license upgrade
- [ ] Priced appropriately relative to running value total
- [ ] Creates ongoing relationship or deeper commitment

### 3.2 Page Design
- [ ] Short explanation of how this accelerates their results
- [ ] Social proof: who else has added this and what happened
- [ ] One-click purchase button
- [ ] Clear value proposition in 3-5 bullet points
- [ ] "No thanks" option visible

### 3.3 Downsell 2
- [ ] Lower-commitment version of OTO 2
- [ ] Trial offer or reduced-feature version
- [ ] Payment plan option if price was the barrier
- [ ] Maintains value while reducing financial commitment

---

## STEP 4: OTO 3 - The Shortcut (Optional)

### 4.1 Offer Design
- [ ] "Done-for-you" or premium shortcut offer
- [ ] Highest price in the OTO sequence
- [ ] For buyers who want maximum speed and minimum effort
- [ ] Example: personal setup, custom implementation, 1-on-1 session
- [ ] Clear ROI: "This will save you [X hours/days]"

### 4.2 Downsell 3
- [ ] Partial done-for-you or group version
- [ ] Payment plan for premium offer
- [ ] Still meaningfully different from OTO 1 and 2

---

## STEP 5: OTO Sequence Economics

### 5.1 AOV Optimization
- [ ] AOV tracked with and without OTOs
- [ ] OTOs increase AOV by 30-100% (target)
- [ ] OTO 1 take rate above 15%
- [ ] OTO 2 take rate above 8%
- [ ] OTO 3 take rate above 5%
- [ ] Downsell recovery rate above 10% of declined OTOs

### 5.2 Refund Rate Check
- [ ] OTO refund rate not significantly higher than frontend
- [ ] OTOs delivering genuine value (not buyer's remorse)
- [ ] Customer satisfaction maintained across all purchases
- [ ] Support inquiries not spiking due to OTO confusion
- [ ] OTO products fulfillment is seamless

---

## Validation
- [ ] Complete OTO sequence tested end-to-end (accept and decline paths)
- [ ] One-click purchase working correctly (no re-enter payment)
- [ ] All products deliver correctly after purchase
- [ ] AOV improvement measured and meeting target
- [ ] Customer feedback on OTO experience is positive

---

## Scoring
- 28-32/32 = Optimized OTO sequence boosting AOV significantly
- 22-27/32 = Good OTO sequence, optimize pricing and take rates
- 16-21/32 = OTO sequence needs better offers or presentation
- Below 16 = OTO fundamentals needed, redesign sequence
