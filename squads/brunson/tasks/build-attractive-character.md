## Purpose
Build a complete Attractive Character (AC) profile — the persona that your audience connects with, trusts, and buys from. The AC is not about being perfect; it's about being relatable, polarizing, and compelling.

> "People don't buy from companies. They buy from people they feel connected to. Your Attractive Character is the bridge between you and your audience." — Russell Brunson

---

## The Framework: The Attractive Character

### The 4 Elements of an Attractive Character
```
1. BACKSTORY      → Where you came from (relatability)
2. PARABLES       → Stories you tell regularly (teaching)
3. CHARACTER FLAWS → Your imperfections (authenticity)
4. POLARITY       → Your strong opinions (magnetism)
```

### The 4 Attractive Character Identity Types
```
TYPE 1: THE LEADER
  → "Follow me, I'll show you the way"
  → Most common for gurus, coaches, experts

TYPE 2: THE ADVENTURER/CRUSADER
  → "Come with me on this journey"
  → For explorers, testers, pioneers

TYPE 3: THE REPORTER/EVANGELIST
  → "Let me share what I've discovered"
  → For researchers, curators, interviewers

TYPE 4: THE RELUCTANT HERO
  → "I didn't plan this, but here's what I found"
  → Most powerful for authenticity
```
