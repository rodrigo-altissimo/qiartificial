## Purpose
Build a complete expert positioning strategy from scratch. Transform subject matter knowledge into a recognized expert brand with a clear message, unique framework, and movement-building identity.

> "You don't need a PhD to be an expert. You need to be one chapter ahead of the people you serve, and have a framework that gets results." — Russell Brunson

---

## The Framework: Expert Positioning Architecture

Brunson's Expert Model has 5 pillars that together create an unshakeable expert position:

### The 5 Pillars of Expert Positioning
1. **The Attractive Character** — Your relatable persona
2. **The Cause** — The movement you lead
3. **The New Opportunity** — What you offer that's different
4. **The Framework** — Your unique methodology
5. **The Results** — Proof that it works

### Expert Types
- **The Reluctant Hero** — "I didn't plan this, but I found something that works"
- **The Crusader** — "I'm on a mission to change this industry"
- **The Reporter** — "I researched and found what works"
- **The Adventurer** — "I tested everything and discovered the path"

**Brunson's Rule:** "Your mess is your message. The darker the backstory, the brighter the transformation shines."

### Step 3: Identify Your Cause (Movement)
Experts don't just sell products — they lead movements.

```yaml

  what_youre_fighting_against: ""
  what_youre_fighting_for: ""
  the_enemy: ""             # Industry norm, common belief, status quo
  the_future_you_paint: ""  # Vision of what's possible
```

**Example:**
- Against: "The college industrial complex that puts people in debt"

- Enemy: "Traditional education's gatekeeping"
- Rallying Cry: "You are one funnel away from freedom"
