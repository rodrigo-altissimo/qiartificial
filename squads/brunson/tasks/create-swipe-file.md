## Purpose

Create an organized, searchable swipe file compiled from competitor funnel hacks, proven templates, and winning examples. A swipe file is NOT a collection of things to copy -- it is a curated library of PROVEN PATTERNS organized by element type so you can quickly find inspiration and frameworks when building any part of your funnel.

> "Every great copywriter, every great marketer has a swipe file. It's your most valuable business asset because it contains PROOF of what works. Not theory. Proof." -- Russell Brunson

## The Framework: The Organized Swipe System

### Swipe File Categories

```
SWIPE FILE
├── Headlines & Hooks
├── Email Subject Lines
├── Lead Magnets
├── Sales Page Structures
├── Offer Stacks
├── Upsell Pages
├── Guarantees
├── Testimonial Formats
├── Ad Creatives
├── CTAs & Buttons
├── Urgency/Scarcity Tactics
└── Story Frameworks
```

### What Makes a Great Swipe Entry

Every swipe entry should include:
1. **The Example** -- The actual text, image, or structure
2. **The Source** -- Where it came from (competitor, funnel, date)
3. **Why It Works** -- The principle or psychology behind it
4. **How to Adapt** -- Notes on how to use this pattern for your business
5. **Performance Note** -- Any data on how well it performed (if known)

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Collect Raw Swipe Material
Gather every piece of proven marketing from your research:

**From Funnel Hacks (BR_SWP_001):**
- Headlines from every page
- Email subject lines from tracked sequences
- Ad copy from documented ads
- Offer structures and bonus stacks
- Guarantee language
- CTA button text
- Testimonial formats
- Urgency mechanisms

**From Industry Research:**
- Top-performing ads in your niche (from Ad Library)
- Award-winning landing pages
- Viral social media posts in your space
- High-performing email subject lines

**From Adjacent Markets:**
- Funnels outside your niche that use transferable strategies
- Direct response classics (timeless principles)

### Step 2: Organize by Category
Sort every swipe into its appropriate category:

**Category 1: Headlines & Hooks**
```
Entry Format:
  HEADLINE: "[Exact headline text]"
  TYPE: [curiosity | benefit | social proof | contrarian | how-to | list]
  SOURCE: [Where it came from]
  WHY IT WORKS: [Psychology/principle]
  ADAPTATION: [How to adapt for your niche]
  RATING: [1-5 based on perceived effectiveness]
```

**Category 2: Email Subject Lines**
```
Entry Format:
  SUBJECT: "[Exact subject line]"
  TYPE: [curiosity | urgency | personal | question | controversial | story]
  OPEN RATE: [If known]
  SOURCE: [Competitor name, day in sequence]
  WHY IT WORKS: [Psychology]
  ADAPTATION: [Your version]
```

**Category 3: Lead Magnet Concepts**
```
Entry Format:
  TITLE: "[Lead magnet name]"
  FORMAT: [PDF | video | quiz | checklist | template | mini-course]
  HOOK: [How it's positioned]
  SOURCE: [Where found]
  WHY IT WORKS: [What makes it irresistible]
  ADAPTATION: [Your version]
```

**Category 4: Sales Page Structures**
```
Entry Format:
  PAGE: "[Page name/URL]"
  STRUCTURE: [Headline → Video → Bullets → CTA → Testimonials → ...]
  LENGTH: [Short/Medium/Long]
  KEY ELEMENTS: [What stands out]
  SOURCE: [Competitor]
  WHY IT WORKS: [Structure reasoning]
```

**Category 5: Offer Stacks**
```
Entry Format:
  OFFER: "[Product name]"
  PRICE: $[X]
  COMPONENTS:
    - Core: [Main product]
    - Bonus 1: [Name] - $[Stated value]
    - Bonus 2: [Name] - $[Stated value]
  GUARANTEE: [Type and language]
  TOTAL VALUE: $[Stated]
  WHY IT WORKS: [Offer logic]
```

**Category 6: Ad Creatives**
```
Entry Format:
  PLATFORM: [Facebook/Google/YouTube]
  FORMAT: [Image/Video/Carousel]
  HOOK: [First line or visual hook]
  COPY: [Key copy elements]
  CTA: [Call to action]
  RUN TIME: [How long ad has been active]
  WHY IT WORKS: [Creative analysis]
```

**Category 7: Guarantees**
```
Entry Format:
  TYPE: [Money-back | Conditional | Double-your-money | Results-based]
  LANGUAGE: "[Exact guarantee text]"
  DURATION: [30/60/90/365 days]
  SOURCE: [Competitor]
  WHY IT WORKS: [Risk reversal psychology]
  ADAPTATION: [Your version]
```

**Category 8: CTAs & Buttons**
```
Entry Format:
  TEXT: "[Button/CTA text]"
  CONTEXT: [Where it appears in the funnel]
  COLOR/DESIGN: [Notable design elements]
  WHY IT WORKS: [Action trigger analysis]
```

**Category 9: Urgency/Scarcity**
```
Entry Format:
  TACTIC: "[Description]"
  TYPE: [Deadline | Limited qty | Price increase | Bonus removal | Cart close]
  LANGUAGE: "[Exact text used]"
  REAL OR MANUFACTURED: [Assessment]
  WHY IT WORKS: [Urgency psychology]
```

### Step 3: Add Your Adaptations
For the top 20% of swipes (the best ones), write your adapted version:

**Adaptation Process:**
1. Identify the PRINCIPLE (not the specific words)
2. Apply the principle to YOUR audience and offer
3. Write YOUR version using YOUR voice
4. Note what you changed and why

### Step 4: Create Quick-Reference Index
Build a searchable index:

**By Use Case:**
- "I need a headline for my squeeze page" → Category 1, Type: curiosity/benefit
- "I need email subject lines for a launch" → Category 2, Type: urgency
- "I need to structure my offer stack" → Category 5

**By Emotion:**
- Curiosity → [List of swipes that trigger curiosity]
- Urgency → [List of swipes that create urgency]
- Trust → [List of swipes that build trust]
- Desire → [List of swipes that amplify desire]

### Step 5: Rate and Rank
Give each swipe entry a rating:
- 5 stars: "Use this immediately, adapt this week"
- 4 stars: "Strong pattern, use when building [specific element]"
- 3 stars: "Good reference, useful for inspiration"
- 2 stars: "Decent example, not top priority"
- 1 star: "Noted but not directly applicable"

### Step 6: Maintain and Update
Set up a process for ongoing swipe collection:

**Weekly habit:**
- Save 3-5 new swipes per week
- Screenshot ads that catch your attention
- Save emails with great subject lines
- Document any funnel that impressed you

**Monthly review:**
- Remove outdated swipes
- Update ratings based on your own testing results
- Add adaptations you've actually used and their performance
