## Purpose

Plan a complete product launch sequence that builds anticipation, creates urgency, and drives maximum sales in a concentrated window. Brunson's approach to launches combines Jeff Walker's Product Launch Formula with funnel architecture to create a systematic, repeatable event that can be executed multiple times per year with predictable results.

> "A launch isn't an event. It's a conversation that builds belief over time, culminating in an irresistible moment where the only logical action is to buy." -- Russell Brunson

## The Framework: The Launch Sequence Architecture

### Brunson's Launch Phases

```
Phase 1: PRE-PRE-LAUNCH (2-4 weeks before)
  → Build anticipation, grow the list, seed the idea

Phase 2: PRE-LAUNCH (1-2 weeks before)
  → Deliver value, break false beliefs, build desire

Phase 3: LAUNCH (3-7 days)
  → Open cart, create urgency, handle objections

Phase 4: POST-LAUNCH (1-2 weeks after)
  → Onboard buyers, nurture non-buyers, collect testimonials
```

### The Launch Content Framework

Each pre-launch content piece should follow the "Hook, Story, Offer" framework:

| Content | Hook | Story | Offer |
|---------|------|-------|-------|
| PLC 1 (Opportunity) | "Everything you know about X is wrong" | Vehicle Epiphany Bridge | "Coming soon" tease |
| PLC 2 (Transformation) | "Here's what's possible" | Internal belief story + case studies | "Would you like this?" |
| PLC 3 (Ownership) | "How to get started" | External belief story + framework | "Here's how to join" |
| Launch | "Doors are open" | Origin story + full offer stack | "Buy now" |

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Define Launch Parameters
Set the strategic framework:

**Launch Type Selection:**
- **Seed Launch** (first time, small list): Focus on direct outreach and early feedback
- **Internal Launch** (to your own list): Full sequence with email + social
- **JV Launch** (with affiliates): Coordinate partner promotions
- **Challenge Launch** (5-day challenge → offer): Build community then pitch
- **Webinar Launch** (webinar → offer): Education-based conversion
- **Hybrid** (combination of above)

**Key Dates:**
```
Pre-pre-launch starts: [Date - 4 weeks before]
Pre-launch content 1: [Date - 2 weeks before]
Pre-launch content 2: [Date - 1 week before]
Pre-launch content 3: [Date - 3 days before]
Cart Open: [Date]
Cart Close: [Date + 5-7 days]
Onboarding starts: [Date + 1 day after close]
```

**Revenue Targets:**
```
Conservative: $[X] (based on [Y]% of list converting at $[Z])
Target: $[X] (based on [Y]% conversion)
Stretch: $[X] (based on [Y]% conversion + upsells)
```

### Step 2: Design the Pre-Pre-Launch Phase
Build anticipation 2-4 weeks before:

**Actions:**
- Announce "something big is coming" on social media
- Start a waitlist/interest list
- Create curiosity-based content related to the topic
- Run polls/surveys ("Would you want to learn about X?")
- Seed testimonials from beta users (if available)
- Begin warming up email list with related content

**Waitlist Funnel:**
```
[Curiosity Ad/Post] → [Waitlist Squeeze Page] → [Thank You + Survey]
```
The survey collects data on their biggest challenges (feeds into launch content).

### Step 3: Design Pre-Launch Content Sequence

**PLC 1: "The Opportunity" (2 weeks before)**
```
GOAL: Introduce the new opportunity and create desire
FORMAT: Video (20-30 min) or written content
STRUCTURE:
  - Hook: Challenge a commonly held belief
  - Your origin story (abbreviated Epiphany Bridge)
  - Introduce the vehicle (new opportunity)
  - Case study or proof
  - Tease what's coming next
  - CTA: Comment/share/engage
```

**PLC 2: "The Transformation" (1 week before)**
```
GOAL: Break internal false belief and show what's possible
FORMAT: Video or written content + live Q&A
STRUCTURE:
  - Recap PLC 1
  - Internal belief story ("You CAN do this")
  - Framework teaching (give them a quick win)
  - Multiple case studies (diverse demographics)
  - Address common objections
  - Tease the offer
  - CTA: Share your progress/wins
```

**PLC 3: "The Ownership Experience" (3 days before)**
```
GOAL: Break external false belief and paint the vision
FORMAT: Video or written content + community engagement
STRUCTURE:
  - Recap PLC 1 + 2
  - External belief story ("Your situation won't stop you")
  - Walk through what the product/program looks like
  - Detailed FAQ
  - "Imagine your life in 90 days" visualization
  - Pre-announce launch date and bonuses
  - CTA: Get ready for launch day
```

### Step 4: Design the Launch Sequence

**Launch Day (Cart Open):**
```
Email 1 (6am): "Doors are open" + full offer details
Email 2 (12pm): FAQ + social proof
Social: Multiple posts throughout the day
Live stream/webinar: Full pitch presentation

OFFER STRUCTURE:
  Core Product: $[X]
  Bonus 1: [Fast-action bonus, first 24-48 hours only]
  Bonus 2: [Value-add bonus]
  Bonus 3: [Community/support bonus]
  Guarantee: [Risk reversal]
  Payment plan option: [If applicable]
```

**Days 2-4 (Middle of launch):**
```
Daily emails focusing on:
  - Day 2: Case study deep dive + objection handling
  - Day 3: Behind the scenes + "day in the life" of a member
  - Day 4: FAQ responses + overcoming external obstacles

Social: Daily stories, testimonials, behind-the-scenes
Retargeting: Active on all platforms
```

**Days 5-7 (Close sequence):**
```
Day 5: "48 hours left" + bonus removal warning
Day 6: "24 hours left" + scarcity amplification + testimonial stack
Day 7 (Close day):
  Email AM: "Final day" + urgency
  Email Midday: "Hours left" + last testimonials
  Email PM: "Final hours" + last chance
  Email 1hr before: "Closing in 60 minutes"
  Email at close: "Doors are closed"
```

### Step 5: Design Post-Launch Phase

**For Buyers:**
- Immediate welcome email with access details
- Onboarding sequence (days 1-7)
- Quick win challenge (first 48 hours)
- Community introduction
- First group call/training date

**For Non-Buyers:**
- "Doors are closed" confirmation
- Continue value-based nurturing
- Survey: "What held you back?"
- Offer waitlist for next opening
- No hard feelings, maintain relationship

List every asset needed:

Day-by-day action plan with specific deliverables and deadlines.
