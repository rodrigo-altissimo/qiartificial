## Value Ladder Overview

The Value Ladder is the core business architecture: a sequence of offers at increasing price points, where each tier delivers more value, builds more trust, and ascends customers toward higher-ticket solutions.

```
                                    +------------------+
                                    |   HIGH-TICKET    |  $5,000 - $100K+
                                    |  (Inner Circle)  |  1-on-1, mastermind
                                    +--------+---------+
                                             |
                                    +--------+---------+
                                    |     BACKEND      |  $500 - $5,000
                                    |  (Signature)     |  Course, coaching
                                    +--------+---------+
                                             |
                                    +--------+---------+
                                    |     MIDDLE       |  $47 - $497
                                    |  (Core Offer)    |  Digital product
                                    +--------+---------+
                                             |
                                    +--------+---------+
                                    |    FRONTEND      |  $1 - $47
                                    |  (Tripwire/SLO)  |  Low-barrier entry
                                    +--------+---------+
                                             |
                                    +--------+---------+
                                    |      FREE        |  $0
                                    |  (Lead Magnet)   |  Opt-in, value first
                                    +------------------+
```
