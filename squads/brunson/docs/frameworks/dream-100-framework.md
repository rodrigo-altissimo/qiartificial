# Dream 100 Framework

> Source: Traffic Secrets, Chapters 3-5 — Russell Brunson
> Strategic congregation infiltration for consistent traffic

---

## The Concept

"Your dream customers are already gathered somewhere. Your job is not to create an audience — it's to FIND where your audience already exists and get in front of them."

The Dream 100 is a list of the top 100 people, platforms, and places where your dream customers congregate. You then systematically infiltrate those congregations through two methods: Working In and Buying In.

---

## Building the Dream 100 List

### Step 1: Identify Congregation Types
- **People**: Influencers, podcasters, authors, YouTubers, coaches
- **Platforms**: Facebook groups, subreddits, Slack communities, forums
- **Publications**: Newsletters, blogs, magazines, podcasts
- **Events**: Conferences, workshops, meetups

### Step 2: Research (for each entry)
- Name / URL
- Audience size
- Audience overlap with your dream customer (1-10)
- Accessibility (how easy to reach, 1-10)
- Current relationship status (cold / warm / hot)

### Step 3: Prioritize
Rank by: (Audience Overlap x Audience Size x Accessibility)

---

## Two Strategies: Work In and Buy In

### Work In (Earn Your Way In)
"Provide value first, build relationships, earn access to their audience."

**Tactics (in order of commitment):**
1. Follow and engage with their content consistently
2. Share their content with thoughtful commentary
3. Send a genuine, personalized message (no pitch)
4. Send a gift or handwritten note
5. Create content featuring their ideas (with credit)
6. Interview them for your podcast or content
7. Invite them to collaborate on a project
8. Attend their events and connect in person
9. Refer business or leads to them
10. Propose a joint venture or affiliate partnership

### Buy In (Pay for Access)
"Shortcut the relationship by paying to access their audience."

**Tactics:**
1. Sponsor their podcast or content
2. Run ads targeting their audience (lookalike)
3. Pay for a guest post or email blast
4. Buy a booth at their event
5. Invest in their program to build relationship
6. Offer affiliate commissions for referrals
7. Buy ad space on their platform

## The Dream 100 Process

### Month 1: Research & Engage
- Build the complete list (100 entries)
- Start engaging with top 25 targets daily
- Begin Work In strategy (commenting, sharing)

### Month 2: Outreach
- Send personalized outreach to top 10
- Propose value-first collaborations
- Launch Buy In campaigns for quick results

### Month 3: Collaborate & Scale
- Execute collaborations
- Measure results per source
- Double down on top performers
- Replace underperformers with new targets

---

## Key Principles

1. **Give before you ask** — Lead with value, not a pitch
2. **Think long-term** — These are relationships, not transactions
3. **One platform at a time** — Master one platform before moving to the next
4. **Convert all traffic to OWNED** — Always send traffic to your list
5. **Consistency beats intensity** — Daily small actions > occasional big pushes

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Pitching too early | Spend 30 days providing value before any ask |
| Too broad (not 100 specific) | Get specific names and URLs |
| Only doing Work In (too slow) | Combine with Buy In for faster results |
| Only doing Buy In (no relationships) | Invest in long-term relationship building |
| Not tracking results | Use UTMs and track conversions per source |
