# Funnel Hacking Framework

> Source: DotCom Secrets, Chapter 3 + 74 Funnel Swipefile — Russell Brunson
> Reverse engineering what works so you don't start from scratch

---

## The Concept

"You don't need to reinvent the wheel. Someone in your market (or an adjacent market) has ALREADY figured out what works. Find them and model their success."

Funnel Hacking is NOT copying. It's understanding WHY something works and adapting those principles to YOUR business with YOUR voice and YOUR offers.

---

## The 5-Step Funnel Hacking Process

### Step 1: Identify Winners
Find funnels that are clearly working (evidence of profitability):
- Running paid ads consistently (check Facebook Ad Library)
- Large, growing audience
- Known revenue (mentioned in interviews, podcasts)
- Recommended by other marketers
- Been running for 6+ months (longevity = profitability)

### Step 2: Enter the Funnel
Go through the entire customer experience:
- Opt in to every lead magnet
- Buy the frontend offer
- Screenshot EVERY page
- Save EVERY email
- Document every upsell and downsell
- Note the timing of everything

### Step 3: Document the Architecture
Map what you found:
- Funnel type (which of the 9 core funnels?)
- Page flow (every page in sequence)
- Offer structure (main + upsells + downsells)
- Price points at each step
- Hook-Story-Offer analysis per page
- Follow-up email structure

### Step 4: Identify the Principles
Understand WHY it works:
- Why does this hook work? (psychological trigger)
- Why is this story effective? (emotional journey)
- Why is this offer irresistible? (value perception)
- What patterns appear across multiple winners?
- What's unique vs. what's universal?

### Step 5: Adapt and Build
Create YOUR version:
- Same FRAMEWORK, different content
- Your unique Attractive Character
- Your own stories and proof
- Your offers (not their exact offers)
- Your differentiators

## What to Hack

| Element | What to Look For |
|---------|------------------|
| Funnel type | Which of the 9 core funnels? |
| Traffic source | Where does their traffic come from? |
| Hook | What headline/hook are they using? |
| Story | What origin story or proof do they share? |
| Offer | How is the offer structured and priced? |
| Value Stack | What's in their stack and how do they present it? |
| Follow-up | What emails do they send and when? |
| Guarantee | What guarantee do they offer? |

## Tools for Funnel Hacking

- **Facebook Ad Library** — See all active ads from any advertiser
- **SimilarWeb** — Traffic estimates and sources
- **BuiltWith** — Technology and platform detection
- **Archive.org** — Historical versions of pages
- **SpyFu / SEMrush** — Keyword and ad data

---

## Key Principles

1. **Model, don't copy** — Use the framework, create your own content
2. **Hack multiple funnels** — Don't rely on just one source of inspiration
3. **Focus on WHY, not WHAT** — Understand principles, not just tactics
4. **Test and iterate** — Your market may respond differently
5. **Give credit** — If you're inspired by someone, acknowledge it

---

## Ethical Boundaries

**Funnel Hacking IS:**
- Studying successful funnel structures and applying principles
- Going through a competitor's customer journey to understand their approach
- Modeling proven frameworks with your unique content

**Funnel Hacking is NOT:**
- Copying someone's exact page designs or copy
- Stealing images, testimonials, or branding
- Claiming someone else's results as your own
- Duplicating a funnel word-for-word
