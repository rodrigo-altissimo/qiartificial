# Soap Opera Sequence Framework

> Source: DotCom Secrets, Chapter 7 — Russell Brunson
> The 5-email welcome sequence that creates instant connection

---

## The Concept

"Like a soap opera, each email ends with a cliffhanger that makes them NEED to open the next one."

The Soap Opera Sequence (SOS) is the first 5 emails every new subscriber receives. It builds rapport, shares your Attractive Character backstory, and makes the first offer — all while being entertaining enough that they look forward to each email.

---

## The 5 Emails

### Email 1: Set the Stage
- **Goal**: Welcome + create anticipation
- **Content**: Deliver the lead magnet + tease your backstory
- **Open loop**: "Let me tell you how I got here... I'll share more tomorrow"
- **Subject line formula**: "[First name], here's what I promised + something unexpected"

### Email 2: High Drama / Backstory
- **Goal**: Create emotional bond through vulnerability
- **Content**: Share the crisis point of your story (rock bottom)
- **Open loop**: Cliffhanger at the darkest moment ("Then something happened...")
- **Subject line formula**: Something personal / emotional hook

### Email 3: Epiphany
- **Goal**: Reveal the turning point — the new belief
- **Content**: "That's when I discovered [the vehicle/opportunity]..."
- **Open loop**: "But what I didn't expect was..." (hidden benefits)
- **Subject line formula**: "The moment everything changed" / "What I discovered"

### Email 4: Hidden Benefits
- **Goal**: Add proof and unexpected value
- **Content**: Share benefits you didn't expect from the discovery
- **Open loop**: "Tomorrow I'll share how you can do this too"
- **Subject line formula**: "What I didn't expect..." / "The surprise benefit"

### Email 5: Urgency & CTA
- **Goal**: Drive purchase / action
- **Content**: Recap the transformation + present the offer
- **Close the loop**: No open loop — this is the call to action
- **Subject line formula**: "This is for you" / "Last chance" / time-sensitive

---

## Key Principles

1. **Each email = one episode** — Tell just enough, leave them wanting more
2. **Open loops are mandatory** — Every email (except #5) ends with a cliffhanger
3. **Emotional first, logical second** — Stories create bonds, facts justify
4. **One CTA max per email** — Don't confuse them with multiple links
5. **Short paragraphs** — 1-3 sentences per paragraph, mobile-friendly

---

## Writing Tips

- Write like you're emailing a friend, not a "list"
- Use first names when possible
- Keep emails 200-400 words
- End with a P.S. (most-read part after subject line)
- Send daily (yes, daily) during the SOS

---

## After the SOS

Once the 5-email Soap Opera Sequence is complete, transition to Seinfeld Emails (daily entertainment emails that continue the relationship and promote offers casually).
