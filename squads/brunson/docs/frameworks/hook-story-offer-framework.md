# Hook-Story-Offer Framework

> Source: Expert Secrets + Traffic Secrets — Russell Brunson
> The universal structure of all successful marketing

---

## The Framework

**Every piece of marketing follows three parts: HOOK → STORY → OFFER**

This applies to EVERYTHING: ads, emails, webinars, sales pages, social media posts, podcasts, videos, and conversations.

---

## Part 1: HOOK — Capture Attention

"You have 3 seconds to stop the scroll. Your hook is everything."

### What Makes a Great Hook
- **Interrupts the pattern** — Something unexpected
- **Creates curiosity** — Opens a loop the brain wants to close
- **Targets specifically** — Speaks to ONE person, not everyone
- **Promises a benefit** — Implies value ahead

### Hook Types
1. **Curiosity Hook**: "The weird trick that..."
2. **Contrarian Hook**: "Why everything you know about X is wrong"
3. **Result Hook**: "How I got [result] in [time]"
4. **Story Hook**: "The day everything changed..."
5. **Pattern Interrupt**: "STOP. If you're a [avatar], read this."
6. **Question Hook**: "What if you could [desire] without [pain]?"
7. **Stat/Fact Hook**: "97% of [group] fail at [thing]. Here's why..."

### Hook Rules
- **Headline = Hook on a page** (most important element)
- **First 3 seconds = Hook in a video** (say something surprising)
- **Subject line = Hook in an email** (curiosity wins)
- **First sentence = Hook in a post** (pattern interrupt)

---

## Part 2: STORY — Build Connection

"Facts tell, stories sell. Stories create the emotional experience that changes beliefs."

### Story Purposes
1. **Build rapport** — "I'm like you"
2. **Create belief** — "This works because..."
3. **Raise desire** — "Imagine when..."
4. **Handle objections** — "I used to think that too..."
5. **Build authority** — "Here's what happened..."

### Story Types for Marketing
- **Origin Story** — How you discovered the solution
- **Epiphany Bridge** — The moment of realization
- **Client Success Story** — Proof it works for others
- **Failure Story** — What NOT to do (builds credibility)
- **Parable** — Everyday situation that illustrates a point
- **Kinda Like Bridge** — "It's kinda like when..."

### Story Framework (Quick)
```
1. SITUATION: Where I was (relatable to audience)
2. STRUGGLE: What went wrong / what I tried
3. SOLUTION: What I discovered
4. SUCCESS: The result I got
```

---

## Part 3: OFFER — Drive Action

"The offer is not just what you sell. It's the entire package of value."

### What Makes an Irresistible Offer
- **Clear transformation** — What they get (result, not features)
- **Value stack** — Multiple components with individual value
- **Guarantee** — Risk reversal
- **Scarcity/Urgency** — Reason to act now
- **Price anchor** — Show total value before revealing price

### Offer Presentation
1. Present each component with its own value
2. Stack them up (visually and verbally)
3. Show total value: "$X,XXX"
4. Reveal price as a fraction
5. Add guarantee to remove remaining risk
6. Add scarcity/urgency for immediate action

## Application by Channel

### Ads
- **Hook**: First 3 seconds (thumb-stopping visual or text)
- **Story**: Body copy or video middle (emotional connection)
- **Offer**: CTA and landing page link (clear next step)

### Emails
- **Hook**: Subject line + first sentence
- **Story**: Email body (entertaining and educational)
- **Offer**: CTA with link (one clear action)

### Sales Pages
- **Hook**: Headline + sub-headline
- **Story**: Origin story, testimonials, case studies
- **Offer**: Value stack, price, guarantee, CTA

### Webinars
- **Hook**: Opening promise + 3 Secrets teaser
- **Story**: Epiphany Bridge stories for each secret
- **Offer**: Stack Slide + close sequence

### Social Media Posts
- **Hook**: First line (curiosity or pattern interrupt)
- **Story**: Body of post (micro-story, 50-200 words)
- **Offer**: CTA (comment, DM, click link)

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Weak hook (no curiosity) | Test 10 hooks, use the strongest |
| No story (just selling) | Add a relatable story before the offer |
| Offer not irresistible | Stack more value, add guarantee |
| All three parts but wrong order | Always Hook FIRST, then Story, then Offer |
| Same hook for every channel | Adapt hooks to each platform's culture |

---

## The Meta-Principle

"If your marketing isn't working, it's ALWAYS one of these three: your hook isn't capturing attention, your story isn't building belief, or your offer isn't irresistible enough. Diagnose which one and fix it."
