# Funnel Hacking Framework

## Reverse Engineer, Adapt, Iterate

---

## Overview

Funnel Hacking is Russell Brunson's systematic approach to competitive intelligence and funnel optimization. Instead of guessing what might work, you study what IS already working for successful competitors, reverse engineer their funnel architecture, adapt it to your offer and audience, and then iterate to outperform the original. Brunson has said repeatedly: "You do not need to be a creative genius. You need to be a funnel hacker." This is not copying -- it is strategic modeling with your own unique offer, voice, and angle.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Model, Do Not Copy** -- Study structure and strategy, never steal creative or copy
2. **Success Leaves Clues** -- If someone is spending money on ads consistently, their funnel works
3. **Speed to Market** -- Funnel Hacking gets you to a working funnel 10x faster than building blind
4. **Test Before Inventing** -- Proven structures de-risk your launch
5. **Iterate Relentlessly** -- Your first version will not beat their tenth; plan for ongoing optimization
6. **Study the Winners** -- Only hack funnels that have proven traction (longevity + ad spend = proof)

## The Funnel Hacking Process

### Phase 1: IDENTIFY (Find Winning Funnels)

How to find funnels worth hacking:

- **Facebook Ad Library** -- Search competitors' active ads (longevity = profitability)
- **Google Ads Transparency** -- See what keywords competitors bid on
- **Similar Web / SEMrush** -- Traffic analysis of competitor sites
- **Dream 100 List** -- Your Dream 100 targets are running funnels right now
- **ClickFunnels Two Comma Club** -- Public list of million-dollar funnels
- **Funnel Hacking Live** -- Event where successful funnels are shared openly

**Proof of success indicators:**
- Ad running for 3+ months = profitable
- Multiple ad variations = actively optimizing
- Presence across multiple platforms = scaling

### Phase 2: REVERSE ENGINEER (Map Their Funnel)

Document every step of the competitor's funnel by going through it as a customer:

1. **Ad/Hook** -- What hook are they using? What is the headline/visual?
2. **Landing Page** -- Opt-in page structure, copy, design, lead magnet
3. **Thank You Page** -- What happens after opt-in? Tripwire offer?
4. **Email Sequence** -- Sign up and document every email for 30 days
5. **Sales Page** -- Copy structure, proof elements, guarantee, pricing
6. **Order Form** -- Bump offers, order form structure
7. **Upsell Sequence** -- What OTOs (One Time Offers) follow the purchase?
8. **Follow-Up** -- What emails/retargeting happen after purchase?

### Phase 3: ADAPT (Make It Yours)

Transform the structure for your specific offer:

- **Keep the structure** -- Page sequence, funnel type, general flow
- **Replace the content** -- Your hook, story, offer, copy, design
- **Adjust for your audience** -- Different pain points, language, price sensitivity
- **Add your AC** -- Your Attractive Character, your stories, your voice
- **Improve weaknesses** -- Fix gaps you identified in their funnel

### Phase 4: ITERATE (Test and Optimize)

Launch and improve continuously:

- **Week 1-2:** Launch and collect initial data
- **Week 3-4:** Optimize headlines, hooks, and ads (top of funnel)
- **Month 2:** Optimize sales page, pricing, and offer structure
- **Month 3+:** Optimize email sequences, upsells, and retention
- **Ongoing:** A/B test one element at a time, measure impact

## Step-by-Step Application

1. **Build your Dream 100 competitor list** -- Who is selling to YOUR audience?
2. **Identify 5-10 active funnels** -- Confirm they are spending on ads (proof of profitability)
3. **Go through each funnel as a customer** -- Buy if necessary; the investment is worth it
4. **Document everything** -- Screenshots, email copies, page structures, upsell flows
5. **Map the architecture** -- Draw out the funnel flow on paper or whiteboard
6. **Identify patterns** -- What do the top 3-5 funnels have in common?
7. **Build your version** -- Same structure, your offer, your voice, your stories
8. **Launch with paid traffic** -- Start small ($20-50/day) and measure
9. **Iterate weekly** -- One change at a time, measure results, keep winners

## Common Mistakes

1. **Copying instead of modeling** -- Stealing copy, design, or creative is unethical and illegal
2. **Hacking failing funnels** -- Only hack funnels with PROVEN traction (long-running ads)
3. **Not going through the funnel** -- You must experience it as a customer to understand it
4. **Only hacking one funnel** -- Study 5-10 to find patterns, not just one
5. **Skipping the adaptation** -- Using someone else's structure without your voice and offer
6. **Not iterating** -- Launching once and expecting it to work perfectly immediately
7. **Analysis paralysis** -- Studying forever without building; hack, adapt, launch, iterate
