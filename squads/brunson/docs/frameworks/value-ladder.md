# Value Ladder Framework

## Free --> Frontend --> Backend --> High-Ticket Progression

---

## Overview

The Value Ladder is Russell Brunson's core business architecture framework. It structures your entire product ecosystem into ascending tiers of value and price. Instead of trying to sell your most expensive offer to cold traffic, the Value Ladder moves customers through a logical progression -- each step delivering more value, more intimacy, and commanding a higher price. The critical insight: most of your profit comes from the top rungs, but you need the bottom rungs to build trust and qualify buyers.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Ascending Value** -- Each rung delivers more value than the last
2. **Ascending Price** -- Higher value justifies higher investment
3. **Ascending Intimacy** -- Higher tiers mean more personal access (1-to-many to 1-to-1)
4. **Progressive Qualification** -- Each purchase qualifies the customer for the next offer
5. **Non-Linear Profit Distribution** -- 70-80% of profit comes from the top two rungs
6. **Natural Ascension** -- Each product naturally creates desire for the next

---

## The Four Rungs

### Rung 1: BAIT (Lead Magnet / Low-Ticket)

- **Price Range:** $0 - $50
- **Purpose:** Convert strangers into leads and first-time buyers
- **Format:** Free + shipping books, lead magnets, tripwires, low-cost digital products
- **Delivery:** Automated, one-to-many, fully scalable
- **Conversion Target:** 20-40% of traffic
- **Profit Expectation:** Break-even or slight profit; goal is customer acquisition

### Rung 2: FRONTEND (Core Offer)

- **Price Range:** $100 - $2,000
- **Purpose:** First real profit; qualify buyers from leads
- **Format:** Online courses, software subscriptions, product bundles, workshops
- **Delivery:** Semi-automated, one-to-many with some support
- **Conversion Target:** 10-30% of bait buyers
- **Profit Expectation:** 20-30% of total business profit

### Rung 3: MIDDLE (Premium Offer)

- **Price Range:** $2,000 - $10,000
- **Purpose:** Deeper transformation through higher-touch delivery
- **Format:** Group coaching, masterminds, certifications, live events, intensives
- **Delivery:** One-to-group, high-touch
- **Conversion Target:** 5-15% of frontend buyers
- **Profit Expectation:** 30-40% of total business profit

### Rung 4: BACKEND (High-Ticket)

- **Price Range:** $10,000 - $100,000+
- **Purpose:** Maximum value through fully customized, personal delivery
- **Format:** 1-on-1 consulting, done-for-you services, implementation partnerships, private retreats
- **Delivery:** One-to-one, fully customized
- **Conversion Target:** 1-5% of middle buyers
- **Profit Expectation:** 30-50% of total business profit

---

## Step-by-Step Application

1. **Audit your current offers** -- Map every product/service to a rung
2. **Identify gaps** -- Which rungs are missing? Most businesses lack Rung 1 or Rung 4
3. **Design ascending offers** -- Each rung must logically lead to the next
4. **Build connecting funnels** -- Create automated pathways between rungs
5. **Measure ascension rates** -- Track what percentage move from each rung to the next
6. **Optimize the weakest link** -- Find the rung with the lowest ascension rate and fix it

| Rung | Offer | Price |

## Common Mistakes

1. **No bait rung** -- Trying to sell $997+ to cold traffic without building trust first
2. **No backend** -- Leaving massive revenue on the table by not offering high-ticket
3. **All products at same price** -- No ascension path; customers have nowhere to go
4. **Forcing ascension** -- Pushing upsells before value is delivered at current rung
5. **Disconnected offers** -- Products that do not naturally lead to the next rung
6. **Not segmenting by rung** -- Sending backend offers to people still at bait level

---

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| LTV (Lifetime Value) | Total revenue per customer across all rungs | 3-10x acquisition cost |
| Ascension Rate | % moving from one rung to the next | Bait-to-FE: 10-30%, FE-to-Mid: 5-15%, Mid-to-BE: 1-5% |
| Ascension Velocity | Time between rung purchases | Optimize to reduce without forcing |
| Rung Profit Margin | Profit per rung after costs | Should increase at each rung |

## Related Frameworks

- **Secret Formula** -- Defines WHO and RESULT; Value Ladder defines the journey between them
- **Nine Core Funnels** -- Each funnel type maps to a specific rung on the Value Ladder
- **Stack Slide** -- Used to present value at each rung during the sales process
- **Hook-Story-Offer** -- The communication framework used to move people between rungs
- **Opportunity Switch** -- The conceptual shift that makes customers receptive to ascending
