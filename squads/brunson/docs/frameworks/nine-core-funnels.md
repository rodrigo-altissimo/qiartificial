# Nine Core Funnels Framework

## The 9 Funnel Archetypes

---

## Overview

Russell Brunson teaches that while there are infinite variations of online sales funnels, they all map to a small number of core archetypes. Across DotCom Secrets and his broader teaching, Brunson identifies specific funnel types organized by where they sit on the Value Ladder. Each funnel type is designed for a specific price point, traffic temperature, and business objective. Choosing the WRONG funnel type for your offer is one of the most common and expensive mistakes in online marketing. This framework maps all nine archetypes so you can select the right funnel for every situation.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Match Funnel to Offer** -- Every price point and offer type has an ideal funnel architecture
2. **Front, Middle, Back** -- Funnels are categorized by where they sit on the Value Ladder
3. **Temperature Matters** -- Cold traffic needs different funnels than warm or hot traffic
4. **Simplicity Wins** -- Start with ONE funnel, master it, then add more
5. **Funnels Work Together** -- A complete business uses multiple funnel types feeding each other
6. **Test the Vehicle Before the Traffic** -- Get the funnel converting before scaling ad spend

## The Nine Funnel Archetypes

### FRONTEND FUNNELS (Bait + Low-Ticket: $0-$100)

#### 1. Free + Shipping Funnel
- **Purpose:** Acquire customers at breakeven using a physical product
- **Flow:** Ad --> Landing Page --> Order Form (pay shipping) --> OTO Upsell
- **Price Point:** Free + $7-15 shipping
- **Best For:** Books, physical products, samples, starter kits
- **Key Metric:** Does the upsell cover ad costs?

#### 2. Self-Liquidating Offer (SLO) Funnel
- **Purpose:** Generate leads AND recoup ad spend simultaneously
- **Flow:** Ad --> Squeeze Page --> Sales Page ($27-97) --> OTO Upsell
- **Price Point:** $27-$97 core, $97-297 upsell
- **Best For:** Digital courses, toolkits, templates, low-ticket offers
- **Key Metric:** Does total funnel value (core + upsells) cover CPL?

#### 3. Continuity / Membership Funnel
- **Purpose:** Create recurring monthly revenue
- **Flow:** Ad --> Free Trial Landing Page --> Membership Area --> Monthly Billing
- **Price Point:** $9-$97/month
- **Best For:** Software (SaaS), membership sites, subscription boxes, communities
- **Key Metric:** MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) and churn rate

### MIDDLE FUNNELS (Core + Premium Offers: $100-$10,000)

#### 4. Perfect Webinar Funnel
- **Purpose:** Sell $297-$2,000 offers through a 90-minute presentation
- **Flow:** Ad --> Registration Page --> Webinar --> Sales Page --> Order Form
- **Price Point:** $297-$2,000
- **Best For:** Online courses, coaching programs, software packages
- **Key Metric:** Show-up rate, offer conversion rate, earnings per registrant

#### 5. Invisible Funnel (Webinar Variant)
- **Purpose:** Remove all risk with a "pay only if you love it" model
- **Flow:** Registration --> Free Webinar/Training --> "Pay what it was worth" close
- **Price Point:** $297-$997
- **Best For:** Establishing authority, new audiences, high-trust markets
- **Key Metric:** Percentage who choose to pay after consuming free content

#### 6. Product Launch Funnel
- **Purpose:** Create massive buzz and demand before opening cart
- **Flow:** PLC Video 1 --> PLC Video 2 --> PLC Video 3 --> Cart Open --> Cart Close
- **Price Point:** $497-$2,997
- **Best For:** New product launches, seasonal offers, event-based sales
- **Key Metric:** Email list engagement, cart conversion rate, total launch revenue

### BACKEND FUNNELS (High-Ticket: $10,000+)

#### 7. High-Ticket Application Funnel
- **Purpose:** Qualify and close high-ticket clients
- **Flow:** Ad --> Case Study/VSL Page --> Application Form --> Phone Call --> Close
- **Price Point:** $3,000-$100,000+
- **Best For:** Consulting, done-for-you services, masterminds, coaching
- **Key Metric:** Application rate, call booking rate, close rate

#### 8. Summit / Virtual Event Funnel
- **Purpose:** Build authority and sell through a multi-day virtual event
- **Flow:** Speaker Promo --> Registration --> Multi-Day Event --> All-Access Pass Offer --> Backend Offer
- **Price Point:** Free entry, $97-297 all-access, $2K-25K backend
- **Best For:** Building list fast, authority positioning, partnership development
- **Key Metric:** Registrations, all-access conversions, backend sales

#### 9. Challenge Funnel
- **Purpose:** Create engagement and sell through a multi-day challenge experience
- **Flow:** Ad --> Challenge Registration --> 5-Day Challenge --> Offer Presentation --> Sales Page
- **Price Point:** Free or $47 entry, $497-$2,997 core offer
- **Best For:** Building community, demonstrating methodology, coaching programs
- **Key Metric:** Completion rate, engagement rate, offer conversion rate

## Funnel Selection Guide

| Your Situation | Recommended Funnel | Why |
|---------------|-------------------|-----|
| New business, no audience | Free + Shipping or SLO | Low risk, builds customer list |
| Have a $297-2K course to sell | Perfect Webinar | Proven conversion for this price range |
| Want recurring revenue | Continuity / Membership | Predictable monthly income |
| Selling $5K+ coaching | Application Funnel | Qualifies leads, high-touch close |
| Launching a new product | Product Launch | Creates urgency and buzz |
| Building authority fast | Summit Funnel | Leverages other experts' audiences |
| Want engaged buyers | Challenge Funnel | Action-takers become best customers |
| Establishing trust with cold traffic | Invisible Funnel | Removes all risk for the buyer |
| Selling a physical book | Free + Shipping | Classic Brunson model (DotCom Secrets launch) |

## Step-by-Step Application

1. **Identify your offer's price point** -- This determines front/middle/back category
2. **Assess your traffic temperature** -- Cold traffic needs lower-friction funnels
3. **Select ONE funnel type** -- Do not build multiple funnels simultaneously
4. **Funnel hack 3-5 examples** -- Find successful versions of your chosen funnel type
5. **Build a minimum viable funnel** -- Get it live with the essential pages only
6. **Drive test traffic** -- $500-1,000 in ads to validate conversion
7. **Optimize before scaling** -- Fix conversion issues before increasing ad spend
8. **Add the next funnel** -- Only after the first one is profitable

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Wrong funnel for the price point** -- Using a webinar to sell a $27 product (overkill)
2. **Building too many at once** -- Master one funnel before adding another
3. **No upsell sequence** -- Frontend funnels without OTOs rarely cover ad costs
4. **Skipping the application for high-ticket** -- Selling $10K+ without qualifying leads
5. **No follow-up sequence** -- Most sales happen in the follow-up, not on the first visit
6. **Over-engineering** -- Adding unnecessary complexity; start with minimum viable funnel
