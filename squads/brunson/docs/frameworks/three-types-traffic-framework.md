# Three Types of Traffic Framework

> Source: Traffic Secrets, Chapter 1 — Russell Brunson
> The fundamental classification of all traffic

---

## The Three Types

### 1. Traffic You CONTROL
"Traffic you can direct wherever you want by paying for it."

- **What it is**: Paid advertising, affiliate traffic, sponsorships
- **Examples**: Facebook ads, Google ads, YouTube ads, solo ads, podcast sponsorships
- **Advantage**: Predictable, scalable, immediate
- **Disadvantage**: Costs money, stops when you stop paying
- **Rule**: ALWAYS send controlled traffic to a page that converts it to OWNED traffic

### 2. Traffic You EARN
"Traffic you get through creating value and building relationships."

- **What it is**: Organic reach, SEO, PR, word-of-mouth, guest appearances
- **Examples**: Social media posts, blog articles, podcast interviews, viral content, press mentions
- **Advantage**: Free, builds authority, compounds over time
- **Disadvantage**: Unpredictable, slow to build, algorithm-dependent
- **Rule**: Create content that provides value AND includes a CTA to become owned traffic

### 3. Traffic You OWN
"Traffic you can reach anytime, for free, without paying anyone."

- **What it is**: Your email list, SMS list, push notification subscribers
- **Examples**: Email newsletter, SMS broadcast, Messenger bot list
- **Advantage**: Free to access, full control, highest conversion
- **Disadvantage**: Takes time to build, requires nurturing
- **Rule**: THIS IS THE GOAL. Everything else exists to build this.

---

## The Golden Rule

**"The ONLY goal of traffic you control and traffic you earn is to convert it into traffic you OWN."**

```
Traffic You Control (Paid Ads) ──→ Funnel ──→ Email List (Traffic You Own)
Traffic You Earn (Organic)    ──→ Funnel ──→ Email List (Traffic You Own)
```

If you're driving traffic anywhere that doesn't capture contact information, you're wasting money.

## Strategy by Stage

### Starting Out (No List)
- Focus on EARNING traffic through content and Dream 100
- Invest small amounts in CONTROLLED traffic to test funnels
- Build the list above all else

### Growing (Small List)
- Scale CONTROLLED traffic (paid ads) to proven funnels
- Continue EARNING through content and collaborations
- Nurture OWNED traffic with Soap Opera + Seinfeld emails

### Scaling (Large List)
- Owned traffic becomes primary revenue driver
- Controlled traffic fuels list growth
- Earned traffic builds brand and authority

---

## Key Principles

1. **Never rent when you can own** — Social media followers are rented, email subscribers are owned
2. **Platform risk is real** — Algorithms change, accounts get banned, build what you control
3. **Speed vs. cost tradeoff** — Controlled is fast but costs money, earned is free but slow
4. **All three work together** — The best businesses use all three in harmony
