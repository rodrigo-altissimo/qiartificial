# Value Ladder Framework

> Source: DotCom Secrets, Chapter 2 — Russell Brunson
> The foundation of business growth and customer ascension

---

## The Concept

"Every business needs a Value Ladder — a series of offers that increase in price AND value, moving customers from free to premium."

The Value Ladder is the backbone of every successful online business. Without it, you're leaving 90% of potential revenue on the table.

---

## The 4+ Tiers

### Tier 0: Bait / Lead Magnet (FREE)
- **Purpose**: Attract dream customers and build your list
- **Price**: Free (or free + shipping)
- **Value**: Solve ONE specific problem
- **Examples**: Free report, checklist, video training, quiz
- **Key Metric**: Opt-in rate (target >25%)

### Tier 1: Frontend Offer ($7-$97)
- **Purpose**: Convert leads to buyers, cover ad spend
- **Price**: Low barrier to entry
- **Value**: Solve the NEXT logical problem after the bait
- **Examples**: Book, mini-course, toolkit, templates
- **Key Metric**: Conversion rate (target >2%)

### Tier 2: Middle Offer ($97-$997)
- **Purpose**: Deliver the core transformation
- **Price**: Investment-level
- **Value**: Comprehensive solution to the big problem
- **Examples**: Online course, group coaching, membership, software
- **Key Metric**: Ascension rate from frontend (target >5%)

### Tier 3: Backend Offer ($997-$9,997)
- **Purpose**: Premium transformation with higher access
- **Price**: Premium
- **Value**: Done-with-you or intensive experience
- **Examples**: Mastermind, live workshop, certification, consulting
- **Key Metric**: Ascension rate from middle (target >3%)

### Tier 4: High-Ticket / Continuity ($10,000+)
- **Purpose**: Maximum value, longest relationship
- **Price**: Top-tier
- **Value**: Done-for-you or exclusive access
- **Examples**: 1-on-1 coaching, agency services, inner circle, licensing
- **Key Metric**: LTV (lifetime value)

## Ascension Principles

### The Ascending Path
```
FREE → $27 → $297 → $2,997 → $25,000
  ↑      ↑       ↑         ↑          ↑
Bait  Frontend  Core    Premium   Elite
```

### Rule 1: Value Must Exceed Price at Every Level
Each tier must deliver 10x the value of its price. This makes the next purchase feel like a no-brainer.

### Rule 2: Natural Progression
Each tier solves the problem that emerges FROM the previous tier's solution. The customer never feels "sold" — they feel guided.

### Rule 3: Never Skip Tiers
Don't try to sell high-ticket to cold traffic. Customers need to ascend through the ladder, building trust at each step.

## Building Your Value Ladder

### Step 1: Start with Your Core Offer
What do you ALREADY sell? That's likely your middle tier.

### Step 2: Create a Frontend Below It
What's a smaller, cheaper version that introduces people to your method?

### Step 3: Create a Bait Below That
What free resource naturally leads to the frontend?

### Step 4: Create a Premium Above
What's the VIP version with more access and faster results?

### Step 5: Map the Ascension Triggers
What happens (or should happen) that makes someone ready for the next level?

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Only one offer (flat, no ladder) | Create at least 3 tiers |
| Big price jumps between tiers | Add intermediate offers |
| No free entry point | Create a lead magnet |
| Backend not defined | Design premium offer even if no one's ready yet |
| Selling backend to cold traffic | Build trust through lower tiers first |

## The Dentist Analogy

"Think of your business like a dentist. The free teeth cleaning gets people in the door. The whitening is the frontend. The braces are the middle. The veneers are the backend. And the lifetime care plan is the continuity. If a dentist only offered cleanings, they'd go broke."
