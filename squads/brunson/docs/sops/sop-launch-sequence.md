# SOP: Launch Sequence

## Purpose

Execute a complete product launch sequence that builds anticipation, creates urgency, and drives maximum sales within a defined launch window. This SOP covers pre-launch content, cart open/close mechanics, and post-launch follow-up, drawing from Brunson's Product Launch Funnel and Jeff Walker's PLC (Product Launch Content) methodology as adapted by Brunson.

---

## Prerequisites

- Product or offer fully built and ready to deliver
- Email list with minimum 500 engaged subscribers
- Sales page or webinar funnel ready
- Stack slide and offer finalized
- Email platform configured for segmentation and automation
- Social media accounts active
- 4-6 week runway before desired launch date

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Phase 1: PRE-LAUNCH -- Build Anticipation (Weeks 1-3)

**Week 1: Seed the Idea**
1. Share a "behind the scenes" post about what you are working on
2. Tell a story related to WHY you created this product (Epiphany Bridge)
3. Ask your audience: "If I created [general description], would you be interested?"
4. Begin collecting early interest (waitlist, survey, replies)
5. Send 2 emails: teaser + story about the problem your product solves

**Week 2: Pre-Launch Content (PLC)**
1. **PLC Video/Content #1: The Opportunity** -- Show them what is possible
   - Teach something valuable that creates an "aha moment"
   - End with an open loop for PLC #2
2. **PLC Video/Content #2: The Transformation** -- Show them how it works
   - Share a case study or demonstrate your methodology
   - End with an open loop for PLC #3
3. **PLC Video/Content #3: The Ownership Experience** -- Show them what life looks like
   - Help them visualize having the result
   - Tease the upcoming launch with a date

**Week 3: Final Anticipation Build**
1. Share testimonials from beta users or past clients
2. Reveal the product name and visual identity
3. Announce the specific launch date and time
4. Create countdown: "3 days until doors open..."
5. Send daily short emails building excitement

### Phase 2: LAUNCH -- Cart Open (Days 1-7)

**Day 1: Cart Open**
1. Send the "Doors Are Open" email (excitement, full offer details, link)
2. Post on all social channels
3. Run paid ads to warm audience (retargeting)
4. Host a live event or webinar (Perfect Webinar format)
5. First 24 hours typically generate 30-40% of total launch revenue

**Days 2-3: Social Proof Push**
1. Share testimonials from first buyers
2. Send "case study" emails showing transformations
3. Answer FAQs in email and social content
4. Host a live Q&A session

**Days 4-5: Objection Handling**
1. Address the top 3 objections in dedicated emails
2. Share stories (Epiphany Bridges) that break remaining false beliefs
3. Offer a payment plan reminder for price-sensitive prospects
4. Highlight the guarantee prominently

**Day 6: Urgency Ramp**
1. "48 hours left" email with scarcity reminder
2. Remove or announce removal of a bonus at a specific time
3. Share final testimonials and success stories
4. Post a "last chance" social media story

**Day 7: Cart Close**
1. Send 3 emails on final day:
   - Morning: "Last day" reminder with full benefits recap
   - Afternoon: Testimonial + "hours remaining" countdown
   - Evening (1-2 hours before close): "Final call" with direct CTA
2. Live stream or post countdown on social media
3. Close the cart at the announced time (DO NOT extend)
4. Last day typically generates 30-40% of total launch revenue

### Phase 3: POST-LAUNCH (Week After Close)

**Day 1 After Close:**
1. Send "Doors are closed" email to non-buyers
2. Send "Welcome" email to buyers with onboarding instructions
3. Compile launch metrics

**Days 2-7:**
1. Onboard new customers with excellent first experience
2. Send a post-launch "what's next" email to non-buyers (value content, no pitch)
3. Analyze all launch data and document lessons learned
4. Plan the next launch or evergreen conversion for this offer

---

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Pre-launch content (3 PLC pieces) created and published
- [ ] Waitlist or early interest mechanism active
- [ ] Launch emails written for all 7 days (minimum 10 emails total)
- [ ] Social media content planned for each day of the launch
- [ ] Cart open and close dates/times announced clearly
- [ ] Paid retargeting ads configured for warm audience
- [ ] Payment plan option available
- [ ] Guarantee clearly stated in all sales materials
- [ ] Onboarding sequence ready for new buyers
- [ ] Launch debrief scheduled for the week after close

---

## Handoff

Deliver:
1. **Launch Calendar** -- Complete day-by-day plan with all activities
2. **Email Sequence** -- All pre-launch, launch, and post-launch emails
3. **Content Assets** -- PLC videos/content, social posts, ad creative
4. **Launch Metrics Report** -- Revenue, conversion rate, email performance, ad ROI
5. **Lessons Learned Document** -- What worked, what did not, what to improve next time

---

*Estimated Time: 4-6 weeks preparation, 1 week execution*
*Review Cycle: After every launch (debrief within 7 days of close)*
