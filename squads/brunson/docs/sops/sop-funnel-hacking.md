# SOP: Funnel Hacking

## Purpose

Systematically reverse engineer successful competitor funnels to extract proven structures, strategies, and patterns. Adapt these findings to create your own high-converting funnel. This is NOT copying -- it is strategic modeling of architecture and flow.

---

## Prerequisites

- Dream 100 list completed (know who your competitors and adjacent players are)
- Target funnel type identified (from Funnel Selection SOP)
- Access to Facebook Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency, SimilarWeb, or equivalent
- Budget for purchasing competitor products ($100-500 for research)
- Spreadsheet or document for recording findings

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Identify Target Funnels (1 hour)

1. Search Facebook Ad Library for 10-15 competitors running active ads
2. Filter for ads running 3+ months (longevity = profitability)
3. Note ads with high engagement (comments, shares)
4. Check Google Ads Transparency for search ad competitors
5. Ask your audience: "What other courses/products have you bought in this space?"
6. Select 5 funnels to hack in detail

### Step 2: Map the Complete Funnel (2-3 hours per funnel)

Go through each funnel as a real customer:

**Traffic Layer:**
- Screenshot every ad variation (creative, copy, CTA)
- Note the targeting clues (who are they targeting based on ad language?)
- Document the hook types used

**Landing Page:**
- Screenshot the full page (use full-page screenshot tool)
- Copy the headline, sub-headline, and bullet points
- Note the page structure (video vs. text, long vs. short, testimonials)
- Identify the lead magnet offer

**Email Sequence:**
- Subscribe with a dedicated email address
- Copy every email for 30 days
- Note: subject lines, story types, CTAs, frequency, and sequence structure
- Identify the Soap Opera Sequence vs. Seinfeld Email transition

**Sales Process:**
- Go through the sales page or webinar registration
- Attend the webinar (if applicable) and take detailed notes
- Document the offer stack, bonuses, price, and guarantee
- Screenshot the order form and note any order bumps

**Post-Purchase:**
- Buy the product if budget allows
- Document the upsell sequence (each OTO page)
- Note the customer onboarding experience
- Track follow-up emails for the next 30 days

### Step 4: Identify Patterns (1 hour)

After hacking 5 funnels:
1. What page structures do all winners share?
2. What hook types are most common?
3. What price points appear most frequently?
4. What upsell strategies do they use?
5. What email patterns are consistent?
6. What gaps or weaknesses can you spot across all of them?

### Step 5: Adapt for Your Funnel (2-3 hours)

1. Choose the funnel structure most proven for your offer type
2. Replace all content with YOUR hook, story, and offer
3. Use your Attractive Character voice (not theirs)
4. Apply your Epiphany Bridge stories
5. Build your own Stack (do not copy their bonuses)
6. Improve on weaknesses you identified in their funnels
7. Add elements from multiple hacked funnels (hybrid approach)

### Step 6: Build and Launch (Timeline varies)

1. Build your adapted funnel (follow Funnel Page Design SOP)
2. Create your ad creative (follow Hook Creation SOP)
3. Launch with test traffic (follow Traffic Campaign SOP)
4. Compare your metrics to the benchmarks from hacked funnels
5. Iterate based on data

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Minimum 5 competitor funnels fully documented
- [ ] Each funnel hacked end-to-end (ad through post-purchase)
- [ ] Funnel Hack Sheet completed for each target
- [ ] Cross-funnel patterns identified and documented
- [ ] Adapted funnel uses YOUR content, voice, and offer (not copied)
- [ ] Weaknesses identified and addressed in your version
- [ ] No copyrighted material used (structure only, not creative)

---

## Handoff

Deliver:
1. **Funnel Hack Dossier** -- Complete documentation of all 5 hacked funnels
2. **Pattern Analysis** -- Common structures, hooks, and strategies found
3. **Adaptation Plan** -- How findings translate to your funnel build
4. **Competitive Advantage Brief** -- Gaps in competitor funnels you can exploit
5. **Build Specification** -- Detailed specs for your adapted funnel
