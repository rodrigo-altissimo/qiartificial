# SOP: Challenge Funnel Creation

> Source: DotCom Secrets Ignite | Version: 1.0 | Agent: brunson-launch

---

## 1. Purpose
Design and create a challenge funnel that engages participants over 5-30 days, builds community, delivers quick wins, and converts to a paid offer.

## 2. Pre-conditions
- [ ] Core offer identified (what you sell at the end)
- [ ] Topic that can be broken into daily lessons
- [ ] Capacity to engage daily during the challenge

## 3. Procedure

### Step 1: Define Challenge Parameters (15 min)
- Duration: 5, 7, 14, 21, or 30 days
- Daily commitment: 15-30 min per day
- Format: Video + action step per day
- Price: Free or $1-$47 (low barrier)

### Step 2: Design the Transformation Arc (20 min)
- Day 1 state: Where participants start
- Final day state: Where they end up
- Each day should build on the previous
- Quick win by Day 2-3 is essential

### Step 3: Create Daily Content Plan (45 min)
For each day:
- Lesson title (hook)
- Teaching content (5-15 min video)
- Daily action step (specific, doable)
- Community prompt (engagement question)

### Step 4: Design the Community (15 min)
- Platform: Facebook Group, Circle, Discord, or Slack
- Daily engagement: Post schedule, live Q&As
- Accountability: Partners, check-ins, celebrations

### Step 5: Create Registration Funnel (20 min)
- Registration page with challenge hook
- Confirmation page with next steps
- Reminder emails leading to Day 1

### Step 6: Design the Conversion Event (30 min)
- Final day or day after: webinar or sales presentation
- Stack Slide for the paid offer
- Fast-action bonus for challenge participants
- Transition: "Now that you've done X, here's how to get Y"

### Step 7: Create Follow-Up Sequences (20 min)
- Daily lesson delivery emails
- Engagement prompts
- Post-challenge: buyers vs. non-buyers

## 4. Outputs
- [ ] Challenge structure (day-by-day plan)
- [ ] Daily lesson outlines
- [ ] Registration funnel
- [ ] Conversion event script
- [ ] Email sequences

## 5. Quality Checks
- [ ] Quick win by Day 2-3
- [ ] Daily actions are specific and achievable
- [ ] Community engagement plan is realistic
- [ ] Conversion event naturally follows from challenge
- [ ] Follow-up for non-buyers
