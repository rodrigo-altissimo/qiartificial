# Blueprint: Summit Funnel

## Overview

The Summit Funnel leverages a virtual multi-day event featuring multiple expert speakers to build your email list rapidly, establish authority through association, and sell both an all-access pass and backend offers. Summits are a Dream 100 strategy in action -- each speaker promotes the event to their audience, giving you access to audiences you could never reach on your own. A single well-executed summit can add 5,000-50,000 subscribers to your list.

## Components

### Registration Page
- Event name, dates, and "FREE to attend" prominently displayed
- Speaker lineup with headshots and talk titles
- "What you will learn" bullet points
- Social proof: speaker authority, past event stats
- Registration form: name + email
- Urgency: "Event starts [date]"

### Speaker Recruitment
- Identify 15-30 experts for interview-style talks (30-45 min each)
- Provide speakers with promo swipe copy and affiliate links
- Offer speakers: exposure, all-access pass for their audience, affiliate commission
- Each speaker promotes to their email list (primary list growth mechanism)

### Multi-Day Event
- 3-5 days, 5-8 speakers per day
- Each talk available free for 24 hours only
- Hosted on a summit platform or membership site
- Daily email with that day's speaker schedule and links
- Community engagement (chat, Q&A, discussion)

### All-Access Pass (Primary Revenue)
- Lifetime access to all recordings ($97-$297)
- Bonus: transcripts, workbooks, speaker notes
- Available for purchase from Day 1 through event end
- Promoted in every daily email and between sessions
- This is where 60-80% of summit revenue comes from

### Backend Offer (Secondary Revenue)
- Your own premium offer ($997-$5,000+)
- Presented during the event (your own talk or a dedicated session)
- Positioned as "the next step" after consuming summit content
- Can include special summit discount or bonus

## Implementation Steps

1. Define summit theme and target audience
2. Recruit 15-30 speakers (start with Dream 100 list)
3. Build the registration page
4. Create speaker promotional assets (swipe copy, graphics, affiliate links)
5. Record all interviews (pre-record recommended for first summit)
6. Set up the event platform with daily schedule
7. Build the all-access pass sales page
8. Prepare your backend offer presentation
9. Write all email sequences: pre-event, daily during event, post-event
10. Configure speaker affiliate tracking
11. Launch speaker promotions 2-3 weeks before event
12. Run paid ads to registration page
13. Execute the event over 3-5 days
14. Follow up with post-event replay window and offers

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Total Registrations | 5,000-50,000 | Depends on speaker reach |
| Speaker Promotion Rate | 60-80% | Speakers who promoted / Total speakers |
| Daily Attendance | 15-30% | Daily viewers / Registrants |
| All-Access Conversion | 3-8% | Pass buyers / Registrants |
| Backend Offer Conversion | 1-3% | Buyers / Registrants |
| Revenue Per Registrant | $3-$15 | Total revenue / Registrants |
| Cost Per Registrant (paid) | $1-$5 | Ad spend / Paid registrants |
| List Growth | 5K-50K | New subscribers added |

**Goal:** Massive list growth through speaker cross-promotion, with all-access pass revenue covering event costs and generating profit.
