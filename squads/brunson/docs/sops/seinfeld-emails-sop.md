# SOP: Seinfeld Daily Email System

> Source: DotCom Secrets, Ch. 8 | Version: 1.0 | Agent: brunson-email

---

## 1. Purpose
Set up a daily email system that entertains, educates, and sells through short personal stories tied to business lessons.

## 2. Pre-conditions
- [ ] Soap Opera Sequence completed
- [ ] Attractive Character voice defined
- [ ] Products/offers to promote

## 3. Procedure

### Step 1: Generate 30 Story Topics (30 min)
From 7 categories: Personal life, Customer wins, Industry commentary, Behind-the-scenes, Controversial opinions, Media lessons, Historical parallels.

### Step 2: Create 5 Email Templates (30 min)
Reusable structures: "I was at [place] when...", "My friend asked me...", "You won't believe...", "Everyone says X, but...", "I made a huge mistake..."

### Step 3: Write First 7 Emails (90 min)
Follow pattern: Hook → Story (2-3 paragraphs) → Lesson → CTA

### Step 4: Create Subject Line Library (20 min)
50+ formulas across curiosity, story, benefit, controversy, and question categories.

### Step 5: Set Up Daily Send Schedule (10 min)
Configure email platform for daily sends. Same time each day.

### Step 6: Create Batching System (10 min)
Write 5-7 emails at once, schedule ahead. Maintain 2-week buffer.

## 4. Outputs
- [ ] 30 topic ideas
- [ ] 5 email templates
- [ ] 7 written emails
- [ ] 50+ subject line formulas
- [ ] Daily automation configured

## 5. Quality Checks
- [ ] Each email has a personal story
- [ ] Each email ties back to a lesson
- [ ] Each email has a soft CTA
- [ ] Emails are 200-400 words
- [ ] 80% entertainment / 20% selling ratio
