# SOP: Traffic Campaign

## Purpose

Plan and launch a paid traffic campaign that drives controlled traffic to a funnel, converts it into owned traffic (email list), and generates positive ROI. This SOP follows Brunson's traffic philosophy: all traffic must become owned, and funnels must be validated before scaling.

---

## Prerequisites

- Funnel built, tested, and live (from Funnel Page Design SOP)
- Hook library prepared (from Hook Creation SOP)
- Tracking configured (pixel, UTM parameters, conversion events)
- Ad account set up and verified (Facebook, Google, or platform of choice)
- Minimum test budget: $500-1,000 for initial validation
- Email automation live (Soap Opera Sequence ready)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Define Campaign Objectives (30 min)

1. Primary goal: Lead generation (email opt-ins) or Direct sales?
2. Target cost per lead (CPL) or cost per acquisition (CPA)
3. Daily budget for testing phase: $20-50/day
4. Testing duration: minimum 7 days before judging results
5. Success criteria: What numbers make this campaign worth scaling?

### Step 2: Build Audience Targeting (1 hour)

**Cold Audiences (Top of Funnel):**
- Interest-based targeting aligned with Dream 100 list
- Lookalike audiences from existing customer list (if available)
- Competitor audience targeting (followers of Dream 100 targets)

**Warm Audiences (Retargeting):**
- Website visitors (last 30-90 days)
- Video viewers (25%, 50%, 75%, 95% watched)
- Email list segments (non-buyers)

**Hot Audiences (Existing Customers):**
- Past buyers for upsell campaigns
- Email list segments (engaged subscribers)

### Step 3: Create Ad Creative (2 hours)

Create 3-5 ad variations using different hooks:
1. Select top hooks from your Hook Library
2. Match hook style to platform:
   - Facebook/Instagram: Image or short video + copy
   - YouTube: 15-30 second pre-roll or 5-minute story ad
   - Google: Search ad headline + description
3. Each ad follows Hook-Story-Offer in miniature:
   - Hook: First line / thumbnail / opening frame
   - Story: Body copy / video narrative (abbreviated)
   - Offer: CTA directing to funnel page
4. Create mobile-first (vertical video, short copy, clear CTA)

### Step 4: Launch Testing Phase (Week 1)

1. Launch 3-5 ad variations to cold audience
2. Set budget: $20-50/day across all variations
3. Let ads run for 48-72 hours before making changes
4. Track: impressions, CTR, CPL/CPA, landing page conversion rate
5. Do NOT change anything for the first 48 hours

### Step 5: Analyze and Optimize (Week 2)

1. Kill ads with CTR below 1% (for Facebook) or below platform average
2. Kill ads with CPL above 2x your target
3. For winning ads: increase budget by 20% per day (not more)
4. Create 2-3 variations of winning hooks (iterate on what works)
5. Test new audiences with winning creative

### Step 6: Scale Winning Campaigns (Week 3+)

1. Increase daily budget on winners by 20% every 2-3 days
2. Expand to new audience segments with proven creative
3. Add retargeting campaigns for funnel visitors who did not convert
4. Launch lookalike audiences based on converter data
5. Monitor ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) daily; pause if below target for 3+ days

### Step 7: Convert All Traffic to Owned (Ongoing)

1. Verify every ad drives to a page with email capture
2. Tag all subscribers by traffic source and ad campaign
3. Ensure Soap Opera Sequence fires immediately after opt-in
4. Measure email opt-in rate per campaign
5. Calculate revenue per subscriber to determine true campaign ROI

---

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Tracking pixel installed and firing correctly
- [ ] UTM parameters configured for all ad links
- [ ] Minimum 3 ad creative variations for testing
- [ ] Cold, warm, and hot audience segments defined
- [ ] Daily budget set and approved
- [ ] Success criteria defined before launch (CPL target, ROAS target)
- [ ] Email automation triggers confirmed for new opt-ins
- [ ] All traffic directs to email capture (no leaks)
- [ ] Mobile ad rendering tested

---

## Handoff

Deliver weekly:
1. **Campaign Dashboard** -- Spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPL, CPA, ROAS
2. **Creative Performance Report** -- Which ads are winning and why
3. **Audience Performance** -- Which segments convert best
4. **Optimization Actions** -- What was changed and why
5. **Budget Recommendation** -- Scale, maintain, or reduce based on data

---

*Estimated Time: 3-4 hours setup, then 30 min/day management*
*Review Cycle: Daily during active campaigns, weekly reporting*
