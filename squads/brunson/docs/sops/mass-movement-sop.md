# SOP: Mass Movement Building

> Source: Expert Secrets, Ch. 1-5 | Version: 1.0 | Agent: brunson-movement

---

## 1. Purpose
Build a mass movement with a charismatic leader, future-based cause, and new opportunity that creates raving fans, not just customers.

## 2. Pre-conditions
- [ ] Clear expertise or unique perspective
- [ ] Willingness to lead publicly
- [ ] Authentic mission/cause

## 3. Procedure

### Step 1: Define the Vision (20 min)
- What future are you creating?
- What broken system are you fighting?
- What does the world look like when you win?
- Write: "I'm building a movement of [people] who [believe/do] so that [future]"

### Step 2: Define the Common Enemy (15 min)
Not a person — a SYSTEM, BELIEF, or STATUS QUO.
- "The enemy is [system] because [reason]"
- Why the old way keeps people stuck

### Step 3: Build the Leader Profile (30 min)
- Choose identity type: Leader / Adventurer / Reporter / Reluctant Hero
- Write Attractive Character profile (backstory, flaws, parables, polarity)
- Craft origin story (Epiphany Bridge format)

### Step 4: Frame the New Opportunity (20 min)
- Position your solution as NEW, not improved
- "Stop doing [old way]. Start doing [new way]."
- Why it's categorically different
- How it preserves status

### Step 5: Create the Belief System (20 min)
- Core belief (Big Domino)
- 3-5 supporting beliefs
- Language and terminology unique to the movement
- Manifesto (3-5 core principles)

### Step 6: Design Status Mechanics (15 min)
- How do members gain status? (levels, badges, recognition)
- What rituals create belonging?
- What achievements are celebrated?

### Step 7: Design Community Architecture (20 min)
- Platform selection
- Engagement cadence
- Events calendar
- Moderation framework

### Step 8: Plan the Launch (20 min)
- Founding member recruitment
- Launch event format
- Initial content sequence

## 4. Outputs
- [ ] Movement manifesto
- [ ] Leader profile + origin story
- [ ] New Opportunity framing
- [ ] Belief system document
- [ ] Community architecture plan
- [ ] Launch plan

## 5. Quality Checks
- [ ] Vision is inspiring and specific
- [ ] Enemy is systemic, not personal
- [ ] Leader profile is authentic
- [ ] Opportunity is NEW (not improvement)
- [ ] Community has clear engagement plan
