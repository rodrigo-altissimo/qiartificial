## Email Templates

### Template A: "I was at [place] when..."

Subject: [curiosity hook]

[2-3 sentences continuing the story]

And that's when it hit me:

[Business lesson in 1-2 sentences]

This is exactly why [bridge to offer].

If you want [result], check this out:
[CTA link]

Talk soon,
[Name]

P.S. [Reinforcement or urgency]
