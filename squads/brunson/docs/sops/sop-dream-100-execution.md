# SOP: Dream 100 Execution

## Purpose

Build and execute a Dream 100 list of people and places that already have your dream customer's attention. Systematically develop relationships, earn access to their audiences, and convert borrowed traffic into owned traffic.

---

## Prerequisites

- Completed Secret Formula (WHO and WHERE clearly defined)
- Active social media profiles on platforms where your audience congregates
- Lead magnet or squeeze page ready to capture traffic
- CRM or spreadsheet for tracking Dream 100 contacts
- Minimum 60-day commitment (this is a relationship strategy, not a quick hack)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Build the Dream 100 List (2 hours)

1. Create a spreadsheet with columns: Name, Platform, Audience Size, Relevance Score (1-10), Status
2. List 50 PEOPLE:
   - 15 podcast hosts in your niche
   - 10 YouTube creators your audience follows
   - 10 newsletter/blog writers
   - 10 social media influencers
   - 5 authors or speakers
3. List 50 PLACES:
   - 15 Facebook groups or online communities
   - 10 subreddits or forums
   - 10 podcasts (the show itself, not just the host)
   - 10 newsletters or publications
   - 5 events or conferences
4. Score each target's relevance (1-10) based on audience overlap
5. Rank by relevance and prioritize top 20 for immediate action

### Step 2: Research and Study (2 weeks)

For your top 20 targets:
1. Follow them on all their active platforms
2. Subscribe to their newsletters and podcasts
3. Consume their content daily for 14 days
4. Note: tone, values, content gaps, collaboration patterns, audience pain points
5. Identify how you can uniquely add value to THEIR audience

### Step 3: Begin Engagement (Weeks 3-6)

1. Comment on their content 3-5 times per week (substantive, not generic)
2. Share their content with genuine commentary on your platforms
3. Tag them in relevant posts that add value (not self-promotion)
4. Respond to their questions or polls
5. Engage with their audience members in comments
6. Track all engagement in your spreadsheet

### Step 4: First Contact (Weeks 5-8)

1. Send a personalized DM or email (NOT a pitch -- pure value)
2. Reference specific content of theirs you found valuable
3. Offer something: introduction, resource, insight, collaboration idea
4. Keep it short, specific, and about THEM
5. If no response after 7 days, continue engaging publicly; try again in 2 weeks

### Step 5: Propose Collaboration (Weeks 8-12)

Once a relationship is established:
1. Suggest a specific, mutually beneficial collaboration:
   - Guest on their podcast
   - Co-host a webinar or live stream
   - Write a guest article
   - Joint venture or affiliate promotion
   - Feature them in your content (interview)
2. Make it easy for them to say yes (handle all logistics)
3. Focus on what is in it for THEIR audience

### Step 6: Convert Traffic to Owned (Ongoing)

For every collaboration:
1. Create a custom landing page or lead magnet for their audience
2. Track traffic and opt-ins from each Dream 100 source
3. Tag subscribers by source for segmentation
4. Follow up with a Soap Opera Sequence tailored to that audience
5. Measure cost per lead equivalent for each Dream 100 source

---

## Quality Checks

- [ ] 100 targets listed (50 people + 50 places)
- [ ] Top 20 targets researched for minimum 2 weeks before outreach
- [ ] Engagement logged consistently (3-5 touchpoints per target per week)
- [ ] First contacts are value-first, not pitch-first
- [ ] Custom landing pages created for each collaboration
- [ ] Opt-in tracking configured per source
- [ ] Spreadsheet updated weekly with status for each target

---

## Handoff

Deliver monthly:
1. **Dream 100 Tracker** -- Updated spreadsheet with all contacts and statuses
2. **Collaboration Calendar** -- Upcoming and completed collaborations
3. **Traffic Report** -- Opt-ins and leads generated per Dream 100 source
4. **Relationship Pipeline** -- Who is in engagement, first contact, proposal, or active collaboration stage
5. **Next Month Focus** -- Top 5 targets to prioritize
