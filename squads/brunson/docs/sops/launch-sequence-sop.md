# SOP: Product Launch Sequence

> Source: DotCom Secrets + Expert Secrets | Version: 1.0 | Agent: brunson-launch

---

## 1. Purpose
Plan and execute a product launch using a multi-phase campaign that builds anticipation, delivers value, and converts during a defined launch window.

## 2. Pre-conditions
- [ ] Product/offer ready or near-ready
- [ ] Email list with 500+ subscribers
- [ ] Launch date set (4-6 weeks out)
- [ ] Content creation capacity

## 3. Procedure

### Step 1: Set Launch Goals (15 min)
- Revenue target: $___
- Units to sell: ___
- Conversion rate needed: ___%
- List size needed at launch: ___

### Step 2: Create Launch Timeline (20 min)
- Week 1-3: Pre-launch (content seeding)
- Week 4: Build-up (daily engagement)
- Week 5: Launch (3-7 day cart open)
- Week 6: Post-launch (onboarding + analysis)

### Step 3: Create Pre-Launch Content (60 min)
3-4 value pieces that build toward the offer:
- PLC 1: "The Opportunity" (why now)
- PLC 2: "The Transformation" (case studies)
- PLC 3: "The Experience" (behind the scenes)
- PLC 4: (Optional) Q&A / objection handling

### Step 4: Write Email Sequences (90 min)
- Pre-launch emails (awareness + anticipation)
- Build-up emails (urgency + registration)
- Cart-open emails (selling + proof)
- Cart-close emails (urgency + last chance)
- Post-launch emails (onboarding or waitlist)

### Step 5: Plan the Launch Event (30 min)
- Webinar, challenge, or live event format
- Registration page
- Reminder sequence
- Event script/content

### Step 6: Prepare Close Sequence (20 min)
- 48-hour countdown emails
- Social proof posts
- Fast-action bonus removal
- Final cart-close email

### Step 7: Plan Post-Launch (15 min)
- Buyer onboarding
- Non-buyer follow-up (waitlist)
- Launch debrief and analysis

## 4. Outputs
- [ ] Launch timeline
- [ ] Pre-launch content (3-4 pieces)
- [ ] Email sequences (20-30 emails total)
- [ ] Launch event materials
- [ ] Close sequence
- [ ] Post-launch plan

## 5. Quality Checks
- [ ] Timeline is realistic
- [ ] Pre-launch content delivers genuine value
- [ ] Cart has a real close date
- [ ] 50-80% of revenue comes from close sequence
- [ ] Buyer onboarding is planned
