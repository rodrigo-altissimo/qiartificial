# SOP: Perfect Webinar Creation

## Purpose

Build a complete Perfect Webinar presentation that follows Brunson's proven structure: Introduction, Three Secrets (belief-breaking), and Stack/Close. This SOP covers scripting, slide creation, and rehearsal to deliver a webinar that converts at 5-15%.

---

## Prerequisites

- Offer defined with price point ($297-$2,000 typically)
- Stack slide components identified (core offer + 4-6 bonuses with values)
- Epiphany Bridge origin story written
- Three false beliefs identified (Vehicle, Internal, External)
- Three Epiphany Bridge stories prepared (one per false belief)
- Webinar platform configured (Zoom, WebinarJam, or similar)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Write the Big Domino Statement (30 min)

1. Complete this template: "If I can make them believe that [new opportunity] is the key to [what they desire most], and is only achievable through [my specific vehicle], then all other objections become irrelevant."
2. Validate: Is this ONE belief powerful enough to make all objections fall?
3. Write it on a card and keep it visible during the entire scripting process

### Step 2: Script the Introduction (1 hour)

1. **Title Slide:** "How to [desired result] without [biggest fear]"
2. **Rapport:** Use Blair Warren's 5 elements (justify failures, calm fears, throw rocks, confirm suspicions, encourage dreams)
3. **Ruler #1:** "Whether you are a complete beginner or already advanced..."
4. **Ruler #2:** Introduce the Big Domino (new opportunity) for the first time
5. **Qualification:** Share your external result + internal transformation
6. **Origin Story:** Tell your Epiphany Bridge (8 steps) -- first attempt to knock over the Big Domino
7. **Comparison:** Old way vs. your new way (why yours is easier/better/faster)
8. **Proof:** Case study or testimonial showing it works for others

### Step 3: Script the Three Secrets (2-3 hours)

For each Secret:
1. **Curiosity Title:** "Secret #X: [intriguing name]"
2. **False Belief Statement:** Name the specific belief you are breaking
3. **Epiphany Bridge Story:** Tell the story that breaks this belief (8 steps)
4. **Teaching Point:** Teach the concept that emerges from the story
5. **Transition:** "But there is something else..." (bridge to next Secret)

**Secret 1 (Vehicle):** Break the belief that this type of opportunity does not work
**Secret 2 (Internal):** Break the belief that THEY cannot do this
**Secret 3 (External):** Break the belief that outside factors will prevent success

### Step 4: Build the Stack Slide and Close (1 hour)

1. List all offer components with individual values
2. Build the Stack Slide visual (adding one component at a time)
3. Calculate total value vs. actual price (must be 10x+ gap)
4. Write the transition: "Let me show you everything you are getting..."
5. Script the price reveal moment
6. Write the guarantee (specific, bold, risk-reversing)
7. Add urgency element (limited bonuses, deadline, price increase)
8. Write the CTA script: exactly what to do and what happens next
9. Prepare 5-10 FAQ items for the closing Q&A section

### Step 5: Create Slides (2-3 hours)

1. Create slides following the script (one key point per slide)
2. Keep slides visual -- minimal text, maximum impact
3. Use the Stack Slide template for the close
4. Add proof elements (screenshots, testimonials, numbers) throughout
5. Total slides: 60-120 depending on content density

### Step 6: Rehearse (2-3 sessions)

1. Run through the entire presentation with a timer (target: 60-90 minutes)
2. Record yourself and review for energy, pacing, and clarity
3. Practice the transitions between sections until they are smooth
4. Rehearse the Stack and Close until it feels natural, not salesy
5. Do at least one full run-through with a test audience (friend, team member)

---

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Big Domino Statement written and validated
- [ ] Introduction follows all 8 slide types
- [ ] Three Secrets each contain an Epiphany Bridge story
- [ ] Three false belief categories covered (Vehicle, Internal, External)
- [ ] Stack Slide shows 10x+ value gap
- [ ] Guarantee is specific and bold
- [ ] Urgency element is real and compelling
- [ ] Total runtime between 60-90 minutes
- [ ] Rehearsed minimum 3 times
- [ ] Registration page created with compelling headline

---

## Handoff

Deliver to the team:
1. **Complete Slide Deck** -- Ready for presentation
2. **Speaker Script/Notes** -- Key talking points per slide
3. **Registration Page** -- Live and tested
4. **Email Sequence** -- Pre-webinar reminder series (3-5 emails)
5. **Follow-Up Sequence** -- Post-webinar emails for attendees and no-shows
6. **Replay Strategy** -- How long the replay will be available

*Estimated Time: 10-15 hours total*
*Review Cycle: After every live delivery (optimize based on data)*
