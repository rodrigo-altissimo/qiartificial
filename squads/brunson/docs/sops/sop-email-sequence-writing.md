# SOP: Email Sequence Writing

## Purpose

Write complete email sequences following Brunson's Soap Opera Sequence (for new leads) and Seinfeld Email (for ongoing engagement) frameworks. Ensure every new subscriber receives a story-driven onboarding that builds the Attractive Character relationship and converts leads into buyers.

---

## Prerequisites

- Attractive Character profile defined (backstory, parables, flaws, polarity)
- Epiphany Bridge origin story written
- Core offer and price point finalized
- Email marketing platform configured with automation capability
- Lead magnet or entry point defined (what triggered the opt-in)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Write the Soap Opera Sequence -- 5 Emails (3-4 hours)

**Email 1: Set the Stage (Day 0 -- immediate after opt-in)**
1. Deliver the lead magnet with a download link
2. Introduce yourself with a compelling one-sentence hook
3. Open a dramatic loop: hint at a story you will tell tomorrow
4. Set expectations: "Over the next few days, I am going to share..."
5. Subject line formula: "Your [lead magnet] + something personal..."

**Email 2: High Drama / Backstory (Day 1)**
1. Close the loop from Email 1
2. Tell your full backstory -- the struggle, the low point, the frustration
3. Build to the moment just BEFORE the epiphany
4. End with a new open loop: "And then I found something that changed everything..."
5. Subject line formula: "[Emotional moment from the story]"

**Email 3: The Epiphany (Day 2)**
1. Close the loop from Email 2
2. Share the breakthrough -- the "aha moment"
3. Connect the epiphany to your new opportunity
4. First soft mention of your offer (link, no hard sell)
5. Subject line formula: "The [discovery/realization] that changed everything"

**Email 4: Hidden Benefits (Day 3)**
1. Share 2-3 unexpected benefits they have not considered
2. Include a case study or testimonial as proof
3. Make the offer more concrete (what they get, how it works)
4. Stronger CTA with link to sales page
5. Subject line formula: "3 things about [topic] nobody tells you"

**Email 5: Urgency + Close (Day 4)**
1. Summarize the journey (where you started, what you found, results)
2. Social proof: others who took action and succeeded
3. Present the offer clearly and directly
4. Add urgency (deadline, scarcity, price increase)
5. Strong CTA: "Click here to [action] before [deadline]"
6. Subject line formula: "Last chance: [specific benefit or deadline]"

### Step 2: Write the Seinfeld Email Templates -- 10 Emails (3-4 hours)

After the SOS, transition to Seinfeld Emails (daily or 3-5x/week):

Create 10 template emails rotating through these types:
1. **Personal Story + Lesson** -- Everyday experience that connects to your expertise
2. **Us vs. Them** -- Your philosophy vs. the mainstream approach
3. **Case Study** -- Client success story with CTA to your offer
4. **Curiosity + Content** -- Share a tip with a "for the full system, check out [offer]"
5. **Controversy/Polarity** -- Strong opinion that your audience agrees with
6. **Behind the Scenes** -- Show your process, failures, or real life
7. **FAQ/Objection Handling** -- Answer a common objection through a story
8. **Social Proof Roundup** -- Multiple testimonials with CTA
9. **Parable** -- A metaphor or analogy that teaches
10. **Direct Pitch** -- Straightforward offer presentation (use sparingly)

### Step 3: Write Subject Lines (1 hour)

For each email, write 3 subject line options:
1. One curiosity-based ("The weird thing about...")
2. One benefit-based ("How to [result] in [timeframe]")
3. One story-based ("[Person] did [unexpected thing]")

Mark the top pick for each and save alternates for A/B testing.

### Step 4: Configure Automation (1 hour)

1. Set up the SOS as a 5-email automated sequence (one per day)
2. Set trigger: new subscriber opt-in
3. Set delay: 1 day between each email
4. After SOS completes, move subscriber into Seinfeld broadcast list
5. Tag subscribers who click offer links for retargeting

### Step 5: Review and QA (30 min)

1. Send test emails to yourself on desktop and mobile
2. Check all links work correctly
3. Verify formatting renders properly across email clients
4. Confirm automation triggers fire correctly
5. Review for typos, broken personalization tokens, and CTA clarity

---

## Quality Checks

- [ ] All 5 SOS emails follow the prescribed structure
- [ ] Each SOS email ends with an open loop (except Email 5)
- [ ] Offer is first mentioned in Email 3 (not before)
- [ ] Urgency in Email 5 is real and specific
- [ ] 10 Seinfeld email templates created covering all types
- [ ] 3 subject line options per email
- [ ] Automation tested end-to-end
- [ ] Mobile rendering verified
- [ ] All links tested and working

---

## Handoff

Deliver:
1. **Soap Opera Sequence** -- 5 complete emails, ready to load into platform
2. **Seinfeld Email Templates** -- 10 templates with fill-in sections
3. **Subject Line Library** -- All options organized by email
4. **Automation Map** -- Visual flow showing triggers, delays, and tags
5. **Performance Benchmarks** -- Target open rates and click rates per email

---

*Estimated Time: 8-10 hours total*
*Review Cycle: Monthly (analyze open/click rates and refresh underperformers)*
