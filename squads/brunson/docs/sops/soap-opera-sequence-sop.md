# SOP: Soap Opera Sequence

> Source: DotCom Secrets, Ch. 7 | Version: 1.0 | Agent: brunson-email

---

## 1. Purpose
Write a 5-email Soap Opera Sequence that builds instant rapport with new subscribers and makes the first offer.

## 2. Pre-conditions
- [ ] Lead magnet created and delivered
- [ ] Attractive Character defined
- [ ] First offer to promote identified
- [ ] Email service provider configured

## 3. Procedure

### Step 1: Define the Narrative Arc (10 min)
Map the 5-email story arc from your origin story. Each email is one episode.

### Step 2: Write Email 1 — Set the Stage (20 min)
- Deliver lead magnet
- Tease backstory: "I haven't always been where I am..."
- Open loop (cliffhanger ending)
- Set expectation for daily emails

### Step 3: Write Email 2 — High Drama (20 min)
- Continue backstory with crisis point
- Vulnerability and emotional depth
- End with cliffhanger before the epiphany

### Step 4: Write Email 3 — Epiphany (20 min)
- Reveal the discovery / turning point
- Share the core insight
- Transition from story to teaching
- Tease hidden benefits

### Step 5: Write Email 4 — Hidden Benefits (20 min)
- Share unexpected benefits
- Add social proof from others
- Soft transition toward the offer

### Step 6: Write Email 5 — Urgency & CTA (20 min)
- Recap the complete transformation
- Present the offer clearly
- Add genuine urgency
- Strong CTA with specific next step

### Step 7: Write Subject Lines (15 min)
Write 2-3 subject line options per email. Test the top performers.

### Step 8: Set Up Automation (15 min)
Configure in email platform:
- Trigger: new subscriber
- Delay: 1 email per day for 5 days
- After SOS: transition to Seinfeld Emails

## 4. Outputs
- [ ] 5 emails (200-400 words each)
- [ ] Subject lines (2-3 per email)
- [ ] Automation workflow configured

## 5. Quality Checks
- [ ] Each email has an open loop (except #5)
- [ ] Stories are emotionally engaging
- [ ] Final email has clear CTA
- [ ] Emails are 200-400 words
- [ ] Mobile-friendly formatting
