# SOP: Mass Movement Launch

## Purpose

Launch a mass movement around your expertise following Brunson's three-pillar framework: Charismatic Leader, Future-Based Cause, and New Opportunity. This SOP covers the full sequence from identity creation through community activation to self-sustaining growth.

---

## Prerequisites

- Attractive Character profile fully developed
- Opportunity Switch clearly defined (old way vs. new way)
- Epiphany Bridge origin story written and polished
- Value Ladder designed with at least the first two rungs
- Content creation capability (video, written, or audio)
- Community platform selected (Facebook Group, Circle, Skool, or similar)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Define Movement Identity (2 hours)

1. **Name the movement:** Create a distinctive, memorable identity label
   - Formula: [Verb/Noun] + [Modifier] (e.g., "Funnel Hackers," "Lady Bosses")
   - Test: Can someone proudly say "I am a [label]"?
2. **Write the rallying cry:** One sentence that captures the movement's promise
   - Formula: "You are just one [vehicle] away from [dream result]"
3. **Identify the common enemy:** What are you fighting against?
   - Old way, establishment, gatekeepers, ignorance, or complication
4. **Write the manifesto:** 1-page declaration of movement beliefs and values
   - What we believe, what we stand for, what we reject, where we are going
5. **Create visual identity:** Logo, colors, hashtag for the movement

### Step 2: Build the Origin Content (1 week)

1. Record your origin story as a video (15-30 minutes)
2. Write the origin story as a long-form post or article
3. Create a "manifesto video" (3-5 minutes) summarizing the movement's purpose
4. Design a "welcome sequence" for new movement members (3-5 pieces of content)
5. Create shareable assets: quote graphics, manifesto snippets, identity badges

### Step 3: Launch the Community (1 week)

1. Create the community space (group, forum, or platform)
2. Write the community description using movement language
3. Set community rules that reinforce movement values
4. Invite your first 50-100 members personally (existing audience, friends, early supporters)
5. Post the origin story and manifesto as the first content
6. Host a live kickoff event (webinar, live stream, or call)

### Step 4: Activate the First Believers (Weeks 2-4)

1. Post daily content in the community using AC storyline types
2. Host weekly live sessions (Q&A, training, guest interviews)
3. Create a "founding member" challenge or mini-course (free)
4. Celebrate early wins loudly (spotlight members who take action)
5. Encourage members to share their own stories using the movement hashtag
6. Begin building the "Four-Minute Mile" proof (first success stories)

### Step 5: Convert Believers to Customers (Month 2)

1. Run the Perfect Webinar to the community (first live offering)
2. Launch the frontend offer on the Value Ladder
3. Create case studies from founding members who got results
4. Begin Dream 100 outreach to bring outside traffic to the movement
5. Start paid traffic campaigns driving to a lead magnet that feeds into the community

### Step 6: Scale and Sustain (Month 3+)

1. Create rituals: weekly lives, monthly challenges, annual event
2. Develop member ambassadors who recruit new members organically
3. Plan a live or virtual event (Funnel Hacking Live equivalent)
4. Launch upper-tier Value Ladder offers to engaged members
5. Document and share movement milestones (100 members, first $1M, etc.)

---

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Movement name is distinctive and identity-forming ("I am a ___")
- [ ] Rallying cry is one clear, inspiring sentence
- [ ] Common enemy is defined without attacking individuals
- [ ] Manifesto is written and published
- [ ] Origin story recorded in video and written formats
- [ ] Community space is live with rules and welcome content
- [ ] First 50+ members invited and active
- [ ] Weekly live content schedule established
- [ ] First success stories documented
- [ ] Dream 100 outreach initiated

---

## Handoff

Deliver at each phase:
1. **Movement Identity Kit** -- Name, rallying cry, manifesto, visual assets
2. **Origin Content Package** -- Video, written story, shareable graphics
3. **Community Launch Report** -- Members, engagement, first content performance
4. **Growth Dashboard** -- New members, engagement rate, content reach, conversion rate
5. **Phase Plan** -- Next 30-day actions for growth and monetization

---

*Estimated Time: 4-6 weeks for full launch*
*Review Cycle: Weekly during launch, monthly for growth phase*
