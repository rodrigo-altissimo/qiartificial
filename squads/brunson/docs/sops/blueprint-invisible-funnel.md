# Blueprint: Invisible Funnel

## Overview

The Invisible Funnel removes ALL risk for the prospect by letting them consume the content before deciding to pay. The prospect registers for a free training or event, receives full value, and then chooses to pay only if they found it valuable. This flips the traditional model: instead of "pay first, hope it is good," you deliver first and let quality speak for itself. Brunson calls it "invisible" because there is no visible sales barrier -- the funnel feels like pure value delivery. This builds massive trust and works exceptionally well with skeptical or cold audiences.

## Components

### Registration Page
- Headline: "Free [Training/Workshop/Masterclass]"
- No mention of any price or upsell
- Pure value proposition: what they will learn
- Social proof: past attendee results
- Clear date/time and format details
- Registration: name + email

### Free Training Delivery
- Live webinar, recorded training, or multi-session workshop
- Deliver genuine, complete value (do not hold back)
- Use Perfect Webinar elements: stories, belief breaking, teaching
- Duration: 60-120 minutes
- The quality of training IS the sales pitch

### "Pay What It Was Worth" Offer
- Presented at the end of the training (or 24 hours after)
- Framing: "If you found this valuable, here is how to keep lifetime access"
- Price: $97-$997 (includes recordings, bonuses, community access)
- No pressure language: "Only pay if you genuinely found value"
- This creates reciprocity -- people who received value WANT to pay

### Upsell (For Those Who Pay)
- Presented after payment
- Next step on the Value Ladder
- Complementary offer that deepens the transformation

### Non-Buyer Follow-Up
- Continue nurturing with valuable content
- Do NOT pressure or guilt-trip
- Future offers presented through regular email marketing
- They stay on your list as warm leads

## Implementation Steps

1. Design a training that delivers genuine, standalone value
2. Build the registration page (pure value positioning, no sales language)
3. Create the training content (pre-record or prepare for live delivery)
4. Build the "pay what it was worth" offer page
5. Create the offer: lifetime access + bonuses for paying customers
6. Build the upsell page for buyers
7. Write registration confirmation and reminder emails
8. Write the post-training "thank you + offer" email sequence
9. Write the non-buyer nurture sequence
10. Deliver the training (live first time recommended)
11. Present the offer after training
12. Follow up and nurture all leads

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Registration Rate | 25-45% | Registrants / Page visitors |
| Show-Up Rate | 30-50% | Attendees / Registrants |
| Payment Conversion | 10-25% | Payers / Attendees |
| Average Payment | $97-$497 | Total revenue / Payers |
| Upsell Rate | 10-20% | Upsell buyers / Payers |
| 30-Day Non-Buyer Conversion | 3-8% | Late buyers / Non-payers |
| NPS / Satisfaction | 8+ / 10 | Survey score |

**Goal:** Build massive goodwill, an engaged email list, and convert through quality rather than pressure. This funnel optimizes for trust and long-term relationship.
