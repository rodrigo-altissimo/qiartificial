# Blueprint: Affiliate Funnel

## Overview

The Affiliate Funnel leverages other people's audiences to drive traffic into YOUR funnel through affiliate partnerships. Instead of spending money on ads, you incentivize partners (your Dream 100 list) to promote your offer in exchange for commissions. This funnel includes a bridge page that allows affiliates to add their own story and recommendation before sending traffic to your sales process. When scaled, affiliate traffic can become your largest and most profitable traffic source because affiliates pay THEMSELVES from commissions.

## Components

### Affiliate Recruitment System
- Identify 50-100 potential affiliates from Dream 100 list
- Create affiliate program page explaining commissions and benefits
- Personal outreach to top prospects with value-first approach
- Automated affiliate sign-up for smaller partners
- Commission structure: 30-50% on frontend, 20-30% on upsells

### Affiliate Resource Center
- Swipe email copy (5-10 emails they can customize)
- Ad creative templates (images, video scripts)
- Bridge page template (pre-built, affiliate adds their recommendation)
- Product overview and talking points
- FAQ document for their audience's common questions
- Tracking links and unique affiliate IDs

### Bridge Page (Affiliate-Controlled)
- Short page where the affiliate adds their personal recommendation
- Usually a video: "Hey, [affiliate name] here. I want to tell you about..."
- Adds the affiliate's credibility and trust to your offer
- CTA drives to your funnel with affiliate tracking link

### Your Funnel (Affiliate-Optimized)
- Same core funnel (squeeze, sales page, order form, upsells)
- Cookie-based affiliate tracking throughout
- Attribution for commissions on all funnel revenue
- Real-time tracking dashboard for affiliates

### Affiliate Engagement System
- Leaderboard showing top affiliates (competition drives promotion)
- Contest with prizes for top promoters
- Weekly updates to affiliates on conversion rates and tips
- Personal relationship management with top 20 affiliates
- Commission payouts on schedule (weekly or bi-weekly)

## Implementation Steps

1. Set up affiliate tracking software (ClickFunnels Backpack, FirstPromoter, etc.)
2. Define commission structure (percentages, cookie duration, multi-tier)
3. Create the affiliate resource center with all assets
4. Build the bridge page template for affiliates to customize
5. Ensure your funnel is optimized for conversion (affiliates promote winners)
6. Recruit first 10-20 affiliates through personal outreach
7. Provide affiliates with training on how to promote effectively
8. Launch with a coordinated "affiliate launch" date
9. Run a contest with prizes for top affiliates during launch
10. Track performance and optimize based on affiliate feedback
11. Pay commissions on schedule (trust is everything)
12. Scale by recruiting more affiliates and supporting top performers

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Active Affiliates | 20-50 | Affiliates who sent traffic this month |
| Traffic from Affiliates | 30-50% of total | Affiliate traffic / Total traffic |
| Affiliate Conversion Rate | Equal or above paid | Sales / Affiliate visitors |
| Earnings Per Click (EPC) | $1-$5 | Revenue / Affiliate clicks |
| Average Commission | 30-50% | Commission paid / Revenue generated |
| Affiliate Retention | 40-60% | Affiliates promoting 3+ months |
| Cost Per Acquisition (via affil) | < paid CPA | Commissions / Sales |
| Revenue from Affiliates | Growing monthly | Total affiliate-attributed revenue |

**EPC (Earnings Per Click) is the key number.** Affiliates compare your EPC to other programs. High EPC = affiliates promote you more aggressively.
