# SOP: Epiphany Bridge Writing

## Purpose

Write compelling Epiphany Bridge stories that transfer your "aha moment" to the audience, breaking false beliefs and creating desire for your offer. This SOP covers the process of mining stories, structuring them, and polishing them for use in webinars, emails, sales pages, and content.

---

## Prerequisites

- Identified false belief to be broken (Vehicle, Internal, or External)
- Personal experience or case study that relates to this belief
- Understanding of the 8-step Epiphany Bridge structure
- Target audience avatar defined (from Secret Formula)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Identify the Target Belief (15 min)

1. Write the false belief you need to break: "[Audience believes ___]"
2. Write the new belief you need to install: "[They need to believe ___]"
3. Write the experience that likely created their false belief
4. Write the story they tell themselves because of this belief

### Step 2: Mine Your Story (30 min)

Answer these questions to excavate the raw material:

1. When did YOU first have the same false belief?
2. What experience changed your mind?
3. What was happening in your life at that time?
4. Who else was involved?
5. What specific details do you remember (place, time, weather, emotions)?
6. What did you try before the epiphany that failed?
7. What was the exact moment of realization?
8. How did your life change after the epiphany?

If you do not have a personal story, use a client story or a "what if" scenario based on real patterns.

### Step 3: Structure the 8 Steps (45 min)

Write 2-3 sentences for each step:

1. **Backstory:** Set the scene. Where were you? What was your life like?
2. **Desire:** What did you want? (External goal + Internal emotional need)
3. **Wall:** What barrier stopped you? What was the frustration?
4. **Epiphany:** What was the "aha moment"? The realization that changed everything?
5. **Plan:** What did you do with this new understanding?
6. **Conflict:** What went wrong? What obstacle appeared after the epiphany?
7. **Achievement:** What was the result? (Can be success or instructive failure)
8. **Transformation:** Who did you BECOME? How did your identity change?

### Step 4: Add Emotional Depth (30 min)

For each step, enhance with:
- **Sensory details:** What did you see, hear, feel physically?
- **Internal dialogue:** What were you thinking at each moment?
- **Specific details:** Dates, names, numbers, locations (specificity = credibility)
- **Vulnerability:** Where were you most afraid, ashamed, or uncertain?
- **Dialogue:** Recreate key conversations (even approximately)

### Step 5: Write the First Draft (45 min)

1. Write the story as a continuous narrative (500-1,000 words for webinar use)
2. Start with the most dramatic moment (not chronologically)
3. Use present tense for dramatic moments ("I walk into the room and...")
4. End with the bridge to your audience: "And that is exactly why I created [offer]..."
5. Read it aloud and mark where energy drops

### Step 6: Edit and Polish (30 min)

1. Cut anything that does not serve the belief change
2. Strengthen the epiphany moment (this is the climax)
3. Ensure the transformation (Step 8) is clear and emotionally resonant
4. Verify the story naturally leads to your offer without feeling forced
5. Read aloud again -- does it flow? Does it move you?

### Step 7: Adapt for Different Formats (30 min)

- **Webinar version:** 5-10 minutes spoken (500-1,000 words)
- **Email version:** 300-500 words with open loop ending
- **Social media version:** 100-200 words with hook opening
- **Sales page version:** 800-1,500 words with proof elements added

---

## Quality Checks

- [ ] False belief clearly identified and story directly addresses it
- [ ] All 8 steps are present (none skipped)
- [ ] Emotional depth exists (feelings, not just events)
- [ ] Specific details included (dates, names, places, numbers)
- [ ] Vulnerability present (the character has real struggles)
- [ ] Epiphany moment is clear and powerful
- [ ] Transformation (Step 8) shows identity change
- [ ] Story bridges naturally to the offer
- [ ] Multiple format versions created

---

## Handoff

Deliver:
1. **Master Story Document** -- Full-length version with all 8 steps
2. **Webinar Script Version** -- 5-10 minute spoken version
3. **Email Version** -- 300-500 word version for Soap Opera Sequence
4. **Social Media Version** -- 100-200 word version
5. **Belief Change Summary** -- One line: "This story breaks the belief that [X] and installs the belief that [Y]"

---

*Estimated Time: 3-4 hours per story*
*Review Cycle: After each use (refine based on audience response)*
