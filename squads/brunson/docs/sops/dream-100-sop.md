# SOP: Dream 100 Campaign

> Source: Traffic Secrets, Ch. 3-5 | Version: 1.0 | Agent: brunson-traffic

---

## 1. Purpose
Build and execute a Dream 100 traffic campaign that identifies where dream customers congregate and systematically infiltrates those congregations.

## 2. Pre-conditions
- [ ] Funnel built and tested
- [ ] Lead magnet ready
- [ ] Content creation capacity available

## 3. Procedure

### Step 1: Define Your Market Position (10 min)
- Which of the 3 core markets? (Health / Wealth / Relationships)
- Which submarket?
- Which niche within the submarket?

### Step 2: Research Congregations (45 min)
For each platform (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, podcasts, blogs):
- Search for top accounts in your niche
- Identify groups with 10K+ members
- Find podcasts with your audience as listeners
- Note newsletters with relevant readership

### Step 3: Build the Dream 100 List (60 min)
For each of the 100 entries, record:
- Name / URL
- Platform
- Audience size
- Audience overlap (1-10)
- Accessibility (1-10)
- Relationship status (cold/warm/hot)
- Work In strategy
- Buy In strategy

### Step 4: Prioritize Top 25 (15 min)
Score: (Overlap x Size x Accessibility) and rank. Focus on top 25 first.

### Step 5: Design Work In Strategy (30 min)
For top 25:
- Week 1-2: Follow, engage, comment daily
- Week 3-4: Share content, tag, reference
- Week 5-6: Send personalized outreach (value first, no pitch)
- Week 7-8: Propose collaboration

### Step 6: Design Buy In Strategy (20 min)
For quick results:
- Which audiences to target with ads?
- Which podcasts to sponsor?
- Which newsletters to buy ads in?
- Budget allocation per target

### Step 7: Create Content Plan (30 min)
Design content that serves both Work In and audience building:
- Daily social hooks (platform-specific)
- Weekly long-form content
- Collaboration content pieces

### Step 8: Set Up Tracking (15 min)
- UTM parameters per Dream 100 source
- CRM/spreadsheet for relationship tracking
- Conversion tracking per source

## 4. Outputs
- [ ] Dream 100 spreadsheet (complete list)
- [ ] Top 25 research profiles
- [ ] Work In outreach sequence
- [ ] Buy In budget plan
- [ ] Content calendar (30 days)
- [ ] Tracking system

## 5. Quality Checks
- [ ] 100 entries identified
- [ ] Top 25 deeply researched
- [ ] Both Work In and Buy In strategies planned
- [ ] Tracking configured
