# SOP: Hook Creation

## Purpose

Generate a library of high-performing hooks for ads, emails, landing pages, and content. Hooks are the single most important element in marketing -- without a hook, nothing else gets seen. This SOP provides a systematic process for creating, testing, and iterating hooks.

---

## Prerequisites

- Dream customer avatar defined (from Secret Formula)
- Core offer details finalized
- List of customer pain points, desires, and objections
- Access to competitor ads and content for reference (Facebook Ad Library, etc.)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Research Customer Language (30 min)

1. Read 20-30 reviews of competitor products (Amazon, Trustpilot, G2)
2. Scan 10 forum threads or social media posts where your audience discusses their problems
3. Document exact phrases, words, and emotional expressions they use
4. Create a "Voice of Customer" document with 30+ raw phrases

### Step 2: Generate Hook Categories (30 min)

Create 3-5 hooks in each category:

**Question Hooks:** Ask a provocative question
- "What if everything you know about [topic] is wrong?"
- "Are you making this $10K mistake with your [topic]?"

**Contrarian Hooks:** Challenge conventional wisdom
- "Stop [common advice]. Here is why it is killing your [result]."
- "The [topic] advice nobody wants to hear"

**Story Hooks:** Open with a narrative
- "I was [relatable situation] when I discovered..."
- "Last Tuesday, something happened that changed how I think about [topic]..."

**Result Hooks:** Lead with a specific outcome
- "How I [specific result] in [timeframe] using [method]"
- "[Number] [unit] in [timeframe]: the exact strategy"

**Curiosity Hooks:** Create an information gap
- "The #1 [topic] mistake (it is not what you think)"
- "The weird trick that [unexpected result]"

**Fear Hooks:** Highlight a risk or threat
- "Your [asset] has a fatal flaw and you probably do not know it"
- "Why 90% of [audience] fail at [goal] (and how to be in the 10%)"

### Step 3: Apply Hook Rules (15 min)

Review each hook against these criteria:
1. **Specific** -- Contains numbers, timeframes, or concrete details
2. **Relevant** -- Directly connected to the dream customer's desires or fears
3. **Curiosity Gap** -- Creates an open loop the reader must close
4. **Platform-Native** -- Matches the format and tone of the target platform
5. **Honest** -- Accurately represents what follows (no clickbait that disappoints)

### Step 4: Match Hooks to Formats (30 min)

Adapt top hooks for each marketing channel:

| Channel | Hook Format | Length |
|---------|------------|--------|
| Facebook/Instagram Ad | Visual + 1-2 lines text | 5-15 words |
| Email Subject Line | Text only, mobile-preview friendly | 4-9 words |
| YouTube Thumbnail + Title | Image + text overlay | 6-12 words |
| Landing Page Headline | Bold text, above the fold | 8-15 words |
| Social Media Post | First line of caption | 10-20 words |

### Step 5: Prioritize for Testing (15 min)

1. Select your top 5-10 hooks per channel
2. Rank by predicted impact (gut + data from competitor research)
3. Choose 3-5 to test first per campaign
4. Set up A/B testing structure (same content, different hooks)

### Step 6: Test and Iterate (Ongoing)

1. Run each hook for minimum 1,000 impressions before judging
2. Measure: CTR for ads, open rate for emails, scroll-stop rate for social
3. Kill underperformers after sufficient data (below average for the platform)
4. Double down on winners and create variations of top performers
5. Document all results in a Hook Performance Database

---

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Minimum 20 hooks generated across all categories
- [ ] Each hook passes the 5-rule validation (specific, relevant, curiosity, native, honest)
- [ ] Hooks adapted for at least 3 different platforms
- [ ] A/B test structure defined before launch
- [ ] Voice of Customer research informs the language used
- [ ] No hooks that promise what the content cannot deliver

---

## Handoff

Deliver:
1. **Hook Library** -- All generated hooks organized by category and platform
2. **Test Plan** -- Which hooks to test first and on which channels
3. **Voice of Customer Document** -- Raw language research for future hook creation
4. **Performance Tracker** -- Template for logging hook test results
5. **Top 5 Recommended Hooks** -- Best candidates for immediate deployment

---

*Estimated Time: 2-3 hours initial creation, then 30 min/week for testing*
*Review Cycle: Weekly during active campaigns*
