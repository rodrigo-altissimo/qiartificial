# Blueprint: Product Launch Funnel

## Overview

The Product Launch Funnel creates massive anticipation and urgency around a new offer using a series of pre-launch content (PLC) videos followed by a time-limited cart open/close window. Adapted from Jeff Walker's Product Launch Formula and integrated into Brunson's funnel architecture, this funnel leverages scarcity, social proof, and progressive belief-building to generate concentrated revenue within a short window. A well-executed launch can generate 30-70% of annual revenue in a single 7-day period.

## Components

### PLC Video 1: "The Opportunity" (Day 1)
- Show what is POSSIBLE (new opportunity, results, vision)
- Teach one valuable concept (give before asking)
- Establish your authority and relatability
- End with open loop for PLC 2
- Drive engagement: comments, shares, replies

### PLC Video 2: "The Transformation" (Day 4)
- Show HOW it works (case study, methodology preview)
- Demonstrate a client or personal transformation
- Break the primary false belief (Vehicle belief)
- End with open loop for PLC 3

### PLC Video 3: "The Ownership Experience" (Day 7)
- Help them visualize HAVING the result
- Break Internal and External false beliefs
- Build anticipation for the offer
- Tease the launch date and what is coming

### Cart Open Sales Page
- Full Hook-Story-Offer sales page
- Complete Stack with bonuses and values
- Guarantee and payment options
- Launch-exclusive bonuses (not available later)
- Countdown timer to cart close

### Launch Week Email Sequence (7 days)
- Day 1: "Doors are open" + excitement
- Day 2: Social proof + early buyer testimonials
- Day 3: Case study + FAQ
- Day 4: Objection handling via Epiphany Bridge story
- Day 5: More proof + payment plan reminder
- Day 6: "24 hours left" warning
- Day 7: Final call (3 emails: morning, afternoon, evening)

## Implementation Steps

1. Define the offer, price, and launch-exclusive bonuses
2. Script and record 3 PLC videos (15-30 min each)
3. Build PLC hosting pages (video + comments section)
4. Write the complete email sequence (pre-launch + launch = 15-20 emails)
5. Build the sales page with full Stack
6. Configure cart open/close dates with hard deadline
7. Create social media content plan for launch period
8. Set up retargeting ads for PLC viewers
9. Build anticipation: announce launch date 2-3 weeks in advance
10. Release PLC videos on schedule with email support
11. Open cart and execute 7-day launch sequence
12. Close cart at the announced time
13. Debrief and plan post-launch follow-up

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| PLC 1 View Rate | 30-50% | Viewers / List size |
| PLC 2 View Rate | 20-40% | Viewers / PLC 1 viewers |
| PLC 3 View Rate | 15-35% | Viewers / PLC 2 viewers |
| Day 1 Sales (% of total) | 30-40% | Day 1 revenue / Total launch revenue |
| Day 7 Sales (% of total) | 30-40% | Final day revenue / Total launch revenue |
| Launch Conversion | 2-8% | Buyers / Total list size |
| Revenue Per Subscriber | $5-$25 | Total revenue / List size |
| Refund Rate | < 5% | Refunds / Total purchases |

**Revenue Pattern:** Typically U-shaped -- 30-40% Day 1, tapering mid-week, 30-40% final day.
