## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Apple's design language is a study in disciplined restraint. The palette is almost monochromatic — dense black, near-black `#1d1d1f`, and a whisper of warm gray `#f5f5f7` — punctuated by a single accent blue `#0071e3` that arrives only when action is required. There are no gradients, no layered borders, no decorative hues competing for attention. The system trusts whitespace, large-format imagery, and weight 600 headlines to do the emotional work.

Typography is SF Pro, Apple's proprietary family spanning Display and Text optical sizes. Display weights are consistently 600 (semibold), delivering confident, authoritative headlines that anchor each product section without ever reaching 700 or 800. The text optical size switches to weight 400 at body sizes, creating a natural rhythm between dense section headings and open, breathable body copy. Letter-spacing tightens at display sizes and loosens slightly at body sizes — the system never applies positive tracking to large text.

Surfaces are flat and clean. Apple eschews drop shadows on content cards entirely, relying instead on alternating background colors (`#ffffff` and `#f5f5f7`) to create visual separation. The sole exception is the global navigation bar, which employs a frosted-glass `backdrop-filter: saturate(180%) blur(20px)` over a translucent `rgba(250,250,252,0.8)` background — the signature Apple "glass" treatment that grounds the nav without visually blocking content. This is the only glassmorphism in the system.

The single most distinctive visual choice is the pill-shaped button radius: `980px`. Every primary CTA on apple.com is a perfect capsule, immediately recognizable as Apple's button language, with no sharp corners, no subtle rounding, no in-between — full pill or nothing.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Monochromatic palette anchored by near-black `#1d1d1f` and warm gray `#f5f5f7`
- Single accent blue `#0071e3` reserved exclusively for interactive elements
- SF Pro Display at weight 600 for all headlines; SF Pro Text at weight 400 for body
- Pill-shaped buttons with `980px` border-radius as the default CTA shape
- Frosted-glass navigation with `backdrop-filter: saturate(180%) blur(20px)`
- Zero box-shadow on content surfaces — depth through background alternation
- Tight letter-spacing at display sizes, standard at body sizes
- Full-bleed product imagery as the primary visual anchor
- 44px navigation height matching iOS touch-target conventions
- Maximum content width of 980px for contained text blocks

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

### Primary

- **Apple Black** (`#000000`): Brand identity color. The Apple logo, header wordmarks, and brand-immersive dark sections use pure black. Apple's brand identity is black-and-white minimalism — black is the color you'd name when describing the brand.

### Brand & Dark

- **Near Black** (`#1d1d1f`): `--text-color`. Primary heading and hero text color. Used instead of pure `#000000` for body content to reduce harshness on light backgrounds.
- **Dark Surface** (`#161617`): `--r-globalnav-background-opened-dark`. Dark-mode nav and immersive section backgrounds.
- **Dark Gray** (`#333336`): `--r-globalnav-color-secondary`. Secondary text in dark contexts, search input values.

### Accent Colors

- **Apple Blue** (`#0071e3`): `--sk-focus-color`, `--sk-button-background`. The system's sole accent — used for CTAs, focus rings, and interactive highlights.
- **Apple Blue Hover** (`#0077ed`): `--sk-button-background-hover`. Lightened blue for hover states.
- **Apple Blue Active** (`#006edb`): `--sk-button-background-active`. Darkened blue for pressed states.
- **Link Blue** (`#0066cc`): `--buystrip-link-color`, `--sk-blocklink-color`. Body-text link color, slightly deeper than CTA blue.
- **Link Blue Dark** (`#2997ff`): `--sk-blocklink-color` dark variant. Links on dark backgrounds.

### Interactive

- **Focus Ring** (`#0071e3`): `--sk-focus-color`. 2px solid outline with 3px offset for keyboard navigation.
- **Focus Ring Alt** (`#000000`): `--sk-focus-color-alt`. Alternative focus indicator for light-on-dark contexts.
- **Disabled Opacity**: `0.42` via `--sk-link-disabled-opacity`. Applied uniformly to all disabled interactive elements.

### Neutral Scale

- **Near Black** (`#1d1d1f`): Primary heading text.
- **Dark Gray** (`#333336`): Secondary heading / navigation text.
- **Gray 500** (`#6e6e73`): `--r-globalnav-search-icon-fill`. Muted text, placeholders, secondary labels.
- **Gray 400** (`#86868b`): Tertiary labels, helper text.
- **Gray 300** (`#d2d2d7`): Hairline borders, subtle dividers.
- **Gray 200** (`#e8e8ed`): Light borders, hover backgrounds.

### Surface & Borders

- **Warm Gray** (`#f5f5f7`): `--background-color`, `--footer-background`. Alternating section backgrounds, card fills.
- **Off White** (`#fafafc`): `--r-globalnav-background-opened`. Nav flyout background, message bar.
- **Pure White** (`#ffffff`): Page background, button text, card surfaces.
- **Border Gray** (`#d2d2d7`): Standard border/divider color.
- **Section Border** (`rgba(0,0,0,0.16)`): `--footer-border-color`, `--buystrip-border-color`. Transparent black at 16% for subtle section dividers.

### Shadow Colors

The system is intentionally flat. No colored shadows or blue-tinted shadows exist. The sole shadow declaration found is `0 4px 18px rgba(0,0,0,0.25)` on an overlay play button — not a pattern-level decision.

### Color Philosophy

Apple's palette is intentionally desaturated. The near-absence of color forces the eye to product imagery and typography hierarchy. Blue appears only at the moment of decision — "Buy", "Learn More", link text — creating an unambiguous action signal. Everything else is black, gray, or white. This extreme restraint is the palette: not a limitation, but a deliberate brand position that says "the product is the color."

## 3. Typography Rules

### Font Family

The primary family is **SF Pro**, split into two optical sizes: **SF Pro Display** for headlines (≥20px) and **SF Pro Text** for body copy (<20px). The system also includes locale-specific companions: SF Pro JP (Japanese), SF Pro KR (Korean), SF Pro SC/TC/HK (Chinese variants), and SF Pro AR (Arabic). Fallback stack: `Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

No monospace font is declared on this surface. No OpenType feature settings (`font-feature-settings`) are applied.

### Hierarchy

| Role | Font | Size | Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing | Features | Notes |

| section-heading | SF Pro Display | 48px | 600 | 1.088 | -0.003em | — | Section titles ("iPhone", "MacBook Air") |
| subheading-large | SF Pro Display | 32px | 600 | 1.125 | 0.004em | — | Subsection headers |
| subheading | SF Pro Display | 28px | 600 | 1.143 | 0.007em | — | Card titles, feature headings |

| body-large | SF Pro Text | 21px | 400 | 1.381 | 0.011em | — | Lead paragraphs |
| body | SF Pro Text | 17px | 400 | 1.471 | -0.022em | — | Standard body copy |
| body-small | SF Pro Text | 14px | 400 | 1.429 | -0.016em | — | Secondary body, footer links |
| button | SF Pro Text | 17px | 400 | 1.176 | -0.022em | — | Primary CTA labels |

### Principles

- SF Pro Display at weight 600 is the ONLY headline weight — Apple never uses 700, 800, or 900 for page headings.
- Body copy is always weight 400. Weight 500 appears only in rare interactive elements, never in prose.
- Letter-spacing is negative at display sizes (-0.015em at 96px) and transitions to slightly negative (-0.022em) at body 17px — Apple tracks tight, never loose.
- Line height follows a clear inversion: tighter at large sizes (1.04 at 96px) and more open at small sizes (1.47 at 17px).
- The Display↔Text optical size switch happens around 20px — below that threshold, use SF Pro Text for better readability.
- No italic styles are used on apple.com's marketing pages. Emphasis is conveyed through weight or color, never through slant.

## 5. Layout Principles

### Spacing System

Base unit: 4px. Apple's spacing follows a loose 4px grid. Observed values: 4, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 17, 22, 24, 44, 56px. The values `17px` and `22px` appear frequently in Apple's system — not exact multiples of 4, but consistent with Apple's optical-over-mathematical spacing philosophy.

### Grid & Container

- **Max content width**: 980px (`--buystrip-content-maxwidth`)
- **Content padding**: 22px horizontal at desktop, 16px at ≤833px
- **Hero pattern**: Full-viewport-width imagery with centered text overlay
- **Product grid**: 2-up and 3-up card layouts within the 980px container, with `--gutter-width` managing gaps

### Whitespace Philosophy

Apple's whitespace is generous by design. Each product section is a self-contained visual statement: a large image, a concise headline, a sub-line, and 1-2 CTA links — surrounded by expansive vertical padding. The whitespace is not empty space but active composition, directing the eye downward in a controlled scroll narrative. This "one thing at a time" rhythm is the homepage's structural DNA.

### Border Radius Scale

- **0px**: Input fields, inline elements
- **5px**: Small interactive elements, compact UI
- **8px**: Localnav buttons, secondary interactive elements
- **10px**: Mobile-scaled buttons, medium surfaces
- **12px**: Modal corners, medium containers
- **16px**: Section cards, content tiles
- **980px**: Primary CTA buttons (full pill)

## 6. Depth & Elevation

| Level | Treatment | Use |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| Flat | No shadow, solid background | Default. Product cards, section tiles, body content |
| Glass | `backdrop-filter: saturate(180%) blur(20px)` + translucent bg | Global navigation bar only |
| Scrim | `rgba(0,0,0,0.48)` overlay | Modal backdrop, video overlay dimming |
| Ring | `0 0 0 3px #fff, 0 0 0 5px #0071e3` | Keyboard focus indicator (double-ring pattern) |

The system does not use ambient shadows, card elevation, or layered shadow stacks. There is no shadow philosophy because shadows are not part of Apple's web design vocabulary. Depth is communicated exclusively through:
1. Background color alternation (`#ffffff` ↔ `#f5f5f7`)
2. The frosted-glass nav bar (sole glass element)
3. Modal scrims (full-viewport dark overlay)

### Shadow Philosophy

Apple's web surfaces are intentionally shadowless. Unlike iOS/macOS where elevation is communicated through Material-style shadows, apple.com conveys hierarchy through background contrast and content sequencing. This flat approach reinforces the "print editorial" feel — each section is a page in a premium catalog, not a layered interface.

## 7. Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- ✅ Use SF Pro Display at weight 600 for ALL headlines — this is Apple's typographic signature
- ✅ Use `980px` border-radius for primary CTA buttons — the pill shape IS the Apple button
- ✅ Use `#0071e3` Apple Blue exclusively for interactive elements: buttons, links, focus rings
- ✅ Alternate section backgrounds between `#ffffff` and `#f5f5f7` for visual rhythm
- ✅ Use `#1d1d1f` (near-black) for headings, never pure `#000000` in body content
- ✅ Apply `backdrop-filter: saturate(180%) blur(20px)` ONLY to the global navigation
- ✅ Use the double-ring focus pattern: `0 0 0 3px #fff, 0 0 0 5px #0071e3`
- ✅ Keep body copy at SF Pro Text weight 400 — never bold paragraph text
- ✅ Use 44px height for nav elements (matches iOS touch targets)
- ✅ Maintain 980px max-width for text content containers

### Don'ts

- ❌ Don't use weight 700 or 800 for headlines — SF Pro Display 600 is Apple's ceiling; heavier weights are not part of the web design system
- ❌ Don't use rounded corners between 1-15px on CTA buttons — Apple buttons are either full pill (`980px`) or specific component radii (`8px`, `12px`), never generic `4px` or `6px`
- ❌ Don't apply `box-shadow` to content cards — Apple's web cards are flat; depth comes from background alternation, not shadows
- ❌ Don't use `#0071e3` for decorative purposes — Apple Blue is strictly functional (CTA, focus, link)
- ❌ Don't use gradients on surfaces or buttons — Apple's marketing site is flat color only, no linear/radial gradients on UI elements
- ❌ Don't add `backdrop-filter` to cards, modals, or any element besides the nav bar — the glass effect is reserved for navigation only
- ❌ Don't use positive letter-spacing at display sizes — SF Pro Display tracks tight (-0.015em at 96px), never loose
- ❌ Don't use `#000000` pure black for body text — Apple uses `#1d1d1f` which reads as black but is warmer and less harsh
- ❌ Don't use weight 300 or 200 for body text — unlike some premium brands that go ultralight, Apple keeps body at 400
- ❌ Don't add colored borders to cards or sections — Apple uses transparent borders or `rgba(0,0,0,0.16)` at most

## 8. Responsive Behavior

### Breakpoints

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Large | ≥1441px | Full-width hero imagery, maximum typography scale |
| Medium-Large | 1069–1440px | Standard layout, `--background-color: #f5f5f7`, `--text-color: #1d1d1f` |
| Medium | 735–1068px | Reduced hero sizes, 2-column card grids collapse |
| Small | ≤734px | Single column, 48px nav height, enlarged button touch targets |
| Compact | ≤640px | Button padding increases (22px h, 12px v), radius shifts to 10px, margins to 18px |

### Touch Targets

- Navigation bar height: 44px desktop → 48px mobile (≤833px)
- Button minimum width: 60px desktop → 70px mobile
- Button padding: 9px 16px desktop → 12px 22px mobile
- Focus offset: remains 3px across all sizes

### Collapsing Strategy

- **Typography**: SF Pro Display sizes scale down proportionally; 96px heroes may drop to 48-56px on mobile
- **Grid**: 2-up and 3-up card layouts collapse to single column
- **Navigation**: Compact hamburger menu replaces horizontal link bar below 833px
- **Imagery**: Product images scale fluidly, maintaining aspect ratio via `--ac-films-video-aspect-ratio`
- **Content padding**: 22px → 16px horizontal padding below 833px

### Image Behavior

Product imagery is the primary content on apple.com. Images are delivered at multiple resolutions via media queries and scale to fill their containers. Hero images extend full-viewport-width while text content is constrained to the 980px container — creating the signature Apple "image first, text floating" composition.

### Iteration Guide

1. Always use `#1d1d1f` for heading text, never pure `#000000` — this near-black is optically warmer and is Apple's standard
2. Buttons are ALWAYS full pill (`border-radius: 980px`) — if your button looks like a rounded rectangle, it's not Apple
3. Weight 600 is the ONLY headline weight. If you're tempted to use 700 for emphasis, increase font-size instead
4. Section backgrounds alternate `#ffffff` → `#f5f5f7` → `#ffffff` — never stack two white or two gray sections consecutively
5. The frosted-glass nav is `rgba(250,250,252,0.8)` with `saturate(180%) blur(20px)` — both the saturation boost and the blur value matter for the correct glass appearance
6. Link color on light backgrounds is `#0066cc`, NOT `#0071e3` — button blue and link blue are distinct values
7. On dark backgrounds, links switch to `#2997ff` and body text becomes `rgba(255,255,255,0.8)` — never use pure white for body text in dark sections
8. The focus ring is a DOUBLE ring: `0 0 0 3px #fff, 0 0 0 5px #0071e3` — inner white ring + outer blue ring. Never use a single-ring focus style
